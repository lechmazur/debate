China imports most of its oil, its food inputs, and its high-end semiconductors. And crucially, it deters without handing Beijing a pretext, without racing a closing window, and without stacking kindling around the match.
[C1.6] Reducing risk means lowering the probability of war. Militarizing the Strait raises it.
