[P1.1] Platforms already label AI‑generated text, images and video. That is a floor, not a ceiling. [P1.2] Consider a political campaign that uses a language model to generate thousands of slightly different ads, each tuned to the psychographic profile of a 25‑year‑old single mother in Ohio, a 60‑year‑old retiree in Florida, or a college student in Texas. [P1.3] Labeling these campaigns restores the informational symmetry that users need to make autonomous choices. It does not ban the speech; it merely tells the audience who is speaking. [P1.4] The alternative is to let platforms hide the most potent form of manipulation behind a generic “AI‑generated” tag that users have learned to ignore.
