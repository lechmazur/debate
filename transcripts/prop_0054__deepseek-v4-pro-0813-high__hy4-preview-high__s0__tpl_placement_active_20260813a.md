[P1.3] Quality and social context determine the outcome, not the timer.
[P1.4] Caps also backfire in real families. Arbitrary timers breed daily battles, secrecy, and shame. A teenager who loses shared gaming may lose peer support, not gain health. A child with autism using a tablet to communicate is not "wasting screen time."

Daily limits may be a rough fallback, but the center should be what the screen is for and who is in the room.
