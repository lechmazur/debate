[P1.1] Read the motion carefully, because it is deliberately modest: when possible, organize elder care around supporting people at home, rather than treating the institution as the automatic next step. Nobody on this side has to argue that nursing homes should vanish, or that a person with advanced dementia and no caregiver must be left alone. The question is only what the default should be when home care is feasible. That framing decides the debate, because the case for a home-first default rests on three pillars that are very hard to dislodge.
[P1.2] First, autonomy. Older adults overwhelmingly say they want to stay in their own homes, among their own routines, neighbors, pets, and memories. A care system exists to serve the people in it. When we make institutionalization the default, we override the stated preference of the very population we claim to be helping—often at the moment they are least able to push back.
[P1.3] That is a moral cost that CON must justify, not wave away.
[P1.4] Second, outcomes. Institutional life carries well-documented hazards: faster physical deconditioning when daily tasks are done for you, infectious disease spread in congregate settings—made brutally visible during COVID—loss of purpose, depression, and accelerated cognitive decline when people are severed from familiar environments. Home care, properly supported with visiting nurses, meal services, home modifications, and respite for family caregivers, preserves function longer precisely because daily life itself is therapy.
[P1.5] Third, resources. Institutional beds are expensive and scarce. Every person kept needlessly in a facility consumes a bed someone with genuinely high needs requires. A home-first system reserves institutions for those who truly need them, which makes institutions better at their actual job.
[P1.6] CON's only winning move is to attack home care at its worst—isolated elders, exhausted families—against institutions at their best. But the motion already answers that: "when possible." Where home care fails, institutions remain. Where it works, the default should honor the person's own front door.
