[P1.1] The motion is not that IQ tests measure nothing. It is that they measure a narrow slice of the mind, and then that slice gets to decide who advances—into gifted programs, universities, jobs. My case rests on that mismatch: a thin measure carrying a thick consequence.
[P1.2] First, what the tests actually capture. A standardized IQ-style instrument is very good at one family of things: abstract pattern recognition, working memory, verbal analogy, and speed on decontextualized puzzles. That is a real ability. But notice everything it structurally cannot see.
[P1.3] It does not measure creativity—the capacity to generate a problem worth solving, not just solve a given one. It does not measure practical judgment, the street-level intelligence of knowing which rule to break and when. It ignores persistence, collaboration, emotional read of a room, and hard-won domain expertise. A test that must be scorable in an hour, in a booth, alone, by design excludes the abilities that unfold over time and in context.
[P1.4] Second, the outsized role. Because a single number is cheap, portable, and looks objective, institutions lean on it far beyond what its narrowness licenses. A cutoff score routes an eight-year-old into or out of enrichment. An employer screens a stack of résumés with an aptitude test and never meets the candidate whose gift is exactly the thing the test can't register.
[P1.5] The narrowness and the stakes point in opposite directions—and the stakes are winning.
[P1.6] Picture two engineers. One aces spatial-reasoning items but freezes when a project's real constraint is a frustrated client and a shifting spec. The other tests a notch lower but reliably ships, mentors, and anticipates failure. The test ranks the first higher.
[P1.7] The world ranks the second. When our sorting mechanism and reality disagree this predictably, the mechanism is overweighted.
[P1.8] So the question for this debate is simple. Does the band IQ measures match the weight we place on it? I say no—and every hour we let one hour's puzzle-solving stand in for the whole of human capability, we mis-sort real people with real futures.
