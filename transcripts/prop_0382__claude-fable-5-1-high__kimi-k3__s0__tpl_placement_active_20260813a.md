[C1.3] That is why the hydroponic-lettuce analogy fails. Switching soil for water changes nothing about what lettuce is; changing a product's genesis at the cellular level changes everything regulators must examine. Nobody calls that protectionism.
[C1.4] Notice the trap inside the motion. Either adequate standards do not yet exist—then parity now is premature—or they must be newly written for this technology, in which case they are by definition not "the same basic terms." The motion is either reckless or self-defeating.

Parity is a one-way ratchet: once folded into the conventional regime, distinct oversight becomes nearly impossible to restore.
[C1.6] Prudence says regulate the product in front of us first and harmonize later if the evidence earns it. PRO asks you to sign the harmonization before reading the evidence.
