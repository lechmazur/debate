[P1.2] My case rests on three pillars.
[P1.3] First, information value. Marriage is a high-stakes, hard-to-reverse decision involving finances, property, and often children. [P1.5] Second, commitment can precede legal marriage. [P1.6] Third, the "weak commitment" narrative actively harms people. It stigmatizes couples for making a sensible choice, pressures some into marrying too early to avoid social judgment, and offers no protective benefit in exchange. [P1.7] The question isn't whether marriage matters. It's whether the step before it deserves suspicion. It doesn't. Cohabitation is what careful, modern commitment looks like.
