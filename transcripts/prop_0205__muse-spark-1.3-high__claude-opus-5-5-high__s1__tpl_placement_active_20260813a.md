[P1.4] That is why dominance matters. There are essentially two doors to modern life — iPhone and Android — and the landlords compete with the tenants. [P1.5] Rivals do not lose because they are worse; they lose because they are never tried.

Apple and Google could still offer excellent wallets, browsers, and assistants — they would just have to win taps, not inherit them.
