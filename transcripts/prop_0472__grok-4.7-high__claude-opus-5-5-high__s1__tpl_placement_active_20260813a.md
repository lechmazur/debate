[P1.3] Tracking is weak for small fragments, avoidance depends on incomplete data shared across rival operators and states, and end-of-life compliance is still uneven. [P1.4] Astronomy is part of the same externality, not a sentimental add-on. Bright trails already cut through wide-field surveys used to find asteroids and transient events. Radio quiet is being crowded as well. Those harms arrive with launch and cannot be recalled by a later software update.

[P1.6] The claim that a moratorium slows progress confuses speed with prudence. Existing constellations can keep serving users, and terrestrial networks are not frozen.
