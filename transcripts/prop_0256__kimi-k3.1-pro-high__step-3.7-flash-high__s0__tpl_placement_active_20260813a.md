Most cheap, available inland land sits in areas already facing extreme heat, water scarcity, or wildfire risk that will worsen over the coming decades.
[C1.3] Building a new climate refuge in the arid Southwest, for example, swaps storm surge risk for life-threatening heat and water shortage vulnerability—hardly the “durable safety” PRO promises.
