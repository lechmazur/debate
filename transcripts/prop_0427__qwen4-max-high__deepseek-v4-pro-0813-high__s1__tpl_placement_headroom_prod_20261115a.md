[C1.2] To treat clubs as stronger, you need evidence that funding reliably creates inclusion for the least connected. [C1.3] Loneliness and status violence are digitally mediated around the clock. A three-hour Thursday rehearsal is bounded; the feed is not. [C1.5] The dose claim cuts the other way. The proposition would spend scarce money on buildings and buses before building the skill that stops a midnight pile-on.
[C1.6] That demotes the tool that meets harm where it actually lives, rather than merely filling after-school hours. Such ordering is backwards.
