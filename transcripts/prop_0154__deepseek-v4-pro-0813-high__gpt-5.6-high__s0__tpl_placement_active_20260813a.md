[P1.1] Foundation models are industrial copying machines. [P1.2] The current default is broken. Developers invoke fair use; creators allege infringement. Cases will take years and vary across jurisdictions, leaving smaller AI entrants unable to afford the legal risk while large platforms extract enormous value from works without consent or payment. [P1.3] That uncertainty rewards whoever can survive legal exposure, not whoever has the better claim.
[P1.4] Voluntary licensing cannot fix this. A foundation model may train on billions of works from millions of rightsholders, many difficult to identify.
