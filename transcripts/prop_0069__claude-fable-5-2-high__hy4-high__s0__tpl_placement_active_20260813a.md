"Algorithmic reputation" covers credential checks, fraud signals, repeated safety complaints, academic-integrity flags, payment history, and identity links across platforms. [C1.3] Exit protects the already credentialed; it pushes newcomers back toward interviews, deposits, ZIP codes, and informal references—less visible, less contestable, more biased.
[C1.4] Nor can opt-out be made neutral by decree. If disconnection cannot be an adverse signal, a landlord, school, or platform must either treat a missing safety record like a clean one or replace it with blunter substitutes. Bad actors gain most: a serial harasser moves to a new app, a fraudulent landlord relists elsewhere, a student with serious misconduct applies to another school.
[C1.5] Mandated ignorance shifts risk to tenants, passengers, classmates, and coworkers.

Cross-domain use should be justified or barred in context, not switched off by the person with the strongest reason to hide it.
