[C1.1] The proposition rests on a category error: comparing the potency of two molecules without comparing the quantity each dollar can actually prevent. A tonne of methane may matter more than a tonne of CO2 over twenty years, but the motion asks about spending. If equivalent dollars abate one tonne of methane and several tonnes of CO2, methane’s greater potency does not establish the larger climate benefit.
[C1.2] Leak detection is also not the same thing as avoided methane. Much of the money can go into surveys, sensors, data systems, seal replacement, remote access, or even paperwork. Diffuse and intermittent leaks are difficult to quantify and repair; stopping production to fix a serious leak can make abatement costly. A rule can improve compliance while delivering relatively little additional reduction.
[C1.3] PRO’s picture of a leak “found on Tuesday” and “fixed by Friday” describes the easy cases, not the marginal leaks on which regulation’s cost-effectiveness depends.
[C1.4] There is a second problem: repaired methane remains sellable gas. Unless production is constrained, cheaper and more reliable gas can encourage continued or expanded output. Leak repair then may not translate into equivalent reductions in lifecycle greenhouse gases. Capturing CO2, by contrast, directly diverts an emissions stream that otherwise reaches the atmosphere.
[C1.5] CCS is not synonymous with one giant, slow project. Small capture units can serve concentrated industrial streams, and a well-selected project can store carbon sooner than a remote wellhead repair can be scheduled. Nor can “near-term” mean merely that methane responds first. The relevant quantity is cumulative warming avoided during that period.
[C1.6] Durable CO2 reductions continue accumulating benefits, while methane’s relative advantage is greatest immediately after emission and then declines.
[C1.7] PRO must therefore establish not merely that methane is potent, but that an equivalent leak-detection and repair program reliably prevents more warming in the near term. Until it does, the proposition remains unsupported.
