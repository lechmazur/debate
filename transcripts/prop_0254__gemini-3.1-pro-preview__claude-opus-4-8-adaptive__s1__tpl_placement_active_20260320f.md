[P1.1] Gross Domestic Product was designed in the industrial age to measure market production, not human progress. Yet today, it functions as the ultimate scorecard for government success. [P1.2] First, GDP is fundamentally blind to the quality of economic activity. If a nation suffers a massive oil spill, the costs of the cleanup, the legal fees, and the medical bills all increase GDP. If a population becomes sicker and requires more expensive healthcare, GDP goes up. Conversely, GDP places zero value on essential, non-market goods: the unpaid care of an aging parent, the preservation of an old-growth forest, or the mental health of citizens.
[P1.3] When governments optimize for GDP alone, they systematically underfund public goods and ignore massive negative externalities.
[P1.4] Second, targeting national well-being forces better governance. A well-being framework evaluates policy based on holistic indicators like life expectancy, educational attainment, income distribution, and environmental sustainability. When well-being becomes the headline target, a government cannot claim victory simply because a handful of billionaires boosted the national average while the working class stagnated. It fundamentally shifts political incentives.
[P1.5] Instead of pursuing short-term consumption at all costs, policymakers are rewarded for investing in preventative healthcare, affordable housing, and clean energy—the true foundations of a stable, prosperous society.
[P1.6] Finally, infinite exponential growth on a finite planet is a dangerous illusion. The blind pursuit of GDP growth has accelerated climate change, resource depletion, and systemic fragility. By replacing GDP with national well-being as our primary macroeconomic compass, we transition from an extractive economic model to a resilient, regenerative one.
[P1.7] An economy should serve the people, not the other way around. By making national well-being the primary headline target, we ensure that state power and public resources are finally directed toward building a society where citizens actually thrive.
