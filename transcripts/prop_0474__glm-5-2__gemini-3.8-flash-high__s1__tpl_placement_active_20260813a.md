[P1.1] Persistent botnets are not passive infrastructure. They are weapon systems that operate continuously, adapting, expanding, and striking targets across borders. [P1.3] The harboring state may not be pulling the trigger, but it is supplying the gun and the safe house.
[P1.4] The international community already recognizes sanctions against direct cyber-aggressors — those who launch destructive attacks, steal data at scale, or disrupt critical infrastructure. The victims in hospitals, banks, and electoral systems suffer the same whether the traffic originates from a state military unit or a criminal gang operating under state tolerance. If the harm is the same, the accountability must be the same.
