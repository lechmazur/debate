[C1.1] We all want the cloned-voice scam stopped. The question is whether this motion is how to do it. It is not. The entire rule collapses on its own trigger: "verified."
[C1.2] Synthetic-media detection is probabilistic, not adjudicative. A platform can rarely know with legal certainty that the audio is AI, the impersonation unauthorized, and the purpose fraud rather than satire, protest, or public interest. PRO calls this "proven knowledge, not guesswork" [P1.2], but at internet scale it is guesswork with a lawsuit attached. Once repeated reports arrive, the platform is not neutral if it leaves the video up and not neutral if it takes it down.
[C1.3] So it will take it down.
[C1.4] That is the censorship engine hiding inside a "narrow" duty. PRO says the rule "filters out parody, opinion, and honest mistakes" [P1.6]; it does not. Labeled political satire, corporate criticism, and competitive speech sit precisely where fraud allegations flourish. Picture a labor group posting an AI-cloned CEO "confessing" to wage theft, clearly marked as parody.
[C1.5] The company answers with dozens of "verified" fraud reports. Under civil liability, the platform must choose: delete the satire or bet its balance sheet on a jury deciding whether those reports were truly verified. No firm takes that bet. Bad actors will manufacture repeated reports to silence rivals and critics.
[C1.6] Nor is the platform the "cheapest preventer" [P1.4]. The theft is completed when money moves, on payment rails and phone calls platforms often do not control. Removing one video does not stop a re-upload, an encrypted chat, or a live call using the same clone. Liability instead makes hosts the deep-pocketed insurer of last resort, relieving payment firms, telecoms, and police of pressure to interdict the transaction.
[C1.7] The better path is fast notice, evidence preservation, investigator cooperation, and safe harbor for good-faith removal—not damages for ignoring unverifiable reports. The motion would chill speech, weaponize reports, and still not get the money back. Reject it.
