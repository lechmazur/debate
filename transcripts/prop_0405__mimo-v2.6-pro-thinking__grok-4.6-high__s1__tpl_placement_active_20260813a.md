[C1.3] A beep is not a barrier. When liability attaches because a rider ignored those tools, we are not pricing the firm's own negligence. We are demanding that a rental app prevent misuse it cannot see in real time.

[C1.6] It will also misfire. If companies pay for sidewalk collisions, riders have less reason to stay in the street. Cities that want fewer sidewalk rides already have tickets, seized vehicles, and permit conditions. Those hit the person on the vehicle.

[C1.8] Restricted zones cut the other way. Marking a plaza off-limits is an attempt to steer. Treat that map as a confession of duty and firms will draw fewer maps. The pedestrian's bill is real.
