[P1.1] Every study and work pathway asks one core question: can this person do what we need? A verified professional credential answers that question directly. Physical residency answers a different one: where has this person been sleeping? My case is simple.
[P1.2] When a system has to choose which gate to make easier, it should favour the gate that measures competence, not the one that measures location.
[P1.3] First, signal quality. An online credential in data analysis, nursing continuing education, or project management tests a skill against a defined standard. A residency requirement tests nothing about skill. Two years of address history tells a hospital nothing about whether you can read a patient chart.
[P1.4] When residency is the easier route, we reward people for standing in the right place and penalise people for learning the right things.
[P1.5] Second, verification. This is where CON will claim credentials are risky. The opposite is true. A digitally issued credential can be checked against the issuer's own records almost instantly, with tamper-evident signatures and a clear chain back to an accredited body.
[P1.6] Residency proof is a pile of leases, utility bills and landlord letters. Those are easy to fake, hard to audit, and brutally unfair to renters, the recently displaced and anyone living in informal housing. If fraud worries you, the paper trail of residency should worry you far more.
[P1.7] Third, the trap residency creates. Picture a qualified software tester in Nairobi. She cannot get the job without local residency, and she cannot afford to relocate without the job. Residency-first pathways reward people who already have the money to move speculatively.
[P1.8] Credential-first pathways let talent be assessed before the plane ticket is bought, which is fairer to applicants and less wasteful for employers who otherwise gamble on arrivals.
[P1.9] Be clear about what this motion does not require. It does not abolish visas, security checks or licensing exams. It says the credential lane should be smoother than the residency lane.
