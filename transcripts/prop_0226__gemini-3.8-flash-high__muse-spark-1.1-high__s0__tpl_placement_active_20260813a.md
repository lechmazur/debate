[P1.2] Countries must recognize a limited legal right to climate asylum because existing international law contains a fatal void: it offers no protection when a homeland ceases to exist.
[P1.3] This proposal is intentionally and strictly limited. It does not create an open-ended right for every natural disaster or economic hardship. Instead, it targets two physically verifiable, irreversible conditions: territorial inundation from sea-level rise and absolute unhabitability from extreme heat. [P1.4] Sea levels swallow sovereign territory permanently, eliminating any possibility of an internal flight alternative. Similarly, sustained wet-bulb temperatures exceeding human physiological tolerance render outdoor survival and basic biological function impossible without continuous artificial cooling. [P1.5] Denying a legal pathway will not stop displacement; it merely ensures that migration happens through perilous, irregular channels run by smugglers, creating border crises and human tragedy. [P1.6] When the territory of a sovereign state disappears or its climate becomes physiologically incompatible with human life, the social contract dissolves through environmental collapse.
