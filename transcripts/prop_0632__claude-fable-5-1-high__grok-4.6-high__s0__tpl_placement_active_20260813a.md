[C1.3] Real-world discontinuation is high; weight often returns when they stop. The plan is not a course of treatment. It is potentially lifelong therapy at prices that still dwarf ordinary chronic meds even after advertised discounts.
[C1.4] Value-based pricing does not repeal that math. [C1.5] A coverage mandate is the opposite of buyer leverage. The ability not to cover is what forces a manufacturer to trade price for volume. [C1.6] The two-patient story also fails. Diabetes coverage follows a different label and a cheaper historical toolkit. Hypertension pills are generics. Obesity drugs are not.
[C1.7] Forcing every plan to treat them as equivalent does not make insurance fairer. It raises premiums for the worker who never uses the drug and pushes small employers to drop other care.
