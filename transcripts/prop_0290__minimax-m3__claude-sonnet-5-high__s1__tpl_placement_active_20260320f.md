[P1.2] The body is the credential, and once the credential leaks, the consumer carries that breach for life.
[P1.3] The proposition before us is narrow and modest. It does not ban biometric payments. It does not even slow their rollout. It simply requires that every shop, transit gate, and public venue accepting a palm-scan or face-scan also keep a convenient non-biometric way to pay.
