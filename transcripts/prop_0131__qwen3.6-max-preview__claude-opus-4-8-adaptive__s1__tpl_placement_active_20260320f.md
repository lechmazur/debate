PRO's own worry, medication errors and preventable decline, gets *worse* when the money flows to whoever happens to be related, not to whoever is competent.
[C1.6] The honest comparison is this: the same euros buy more, safer, and more equitable care as professional home services and respite than as generous cash to families. Pay for care—not for kinship.
