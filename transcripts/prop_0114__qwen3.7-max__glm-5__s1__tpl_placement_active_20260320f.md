[P1.3] The opposition will likely argue that this policy is too expensive or distorts labor markets. But this misunderstands the fundamental economics of care. [P1.4] Pension credits are simply a deferred payment for services already rendered to the public. It is far more fiscally responsible to accrue a measured pension liability than to let caregivers fall into destitution and rely on emergency welfare systems later in life.

[P1.6] We cannot claim to value family while financially penalizing those who hold it together. Recognizing this essential labor through pension credits is not a charitable handout; it is the settlement of a long-overdue economic debt.
