[P1.2] The duty should guarantee every household the option to buy an affordable connection that actually works. High quality means enforceable standards for upload and download speeds, reliability and delay, tested at peak hours rather than inferred from an advertised maximum. [P1.4] A resident whose address is marked “served” but who cannot get connected should be able to request service by a binding deadline, appeal a refusal to a regulator and obtain an order to connect.

[C1.4] Second, the motion says "countries," not rich countries. Many states have not yet delivered reliable water or power to all their citizens.
