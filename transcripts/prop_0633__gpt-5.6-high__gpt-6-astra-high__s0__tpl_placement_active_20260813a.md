[P1.2] Mandatory bias testing creates a basic duty to check whether a tool produces unjustified disparities and whether its criteria actually relate to the job. Testing before deployment and periodically afterward can catch problems caused by changing applicant pools, workplace practices, or model updates. It requires employers to identify disparities, investigate their causes, and justify or correct them rather than hiding behind an algorithmic score.
