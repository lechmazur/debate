[P1.2] Animal experimentation review frameworks were built precisely for this middle ground: entities that lack human-level cognition and cannot give consent, yet possess sufficient functional organization that their treatment demands ethical justification. [P1.3] Rudimentary learning requires neuroplasticity, organized firing patterns, and information integration. [P1.5] When biological systems begin to learn, adapt, and process inputs, treating them merely as inert chemical assays is an indefensible blind spot.
