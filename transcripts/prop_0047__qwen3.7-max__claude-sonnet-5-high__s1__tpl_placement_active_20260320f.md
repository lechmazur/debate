[C1.5] Third, the subsidy side assumes government can engineer a local sustainable textile industry at the scale needed to replace fast fashion capacity. [C1.6] There are more precise tools already available, extended producer responsibility, durability and labeling standards, direct enforcement against labor abuses, that target the actual externality without taxing a necessity good regressively.
