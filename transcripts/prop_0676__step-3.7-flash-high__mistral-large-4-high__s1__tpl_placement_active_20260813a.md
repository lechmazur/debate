[P1.1] Automated video and personality assessments in hiring are an unregulated disaster for fair labor markets, and government bans or heavy restrictions are urgently needed to stop their spread. These tools don’t reduce bias—they codify and amplify it, wrapped in a false sheen of objectivity that makes discrimination harder to detect and challenge. Facial analysis algorithms routinely penalize neurodivergent candidates for atypical eye contact or speech patterns, and consistently rate darker-skinned applicants as less competent due to biased training data. Personality assessments measure conformity, not skill: they screen out assertive women as “too aggressive,” quiet autistic candidates as “poor culture fits,” and anyone who deviates from a narrow, arbitrary norm for “ideal employee,” all while their scoring criteria are trade secrets candidates can’t contest.

Proponents claim they make hiring more efficient, but independent research consistently finds they have higher error rates predicting job performance than trained human reviewers, while entrenching existing inequities.
[P1.3] There is no legitimate public or private interest served by allowing employers to use unaccountable, discriminatory, invasive tools to decide who gets a job.
