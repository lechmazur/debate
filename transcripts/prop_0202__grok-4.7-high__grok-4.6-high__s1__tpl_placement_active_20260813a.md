[P1.1] Interoperability mandates attack the mechanism that makes digital markets uncontestable: exclusive control of the interface between an installed base and everyone else. Once a gatekeeper must interconnect, a rival no longer needs to recruit the whole network before it can offer value. That is a first-order gain in contestability. Security and quality costs are real, but smaller, designable, and routinely inflated by the firms that collect the rents of closure.
[P1.2] A messenger that can reach users on a dominant service competes on privacy, price, or features without winning a cold-start war against a locked-in billion. The same logic covers app distribution, device capabilities, and data portability. Without a duty to interconnect, the interface is a private tollbooth. With it, complementary entry becomes possible even while the core network stays large.
[P1.3] Email, the web, and telecom became contestable this way. Closed messaging and app ecosystems are the exception regulation corrects, not the natural state of software.
[P1.4] Security does not require a monopoly on the interface. End-to-end encryption, authenticated APIs, scoped permissions, and audit rights can travel with the mandate; DMA-style rules already aim to preserve encryption rather than strip it. Closed platforms concentrate patch authority, hide interfaces from independent scrutiny, and turn one vendor's design choice into systemic risk. A governed increase in attack surface beats a smaller surface whose owner treats "security" as a rationale for exclusion.
[P1.5] Mandatory interconnection did not destroy telephone networks or email. Security engineering adapted.
[P1.6] Quality objections follow the same pattern. Some transitional friction is inevitable when a private standard becomes shared. But quality under lock-in is a retention bundle, not a public good. Users who want deep integration can stay inside the incumbent stack.
[P1.7] Users who want cheaper hardware, different moderation, or another store finally have a path that does not require abandoning their social graph. Competition on those margins raises quality for the market even if the incumbent's preferred bundle is slightly less seamless.
[P1.8] Contestability gains reopen markets network effects had closed. Security and quality losses depend on how interfaces are specified, not on [clipped]
