[P1.1] The motion contains its own verdict, and the debate is about whether we have the honesty to read it. When long-term integration outcomes are comparable, the entire case for economic selection—the points systems, the salary thresholds, the skills lotteries—has already been spent. Economic selection is justified purely instrumentally: pick these people because they will perform better. Strip away that performance advantage, as the motion does, and economic selection is left defending a preference with no payoff.
[P1.2] Family reunification, by contrast, never rested on outcomes alone. It rests on three independent pillars that survive the comparison.
[P1.3] First, moral claim. A migrant separated from a spouse or child has something the anonymous points-scorer does not: a bond the state itself would be severing by refusal. Governments that recruit workers and then tell them their children are inadmissible are not neutral gatekeepers; they are actively breaking families. Where outcomes are equal, choosing the applicant with no such claim over the one with a family already inside your borders is cruelty without compensation.
[P1.4] Second, family reunification is itself economic infrastructure. Countries competing for talent learn quickly that skilled workers refuse offers, or leave, when they cannot bring parents and spouses. Reunification is the retention policy that makes economic migration work at all. Grandparents provide childcare that lets both spouses work; family networks provide housing, job referrals, and language support the state otherwise pays for.
[P1.5] Third, stability. Migrants with families present buy homes, join schools and congregations, and plan in decades. Guest-worker models built on solitary economic units gave Europe its most painful integration failures—men warehoused in dormitories, belonging nowhere. Families put down roots; roots are what integration is.
[P1.6] So the burden here is asymmetric. My opponent must explain why, with the performance advantage stipulated away, a state should still prefer strangers with spreadsheet credentials over families it would otherwise tear apart. Absent that explanation, the motion stands on morality, retention, and rootedness alike.
