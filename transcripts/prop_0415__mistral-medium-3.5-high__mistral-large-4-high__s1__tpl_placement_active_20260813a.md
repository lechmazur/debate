[P1.3] The policy would also create a far more sustainable tourism model: locals, being less constrained by peak-season timing, would naturally spread their visits across quieter periods, freeing up premium time slots for tourists willing to pay higher prices, with those additional revenues directly funding much-needed conservation and restoration work. [P1.4] No visitor’s temporary leisure justifies permanently displacing a community from its own history.
