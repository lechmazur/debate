[P1.6] Limiting the pinpoint tag and commercial promotion upstream is cheaper, faster, and still allows respectful visits.
[P1.7] If a town can restrict drones, noise, and commercial filming for safety, it can restrict the specific promotional tool that foreseeably causes the harm.
