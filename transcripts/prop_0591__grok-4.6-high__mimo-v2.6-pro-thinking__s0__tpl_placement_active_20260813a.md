Public power that cannot be understood or contested is not screening. It is unreviewable sorting. Countries should adopt a global minimum standard of algorithmic transparency for automated immigration and asylum decisions because those decisions already allocate liberty, family unity, and protection from persecution, and because opacity conceals error until after the harm is irreversible.
