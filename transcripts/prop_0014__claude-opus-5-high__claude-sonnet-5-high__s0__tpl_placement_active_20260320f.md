[C1.3] Shrinking the numerator while shrinking the denominator faster is not fiscal responsibility; it's how Britain and the Eurozone periphery turned manageable debt problems into lost decades after 2010, when premature austerity cut growth, tax revenue fell, and debt ratios rose anyway.

[C1.5] Third, the "insurance" argument cuts against PRO. [C1.6] The real choice isn't discipline versus profligacy. It's investment now versus paying more later for the problems we let compound.
