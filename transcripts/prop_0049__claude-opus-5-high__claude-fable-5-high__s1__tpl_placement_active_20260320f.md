[C1.4] Families who can pay keep quality content; families who can't get whatever survives on scraps. A privacy rule becomes a regressive tax on poor kids.
[C1.5] Finally, the category error. [C1.6] The choice isn't surveillance versus innocence. It's precise rules that work versus a blunt ban that backfires on the children it claims to protect.
