[P1.1] Public universities should not measure opportunity by how many students they enroll, but by how many they equip to build stable lives. [P1.2] A resource shift means targeted reallocation, not abolishing every small department or turning universities into trade schools. They should also be stackable, allowing a certificate to count toward an associate’s or bachelor’s degree later.
[P1.3] This improves equity. [P1.4] Keeping resources trapped in programs where noncompletion is predictable does not protect disadvantaged students; it asks them to subsidize institutional inertia.
[P1.5] The shift also serves the public that funds these universities.
