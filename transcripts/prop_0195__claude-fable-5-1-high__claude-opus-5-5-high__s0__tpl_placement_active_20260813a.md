[P1.1] Start with what the principle actually does in a regulator's hands. It does not say "weigh risks against benefits carefully." Ordinary risk assessment already says that. Its distinctive addition is an asymmetry: when evidence is incomplete, the innovator must prove safety before deployment, while the regulator need prove nothing to block.
[P1.2] Absence of evidence of harm becomes insufficient; absence of evidence of benefit is never scrutinized at all.
[P1.3] That asymmetry is the whole case. Harms of approval are vivid, attributable, and photographed. Harms of delay are statistical, diffuse, and belong to no one. A regulator who approves a product that hurts a hundred people faces a hearing.
[P1.4] A regulator who delays a product that would have saved ten thousand faces nothing, because those ten thousand never appear in any file. The precautionary principle takes this natural bias and writes it into law.
[P1.5] Look at the invisible graveyard it fills. Decades of near-freeze on nuclear construction after accidents that killed few people pushed grids toward coal, whose air pollution kills on a scale the nuclear industry has never approached. Golden rice, biofortified to address vitamin A deficiency that blinds and kills children across South Asia, sat for roughly two decades behind regulatory demands for proof of safety no conventional crop has ever met. In early 2020, laboratories capable of running their own coronavirus tests were told to wait for authorization while the virus spread untracked.
[P1.6] Now the concessions, because they sharpen the point rather than weaken it. Thalidomide, leaded gasoline, CFCs: genuine harms, genuinely prevented or stopped. But each was caught by evidence of harm, or would have been by ordinary cost-benefit review. Precaution earns credit only where it acts without evidence, and that is exactly the domain where it blocks vaccines, crops, energy, and diagnostics while catching almost nothing real.
[P1.7] So the comparison is narrow and fair: count only harms that ordinary risk assessment would have missed and precaution uniquely stopped, then set them against the uncounted dead of delay. The ledger is not close.
