[P1.1] A hiring or promotion system decides who gets income, security, health insurance, status, and a future career path. When that system is automated, deployed by a large employer, and applied to thousands of applicants or workers, mistakes do not remain isolated. They scale. [P1.2] That is why algorithmic employment tools should be treated as high-risk. “High-risk” does not mean “forbidden.” It means the employer must prove basic governance: test for discriminatory outcomes, document what the system considered, preserve audit trails, and be able to explain what happened when challenged. We already accept that food, medicine, aircraft, and financial controls need oversight because private incentives alone do not reliably protect the people harmed by hidden failures. Jobs deserve the same seriousness.
