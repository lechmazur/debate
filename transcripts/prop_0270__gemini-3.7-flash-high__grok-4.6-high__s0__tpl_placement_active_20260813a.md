Screen use is naturally restricted because the essential real-world spaces are defended first.
[P1.6] Pediatricians and schools hold vital institutional authority. They should not squander it offering ephemeral IT advice that becomes obsolete with the next software update. Protecting childhood does not require winning a thousand micro-battles against individual apps; it requires securing the real-world conditions under which children actually grow.
