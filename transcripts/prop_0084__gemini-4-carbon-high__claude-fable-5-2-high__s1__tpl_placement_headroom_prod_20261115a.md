[P1.1] Dating apps are not ordinary social networks; their explicit purpose is to cultivate emotional intimacy and arrange private, offline meetings between strangers. Yet most platforms still treat sign-up like downloading a casual game. Because creating an account takes seconds and requires little more than a disposable phone number, predators operate with near-total impunity. Organized romance scammers fabricate personas to drain victims' life savings, extortionists coerce intimate images from behind fake profiles, and users banned for stalking or assault simply open fresh accounts minutes later.
[P1.2] Requiring stronger identity verification and fraud checks, such as matching a live biometric selfie against official identification while screening out burner numbers and suspicious devices, breaks this predatory model at the root. A slower sign-up is not an unfortunate side effect; deliberate friction at the door is precisely what cripples industrial scam farms and makes platform bans permanent. Today, safety teams play endless whack-a-mole only after serious harm occurs. When verified identity anchors an account, bad actors cannot shed their history with a new email address, and genuine users stop wasting hours sifting through bots and frauds.
[P1.3] Crucially, sacrificing full anonymity toward the platform does not mean exposing users to the public or creating reckless data honeypots. Just as banks and lodging apps verify customers privately, dating platforms can confirm legal identity at registration, delete raw document images while retaining a secure cryptographic marker to block repeat offenders, and still let users browse publicly under first names or nicknames. That protects vulnerable or private daters from public exposure while stripping predators of impunity. Nobody needs an untraceable burner account to pursue an honest relationship, whereas scammers and serial abusers depend on it completely.
[P1.4] When a platform's core product puts strangers alone together, basic accountability must outrank frictionless anonymity.
