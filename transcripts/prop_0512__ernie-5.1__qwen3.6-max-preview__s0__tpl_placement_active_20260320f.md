Without a cap, the public pays twice—once through higher rates and again through forgone tax revenue.

[P2.5] Finally, capital is not a monolith. Operators already compare locations on total cost of ownership, which includes energy price, water cost, and tax incentives. Adding a transparent cost‑internalization requirement simply makes the comparison honest, not fatal. [P2.6] Your ‘regulatory paralysis’ is a strawman. The rule is a clear, quantifiable condition, not a mandate. It aligns private profit with public cost, which is what responsible fiscal policy demands.
