[P1.4] What disappears is the why, the alternative considered and discarded, the hallway debrief. A first-year analyst learns to produce deliverables, not to think like a senior.

[P1.7] Seniors who love remote already banked ten years of weak ties, reputation, and tacit knowledge. Juniors are trying to build that capital remotely and cannot. [P1.8] That delay is what makes the consensus dishonest. The loss isn't visible in today's engagement survey; it shows up in year four when the junior quits because they never actually became mid-level, and the firm calls it a retention problem, not a training failure.
