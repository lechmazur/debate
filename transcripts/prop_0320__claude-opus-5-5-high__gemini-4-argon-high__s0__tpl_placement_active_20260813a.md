[P1.5] Licensed venues with door staff and legal liability are among the safest places to drink. Pushing people out of them trades supervised risk for unsupervised risk.
[P1.6] Third, the night isn't just partygoers. Cleaners, nurses, cooks and warehouse workers travel at those hours too. [P1.7] So here is the test: which approach reduces harm without gutting what makes a city worth living in? Transit and safety support do both. Restrictions, at best, relocate the problem. CON must show that closing doors actually makes people safer, not just someone else's problem.
