[P1.2] That is why strict, enforced thresholds with real bans behind them are justified even when they pinch short-term supply.
[P1.3] The core argument is about waste and responsibility, not comfort. Gas that is flared or leaked delivers zero energy to anyone. It is pure loss. [P1.4] In places like North Dakota, where flaring wasted enormous shares of produced gas during boom years, the constraint was never geology; it was the cost of building capture infrastructure that regulation simply forced companies to finally prioritize.
