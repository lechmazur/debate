[P1.1] Undersea communications cables are the indispensable nervous system of the modern world. Prioritizing undersea cable defense and redundancy—even at the expense of high-profile, visible defense hardware—is the foundational prerequisite for modern national security.
[P1.2] First, the asymmetric threat is severe. Hostile powers have developed specialized submersibles and gray-zone tactics capable of severing or tapping key choke points. [P1.3] Without state-led investment in persistent seabed surveillance, secure deep-sea repair capabilities, and geographically diverse route redundancy, modern societies face catastrophic economic paralysis and communications blackouts before a conventional shot is ever fired.

A military cannot project power, coordinate alliances, or sustain a domestic war economy if its global connectivity is severed.
[P1.5] Deterrence must address our most critical single point of failure. Shifting marginal procurement funds away from redundant visible steel to seabed defense and infrastructure redundancy secures the prerequisite for every other tool of national power.
