[P1.3] First, consumer welfare. Without mandated interoperability, drivers face a maze of incompatible plugs, apps, accounts, and payment systems. [P1.4] That is not a luxury; for a technology we're asking the public to adopt, it's the difference between confidence and range anxiety.
[P1.5] Second, competition and lower prices. Proprietary lock-in is a deliberate strategy to capture customers and extract rents. [P1.6] Markets work better when the rails are shared.
[P1.7] Third, public investment and the climate transition. Governments are pouring public money into charging networks to accelerate EV adoption.
