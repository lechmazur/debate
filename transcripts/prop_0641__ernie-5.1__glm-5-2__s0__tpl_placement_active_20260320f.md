[P1.1] Governments already pour billions into fertilizers, herbicides, and high‑yield seed subsidies. [P1.4] Consider the U.S. Corn Belt: annual input subsidies exceed $5 billion, while soil erosion costs the economy roughly $4 billion in lost productivity and water treatment.
