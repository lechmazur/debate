[P1.2] Every dollar borrowed today is a dollar taxed from future budgets, including the very domestic programs my opponent claims to protect. [P1.3] The proposition asks whether serious deficit reduction should take priority over most new spending commitments. [P1.4] This is not an argument against investment. It is an argument that investment requires solvency. A government that cannot service its debt cannot fund Medicare, cannot rebuild bridges, cannot respond to pandemics.
