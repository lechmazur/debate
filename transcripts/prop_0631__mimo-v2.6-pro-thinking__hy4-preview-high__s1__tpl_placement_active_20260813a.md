[P1.1] Copyright exists to reward human creativity, and the law has never pretended otherwise. The constitutional copyright power runs to "authors" of "writings," and courts require protected expression to originate in a human mind: what made early photographs copyrightable was the photographer's choices of pose, lighting, and arrangement, not the mechanical capture. The Copyright Office has refused registration for machine-authored works, and a federal appeals court agreed. PRO writes a settled principle into an enforceable rule.
[P1.2] Without it, copyright becomes a volume game. A stock agency generates a million images from template prompts and claims exclusive rights in every one, fencing off visual vocabulary on a few keystrokes. Downstream creators then bear an impossible burden: proving their image was independently generated rather than copied, when the same model emits near-duplicates on demand. Cheap generation plus exclusive rights is a litigation engine aimed at honest users of these tools.
[P1.3] The standard is not novel. Originality already requires a minimal creative spark from a person. When the human supplies six words and the model supplies composition, palette, character, and mood, the expressive choices were not human. Contrast the novelist who iteratively directs a scene, rewrites passages, and arranges chapters: that person has documented substantial creative control and keeps the copyright.
[P1.4] The rule separates exactly those cases.
[P1.5] Documentation makes the line usable. Registration already collects deposits and claims; requiring the claimant to record direction, selection, revision, and arrangement turns an unprovable guess into a record. Yes, that demands more of claimants, but so does every threshold. And it beats the alternative: case-by-case forensic arguments over how much prompting counts.
[P1.6] Protecting outputs is the wrong incentive. Developers can be protected on the model, the pipeline, the software, the trade secret; what they cannot do is take exclusive rights in expression no human conceived. The proposition is modest: document substantial human creative control, or let the work stay in the public domain where the machine found its material. That is fidelity to copyright.
