[P1.1] Emergency powers may be necessary; indefinite executive discretion is not. [P1.3] The central reason is that the incentives surrounding emergency powers are asymmetric. Executives gain speed and discretion from keeping them; people harmed by restrictions must organize to recover ordinary protections.
