[C1.2] The retention benefit is just as real. [C1.3] Teacher continuity protects learning more than a compressed week threatens it.
[C1.4] The academic case is the weak pillar. Four-day districts are not random. [C1.5] Some even see attendance improve because families use Friday for appointments that would otherwise pull children out of class.
