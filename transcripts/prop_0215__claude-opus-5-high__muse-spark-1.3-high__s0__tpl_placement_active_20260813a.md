[P1.6] Consider the alternative the other side must defend. [C1.6] Robbing today's charity to pay for yesterday's state contract is not restitution.

Reconciliation requires truth, open archives, and voluntary service — not coerced transfers that bankrupt caregivers and teach that justice means finding a scapegoat with stained glass.
