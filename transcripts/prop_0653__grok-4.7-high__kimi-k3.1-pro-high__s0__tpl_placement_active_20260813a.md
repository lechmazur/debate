[C1.1] The motion does not merely ban retaliation, discrimination, or automatic dismissal. It bans a calculation—even when it benefits the worker and no adverse action follows. That overbreadth is the decisive flaw.
[C1.2] A quit-risk flag can prompt a stay interview, a schedule adjustment, or a retention bonus before a valued employee leaves. A productivity forecast can identify someone who needs coaching, better equipment, or realistic targets rather than punishment. Used that way, prediction is prevention, not “pre-crime.” PRO’s entire case silently converts a forecast into a penalty, but that conversion is a management choice—and it can be prohibited directly.
[C1.3] The right rule targets decisions and abuses: no adverse action solely from a score; observed performance remains the basis for discipline; workers can inspect and contest consequential information; vendors must permit auditing; protected or irrelevant data cannot be laundered into predictions. These safeguards address PRO’s black-box and self-fulfilling-prophecy concerns without outlawing beneficial uses. If Maria is denied training because she is labeled a flight risk, prohibit that decision. Do not prohibit the intervention that could have fixed Maria’s workload.
[C1.4] Unionization prediction is PRO’s strongest ground, but it proves too little. Surveillance, blacklisting, and retaliatory interference should be directly prohibited and rigorously enforced. Yet an employer can isolate an organizer using human observations without AI, and AI-assisted interference remains wrongful because it is interference—not because arithmetic occurred. At most, this supports especially stringent restrictions around protected organizing activity, not a categorical ban on every individual productivity and retention forecast.
[C1.5] Worse, the ban does not abolish speculative judgments; it returns them to managerial intuition. A supervisor’s hunch that a caregiver will quit or a newcomer will struggle leaves no model card, decision log, or statistical output to examine. A regulated system at least creates evidence that can be challenged.
[C1.6] The choice is therefore not scores versus rights. It is governed, contestable prediction with prohibited uses versus unregulated intuition plus a ban on helpful tools. Punish the adverse act, constrain the inputs, and require accountability. Do not outlaw foresight itself.
