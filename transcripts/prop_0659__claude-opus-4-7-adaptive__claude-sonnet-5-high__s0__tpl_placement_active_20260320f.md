[P1.3] Selling a hiring algorithm built on that assumption is like selling a breathalyzer that also claims to detect guilt.
[P1.4] Now stack the stakes. A candidate flagged as "low enthusiasm" because they are autistic, have Bell's palsy, wear a hijab that alters lower-face signals, or simply come from a culture that doesn't perform Anglo-American smile-affect, gets silently filtered out. [P1.5] That is discrimination laundered through a confident-looking score.
[P1.6] For incumbent workers it is worse. Continuous emotion surveillance—cameras in call centers scoring "sincerity," webcams rating "engagement" in meetings—coerces performative affect all day. [P1.7] The workplace becomes a stage where your face is your permanent record.
[P1.8] Ordinary tools cannot fix this. Consent is a fiction when the alternative is not getting the job. Anti-discrimination law struggles because the "reason" is a proprietary number. Accuracy audits cannot rescue a construct that isn't valid to begin with.
