[P1.1] National security is no longer just about defending borders; it is about protecting the technological foundations of global stability. Government screening is the only mechanism capable of correcting this critical market failure.
[P1.5] By establishing clear, targeted screening mechanisms for outbound investments, countries protect their technological edge, prevent the circumvention of existing security controls, and ensure that democratic capital does not finance the tools of authoritarian oppression.
