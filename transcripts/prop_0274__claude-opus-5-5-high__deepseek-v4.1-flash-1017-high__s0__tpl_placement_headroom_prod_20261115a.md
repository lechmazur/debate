[C1.3] Limiting geotagging is also either redundant or counterproductive. Exact spots circulate through comments, screenshots, group chats, and old posts; official silence merely makes information a private advantage for tour operators and well-connected creators while casual visitors guess. [C1.5] Finally, "limit promotion" is dangerously vague. Who defines fragile? Which official gets to suppress a viewpoint, a warning, or a public landscape? Destinations can discourage irresponsible behavior without making speech the first lever.
[C1.6] The decisive comparison is not pristine nature versus selfies. It is whether we reduce harm by managing access or by managing information. Managing access works; managing information mostly relocates the crowd. That is a shell game, not conservation.
