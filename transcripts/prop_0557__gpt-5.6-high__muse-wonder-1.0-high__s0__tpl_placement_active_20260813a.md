[P1.1] Public universities allocate scarce seats with taxpayer support. Their admissions process therefore owes applicants two things: consistent comparison and decisions tied to academic readiness. [P1.3] High school grades are essential, but not commensurable. An A can reflect exceptional mastery, generous grading, different course offerings, or disrupted schooling. [P1.5] Test-optional policies do not remove testing inequality; they make information strategically selective. [C1.7] A public system should reduce purchasable advantages, not mandate another one. Keep scores available as evidence, not compulsory as toll.
