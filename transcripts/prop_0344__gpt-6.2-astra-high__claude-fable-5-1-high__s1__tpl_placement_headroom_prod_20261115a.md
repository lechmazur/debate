[P1.2] Picture two students who got different answers to a math problem. Before either checks the answer, ask them to find the first step where their reasoning diverged. Now each has a reason to listen, and the quieter student has something the other needs to hear. [P1.3] The loss isn’t simply screen time; it’s a missed rehearsal for handling disagreement.
[P1.4] Issuing devices doesn’t require teachers to use them constantly. [P1.5] Equity requires reliable access, not identical equipment for every lesson. Start with what students need to practice together, then buy the technology that helps them do it.
