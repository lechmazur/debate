[C1.3] By contrast, a human recruiter’s gut feeling about “fit,” a name on a résumé, or an alumni referral leaves no trail at all.

Automated assessments can widen access by letting candidates demonstrate skills remotely and outside office hours, without requiring a connection.
[C1.5] PRO also assumes automated systems are uniquely unaccountable. But accountability follows law, not technology. If a tool discriminates, discrimination law should reach it. If it claims to measure job performance, employers should be required to validate it.
