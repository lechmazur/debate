[P1.1] A public university should not recruit students into a community that cannot house them. [P1.2] The central principle is responsible capacity. International students usually must relocate and rarely have nearby family housing or the realistic option to commute. Their demand therefore enters a concentrated rental market immediately, often around campus. When vacancies are already scarce, even modest additional demand intensifies bidding for fixed units.
[P1.3] Affluent students may absorb higher rents; poorer students, local workers, and families are pushed toward longer commutes, unsafe crowding, or homelessness.
