[P1.1] A free society requires the ability to evolve, make mistakes, and decide what we reveal to the world. When a private citizen deletes an old post, keeps their location pings private, or retracts personal details, they are making an explicit assertion of personal autonomy. Data brokers fundamentally subvert that choice. By scraping, archiving, and selling digital traces that were intentionally erased or never made public, brokers convert private or fleeting digital moments into permanent, monetizable dossiers.
[P1.2] This practice inflicts severe, asymmetric harms. It strips citizens of the power to retract their own words and actions. Consider an abuse survivor whose unlisted location patterns are packaged and sold, a worker denied employment because a broker archived an indiscreet post deleted years ago, or a patient whose non-public health searches are weaponized to assess insurance risk. When brokers warehouse and trade in these traces, private citizens lose control over their livelihoods, reputations, and physical security to unaccountable third-party entities they never interacted with and never consented to deal with.
[P1.3] Crucially, commodifying deleted and non-public data serves no legitimate democratic or public interest. We are not discussing open court dockets, public property filings, or investigative reporting on public officials. We are discussing the non-consensual harvesting of ordinary people's personal exhaust. Data brokers operate in the shadows, functioning as an unregulated surveillance marketplace for employers, insurers, and predatory actors.
[P1.4] Permitting brokers to archive and sell retracted traces turns digital life into a one-way trap where privacy can be forfeited, but never reclaimed. A third-party middleman has no valid right to turn an individual's private or retracted history into a commercial product. To protect basic dignity, personal autonomy, and digital privacy, data brokers must be prohibited from archiving and selling the deleted or non-public traces of private citizens.
