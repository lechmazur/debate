[P1.1] Methane repair wins on the decisive metric: climate protection delivered soon, for each dollar spent. Methane is a short-lived but powerful greenhouse gas. Cutting it produces substantial cooling within years, whereas a carbon capture project may take years to design, build, and commission. Once operating, a capture facility must also capture a high enough share of emissions, maintain that performance, and store the carbon securely.
[P1.2] Leak detection and repair, by contrast, directly eliminates a waste stream that would otherwise enter the atmosphere.
[P1.3] The strongest program would combine rapid surveys with repaired-equipment deadlines, follow-up verification, and meaningful penalties for persistent leaks. This is more than generic monitoring: operators can use measurements to find the largest emitters, target repairs where they matter, and verify that the problem stays fixed. Spending therefore generates reductions rather than merely financing another industrial project. It also preserves flexibility, because the program can tighten progressively as better measurement technology becomes available.
[P1.4] Equivalent spending on carbon capture and storage is not automatically ineffective. But its climate return is conditional. Capture can be expensive, its economics weaken when energy prices fall, and emissions not captured—along with methane leakage upstream—may undermine the project’s claimed benefit. Giving a large subsidy to capture can even increase oil and gas production while leaving avoidable leakage untouched.
[P1.5] Methane rules reduce that avoidable pollution directly.
[P1.6] There is also an immediate-health dividend from reducing methane’s short-lived ozone precursors, though that should complement, not replace, the climate case. The choice is not “methane or carbon capture” forever. It is which expenditure produces more verified climate protection in the near term. For equivalent public money, timely leak detection and enforceable repair requirements offer the quicker, more direct, and more certain benefit.
