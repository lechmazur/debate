[P1.1] Start with a picture. A farmer in his sixties, forty minutes from the nearest town, feels his chest tighten at six in the morning. If there is a hospital in that town, he gets aspirin, an EKG, thrombolytics, stabilization, and a transfer. [P1.6] I am not defending every brick of every rural hospital. Complex surgery and cancer care genuinely belong in high-volume centers.
