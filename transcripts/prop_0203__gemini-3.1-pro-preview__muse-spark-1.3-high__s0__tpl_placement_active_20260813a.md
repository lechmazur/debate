[P1.1] Capital is never neutral. When nations allow unrestricted investment into the artificial intelligence and semiconductor sectors of authoritarian rivals, they actively fund their own strategic vulnerabilities.
[P1.2] Advanced AI and semiconductors are the bedrock of next-generation warfare. They power autonomous weapons, hypersonic missile modeling, cyber-offensive capabilities, and mass biometric surveillance. Authoritarian regimes systematically erase the boundary between civilian enterprise and state power. Under doctrines of military-civil fusion, there is no meaningful distinction between a private tech startup and the military apparatus.
[P1.3] Any commercial breakthrough funded by foreign capital can and will be co-opted for state aggression or internal oppression.
[P1.4] Furthermore, outbound investment delivers far more than liquid cash. Venture capital and private equity represent "smart money." They inject invaluable intangible assets into rival ecosystems: managerial expertise, operational know-how, access to global talent networks, and reputational validation. While traditional export controls might successfully stop an advanced microchip or a piece of manufacturing equipment from crossing a border, those controls are entirely undermined if our own investors are allowed to finance and build an indigenous, self-sufficient tech ecosystem inside the rival nation.
[P1.5] Unscreened investment essentially pays to erase our own technological overmatch.
[P1.6] Allowing this unchecked capital flow creates profound policy schizophrenia. It is strategically incoherent for a country to enforce strict export controls on advanced technology—and spend billions securing its own domestic supply chains—only to let its private capital build those exact same capabilities in a rival state.
[P1.7] Outbound investment screening is not a blunt instrument of total economic decoupling; it is a highly targeted, necessary mechanism to close a fatal security loophole. By implementing these screens, nations ensure that their financial markets do not arm, legitimize, and empower the regimes most dedicated to undermining global security.

[C1.7] A capital screen is therefore all pain, no prevention: easily evaded, impossible to define cleanly, and guaranteed to expand. Today's 'strategic AI chip' becomes tomorrow's data center, cloud service, or university lab.
