It is vastly more expensive and disruptive to surgically upgrade an existing envelope than to build a high-performance one from the foundation up.
[P1.3] Second, we face a deadly carbon lock-in. Residential buildings last up to a century. Every code-minimum home built today guarantees decades of fossil-fuel reliance. [P1.5] Finally, the electrical grid demands it. As wealthy countries electrify transport and heating, peak grid load will surge. [P1.6] The opposition will fixate on marginal upfront costs to buyers.
