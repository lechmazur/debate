[P1.1] Zero-day exploits and commercial spyware are not ordinary goods. They are silent break-in kits: the power to enter a phone, read messages, switch on a microphone, and leave almost no trace. Permitting their sale to non-democratic regimes does not create neutral security cooperation. It arms systems that lack independent courts, a free press, and real limits on the secret police.
[P1.2] That trade should be banned.
[P1.3] The foreseeable use is repression. Authoritarian buyers can aim these tools at journalists, opposition figures, lawyers, and exiles, and nothing in their institutions will stop them. A license does not install the rule of law. Once delivered, the seller cannot supervise each deployment, and the buyer will not confess abuse.
[P1.4] Export controls exist for this reason.
[P1.5] The harm does not stay inside the buying state. Phones belonging to dissidents, diplomats, and dual nationals become collection points for other countries' secrets. Exploits leak, get resold, or are reverse-engineered. A vulnerability sold as a foreign convenience can return against hospitals, elections, or the seller's own citizens.
[P1.6] Private profit is a poor reason to speed that diffusion.
[P1.7] The ban is the right instrument. It stops sales to non-democratic regimes; it does not forbid defensive use by democracies under warrant and oversight. The line is imperfect, as export categories always are, but it tracks a real difference in accountability. Democracies can research, patch, and share capabilities with trusted partners.
[P1.8] They should not maintain a respectable commercial pipeline into regimes that treat dissent as a security threat.
[P1.9] Critics will call enforcement incomplete and definitions fuzzy. Those are reasons to police the ban, not to keep licensing the trade. A prohibition need not catch every broker. It raises cost and strips financing, insurance, and official cover from a market that sells turnkey intrusion to the customers least entitled to it.
[P1.10] The gain is private profit. The cost is surveillance of people who cannot vote their rulers out, plus blowback to everyone else.
