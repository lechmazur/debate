[P1.5] Furthermore, failing to enforce these rules hollows out the local housing supply. By strictly enforcing nuisance rules—such as steep fines, loss of permits, and immediate shutdown of party houses—cities make this predatory business model less profitable, naturally returning homes to long-term renters and families.
