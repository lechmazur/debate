A cheap unit a disabled tenant cannot enter is not affordable housing; it is a dead end. [C1.6] Third, existing codes already require baseline accessibility and ground-floor adaptability. Adding blanket mandates on top of existing standards harms the very people it aims to protect. We can expand accessibility through targeted subsidies and retrofits without choking off the broader housing supply.
