[C1.3] The fact that fraud is prosecuted and punished is not evidence of low damage; it is evidence of how much electoral integrity matters.

[C1.5] Voter fraud removes that remedy. If citizens conclude that ballots are not counted honestly, they lose the basic mechanism for correcting every other form of misgovernment. [C1.6] The proposition mistakes background cynicism for foundational damage. A captured agency erodes confidence in a policy area; a fraudulent vote erodes confidence in the act that makes democratic correction possible. In wealthy democracies, that foundational harm is greater.
