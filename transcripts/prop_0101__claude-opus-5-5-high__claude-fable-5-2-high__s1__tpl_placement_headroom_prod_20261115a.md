[P1.2] When no one in Year 8 has an account, no one is excluded for lacking one.
[P1.3] Second, the alternatives lose on mechanism, not effort. [P1.4] They ask companies to weaken the engagement engine that is their revenue. [P1.5] Third, we already regulate this way. We don't hand teenagers alcohol and ask parents to install "drinking controls." [P1.6] CON must therefore show that leaving the burden on millions of separate households beats a clear rule that binds the companies profiting from the harm. Imperfect enforcement is not an argument against a floor; it's an argument for enforcing it.
