[C1.5] The pacing guide says move on; the child isn't ready; the teacher is no longer authorized to notice.
[C1.6] The equity argument cuts the other way, too. Struggling readers don't need a uniform sequence; they need diagnosis, which requires professional discretion.
[C1.7] And the systems that actually win at literacy — Finland is the famous case — pair rigorous teacher preparation with high classroom autonomy. The real policy lever is teacher knowledge, which makes autonomy powerful, not dangerous.
[C1.8] Teach teachers the science. Then trust them to use it. Replacing judgment with scripts treats the symptom and entrenches the disease.
