[P1.5] Extend it for months or years and the label fits.
[P1.6] Third, abolition beyond fifteen days costs safety nothing. The motion does not ban separation. A violent prisoner can still be moved, closely supervised, or housed apart. What ends is indefinite, contact-starved isolation.
