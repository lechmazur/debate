In-play sports betting works the same way: instead of one considered wager on a match outcome, the bettor faces a continuous stream of micro-decisions—next corner, next goal—each placed under emotional arousal and resolved within moments.

Slower forms inherently embed cooling-off periods: the trip to a betting shop, the wait for a draw, the pause between hands. Ultra-fast online products strip those frictions away, collapsing the gap between impulse and action to nearly zero.
[P1.4] Financial damage therefore accumulates at a rate slower gambling cannot match—rent money and savings can vanish in a single evening rather than over months. Problem gambling is concentrated among users of these rapid formats, not spread evenly across all gambling.
