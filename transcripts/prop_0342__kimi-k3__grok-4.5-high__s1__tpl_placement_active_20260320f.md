[P1.1] Schools add a core subject when the world changes enough that opting out stops being a real choice. Print literacy became universal because print saturated daily life. That threshold is now crossed: children meet synthetic images, chatbot answers, and engagement-optimized feeds before they can drive, vote, or open a bank account. The question is not whether they face manipulation, but whether they face it equipped.
[P1.2] First, universality. Optional modules reach the students who already choose them — usually the best-resourced and most digitally confident. Those most vulnerable to scams, conspiracy content, and AI-generated deception are the least likely to elect in. Making the subject core is the only structure that reaches everyone, for the same reason we do not offer fractions as an elective.
[P1.3] Second, this is foundational, not vocational. A student who cannot tell a deepfake from footage, who does not grasp why a feed serves them outrage, who takes a chatbot's fluent answer as fact, is handicapped in history, science, and health alike. We already treat writing as a skill exercised across the curriculum because it underlies every subject. AI and manipulation-resistant media literacy now sit at that same layer: they govern how students access everything else.
[P1.4] Third, only core status makes the teaching real. Optional modules are the first casualty of budget pressure, carry no assessment, and decay as the technology moves on. Core standing forces teacher training, standards, and continuous updating — the difference between mentioning deepfakes once in an assembly and building resistance systematically, year after year.
[P1.5] Expect the opposition to cite crowded timetables and costs. Every curriculum is crowded; the question is what we can least afford to leave to chance. A society that graduates students unable to distinguish evidence from fabrication pays for it in elections, in public health, in fraud. We made reading compulsory because print reached everyone.
[P1.6] Synthetic media has reached everyone. The symmetry is the case.
