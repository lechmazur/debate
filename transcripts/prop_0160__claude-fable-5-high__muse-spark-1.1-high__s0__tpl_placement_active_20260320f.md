[P1.3] Three reasons it must be the former. First, the harm profile is invisible and irreversible. If a phone malfunctions, you notice. [P1.6] Third, the medical pathway preserves the upside. Depression, chronic pain, paralysis — stimulation devices can genuinely help, and regulation is how those benefits reach people safely, with trained oversight and recourse when things go wrong.
[P1.7] Innovation survives regulation. Brains do not reliably survive experimentation-by-marketplace. That asymmetry decides this debate.
