[P1.5] A state that insures its future by hiding its past is practicing false economy.
[P1.6] The central clash is not money versus principle. It is between two models of statehood: a liability shield that outsources conscience to lawyers, and a moral agent capable of answering for what it did in its own name. Willingness to pay is not a side effect of a real apology; it is proof of it. A state should be more willing to apologize today, even at financial risk, because the only thing worse than the price of justice is the impunity of refusing to name it.
