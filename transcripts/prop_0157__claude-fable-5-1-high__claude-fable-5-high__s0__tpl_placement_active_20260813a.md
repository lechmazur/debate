[C1.1] The motion sounds like justice. Read it carefully and it collapses, because it commands the wrong actor to hand over something that cannot be handed over by command.
[C1.2] First, wealthy countries do not own vaccine manufacturing technology. BioNTech, Moderna, and Pfizer do. So a law requiring "countries" to share it is really a law requiring governments to expropriate private trade secrets on a timer. That means constitutional takings litigation, treaty disputes, and injunctions fought out in the middle of a pandemic — precisely when we need firms cooperating with governments, not suing them.
[C1.3] The proposition doesn't accelerate access; it converts a public health emergency into a courtroom.
[C1.4] Second, the fixed window creates a perverse incentive at the worst possible moment. If the clock starts at emergency authorization, firms facing forced transfer will delay authorization filings, fragment their IP across jurisdictions, or steer their best platforms away from pandemic pathogens entirely toward oncology and rare disease, where no mandate lurks. The mRNA miracle of 2020 happened because companies bet everything on speed. Tell them in advance that speed triggers confiscation, and the next pandemic gets a slower, thinner pipeline.
[C1.5] Third — and decisively — manufacturing know-how is tacit. It lives in engineers, cell cultures, lipid formulations, and quality-control routines, not in a document you can subpoena. Real transfer requires the originator's staff embedded on-site for months, voluntarily. Even the goodwill-driven mRNA hub in Cape Town needed years to approach production.
[C1.6] A legal mandate can force a company to email documents; it cannot conscript enthusiasm, and half-transferred processes produce substandard doses that poison vaccine confidence across the developing world.
[C1.7] Meanwhile, the actual 2021 bottlenecks — export bans, lipid and bioreactor shortages, rich-world hoarding of finished doses — are all fixable by governments directly, without touching IP.
[C1.8] So the choice is not mandate versus complacency. It is a litigated, incentive-wrecking, unworkable mandate versus tools that actually move vaccines. Choose the tools.
