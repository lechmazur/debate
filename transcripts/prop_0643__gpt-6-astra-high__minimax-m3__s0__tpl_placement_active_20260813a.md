[P1.2] The central issue is whose risks corporate governance recognizes. [P1.3] Yet decisions about closures, automation, and restructuring are generally overseen by boards workers have no direct role in choosing. That imbalance needs correction.
[P1.4] Worker representation also improves the information reaching the board. [P1.5] This is not a requirement to preserve every job forever. Worker directors must confront the company’s finances, and shareholder-elected directors can still approve necessary restructuring.
