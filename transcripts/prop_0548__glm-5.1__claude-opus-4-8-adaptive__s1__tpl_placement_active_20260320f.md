A shooter who expects police on the corner within minutes faces a probability of arrest that shapes behavior. [P1.4] Meanwhile, longer sentences for repeat offenders produce diminishing returns. Most violent offenders age out of crime by their late twenties and thirties. [P1.5] The core comparison is immediate, location-specific prevention versus delayed, offender-specific punishment that arrives only after the body falls. Hot-spot policing with tight accountability reduces harm where it clusters and when it matters. Sentence lengthening reacts to harm already done.
