Rational ministries respond by routing early signals through informal channels, slowing lab reporting, and telling clinicians to escalate internally first.
[C1.3] The motion does not open the black box; it teaches everyone else to build one.
[C1.4] Early crisis messaging is not vanity. It is the instrument that tells a city whether to close schools, whether to trust the clinic, whether to flee. [C1.6] The outbreak that kills millions is the one never reported. This motion makes reporting riskier, not easier.
