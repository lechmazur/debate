[P1.5] The proposition does not ban family content. It demands that when parents build commercial enterprises around their children, those children receive statutory earnings protections and a future right to have their image and likeness removed from that commercial archive. The principle is straightforward: parental authority does not extend to treating a child's identity as unrestricted business capital. We limit parental decisions that cause lasting harm in other domains; permanent digital exploitation of a child's image for profit warrants the same boundary.
