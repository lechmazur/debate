[C1.4] Now the "unpenalized" non-monitored option. When safe drivers accept monitoring and earn their discount, the remaining pool is, on average, riskier. [C1.5] PRO calls the resulting price gap a "toll." It is the risk difference, finally made visible.
[C1.6] The mandatory-insurance point cuts against PRO. Precisely because you cannot exit the market, you should want the pricing method that rewards what you control, not what you cannot.
