[P1.2] Housing scarcity is not merely an inconvenience. [P1.3] Preservation rules cannot be evaluated only by what remains standing; they must also be evaluated by who cannot afford to live there.
[P1.4] Historic districts regulate entire neighborhoods, not just singular monuments.
