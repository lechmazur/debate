[C1.2] First, the promised mental health benefits are built on sand. We are trading certain, irreversible physical alterations for speculative, unproven mental health improvements.
[C1.3] Second, the biological costs of these interventions are permanent. Halting natural puberty compromises bone density. [C1.4] An adolescent—whose brain is still developing—cannot realistically grasp what it means to permanently forfeit future fertility. "Informed consent" at age fifteen for lifelong medical dependency is a clinical fiction.
[C1.5] Finally, "careful screening" is not a shield against long-term regret. Human identity is highly dynamic during adolescence. The growing cohort of detransitioners includes many who were once deemed "ideal candidates" under clinical protocols. When medical intervention goes wrong, the physical damage is absolute.
[C1.6] Because we cannot reliably predict who will desist, and because the evidence of benefit is so weak, subjecting vulnerable adolescents to irreversible medicalization does far more harm than good.
