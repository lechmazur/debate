[P1.2] That is why public funding for youth sports, arts, and clubs should be treated as the stronger tool. These programs intervene at the mechanism. A girl in a funded theater club is not just busy; she rehearses conflict without humiliation, learns to be seen without being rated, and has adults who can spot withdrawal early.
[P1.3] A boy in a neighborhood league gains status from effort and cooperation, not from filming a fight for views.

[P1.5] Funding also reaches those least likely to be reached by messaging.
