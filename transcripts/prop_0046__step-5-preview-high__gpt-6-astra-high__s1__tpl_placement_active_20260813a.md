The motion asks whether society should license that market in consenting adults. It should not.

If it affects reproductive cells or later descendants, it imposes choices on people who never consented. Adults may accept some risk for medical treatment, but no optional aesthetic trait deserves unknown, potentially lifelong biological costs.
[P1.3] The second is coerced choice. Once height or symmetry become purchasable, the unenhanced baseline becomes a deficit.
