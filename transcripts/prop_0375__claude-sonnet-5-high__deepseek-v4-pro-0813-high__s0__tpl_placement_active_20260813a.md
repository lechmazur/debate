[P1.1] Three mechanisms make GLP-1 drugs a live competitive-fairness problem, and elite sport already regulates far smaller effects than this.
[P1.2] First, weight-class sports. Judo, wrestling, boxing, weightlifting, rowing's lightweight category — these all exist on the premise that body mass is the variable being controlled for, so that strength, leverage, and power translate fairly within a bracket. GLP-1 drugs let an athlete strip fat while sport-specific training preserves muscle, meaning two competitors can hit the same scale number with very different lean mass and strength-to-weight ratios. That is precisely the kind of artificial decoupling of "natural" bodyweight regulation from performance that weight classes were built to prevent.
[P1.3] An athlete who used to have to cut water and starve for 24 hours to make weight can now arrive lighter, better fueled, and stronger relative to the number on the scale than a rival who achieved the same weight the old way. That is not a hypothetical edge case; it is the exact mechanism weight-class rules were designed around.
[P1.4] Second, endurance sport. Power-to-weight ratio is the single most decisive variable in cycling, distance running, and rowing. A drug that reliably reduces body fat while blunting appetite-driven fatigue changes that ratio directly, without requiring the years of training adaptation that traditionally produced it. Sport already treats manipulating body composition through pharmacology as suspect — see the historic scrutiny of stimulant-based appetite suppressants and diuretics in exactly these disciplines.
[P1.5] GLP-1 drugs simply do the same job more effectively and with a far gentler side-effect profile, which makes them more attractive to misuse, not less relevant to regulation.
[P1.6] Third, look at the asymmetry of risk. Anti-doping bodies routinely restrict substances long before airtight causal proof of performance enhancement exists — that is the entire logic of the precautionary "risk to spirit of sport" clause. Given a plausible, mechanistically clear pathway by which GLP-1 drugs alter the very variables competitions are built to measure, the burden should fall on defenders of the status quo to show why this substance uniquely deserves a pass. Nothing about it does.
