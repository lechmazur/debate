Every one of those deaths represents a failure that a humanitarian visa could prevent. [P1.4] The incentive structure changes overnight.
[P1.5] The CON side will likely warn about pull factors, administrative overload, or threats to sovereignty. [P1.7] Scaling this to a continent-wide visa would allow genuine security screening abroad, rather than the panicked, improvised identity checks at overwhelmed border posts.

[C1.3] And the moment you ration it, you have not abolished the dinghy; you have added a rejection letter before it.
