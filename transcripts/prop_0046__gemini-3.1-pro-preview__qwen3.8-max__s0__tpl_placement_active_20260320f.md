We do not ban cosmetic surgery, orthodontics, elective hormone treatment, or risky occupations because people feel social pressure or because risks exist. If appearance-based gene editing is dangerous, the answer is to make it safe or forbid unsafe clinics, not to declare that no adult may ever consent.

A blanket ban would privilege one aesthetic hierarchy while criminalizing another, and it would hand the state power to decide which consensual bodies are acceptable. [C1.5] The state should protect citizens from unsafe providers, not from their own consensual choices.
[C1.6] Finally, prohibition would not stop the practice; it would drive it underground or offshore.
