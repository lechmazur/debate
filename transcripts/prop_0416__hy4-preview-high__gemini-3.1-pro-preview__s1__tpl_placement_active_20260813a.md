[P1.1] Egg freezing is sold as freedom: freeze now, decide later, keep your options open. That is exactly why advertising must carry a prominent dose of arithmetic. The procedure is not a reversible backup file; it is a probabilistic medical gamble whose odds are dominated by one variable clinics constantly soften—age at freezing. Ads routinely use baby photos and upbeat slogans, while the decisive facts sit in fine print or are disclosed only after a prospective patient is already in the chair.
[P1.2] The core question is whether ads may sell a feeling the numbers contradict. That mismatch is the harm.
[P1.3] Commercial speech that creates a strong holistic impression can be materially misleading even if every sentence is literally true. When an ad pairs "take control of your timeline" with images of healthy babies, a reasonable viewer infers that freezing eggs at 38 is a reliable bridge to motherhood later. In reality, the chance that a batch of eggs frozen at 38 yields a live birth is far lower than the age-untethered success figures that dominate marketing, and using those eggs often requires more than one attempt.
[P1.4] Patients systematically underestimate how steeply outcomes fall with age and how much uncertainty remains even after freezing. They also underestimate attrition: eggs must survive thaw, fertilize, become embryos, implant, and result in birth. That chain is not intuitive, and marketing that highlights the endpoint while burying the conditional steps exploits that gap.
[P1.5] The remedy is modest and familiar. Financial products must display risk and past-performance limits prominently because salience shapes decisions; the same logic applies when a clinic profits from hope. Requiring prominent age-based success rates and clear statements of uncertainty does not ban egg freezing or dictate choices. It simply ensures the ad's emotional promise is answered by the numbers that actually determine outcomes.
[P1.6] Autonomy is not respected by hiding the odds behind a consultation; it is respected by making the odds impossible to miss.
