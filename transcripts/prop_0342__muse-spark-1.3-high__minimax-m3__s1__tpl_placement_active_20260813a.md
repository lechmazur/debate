[P1.1] Reading became compulsory when print became the gateway to work and citizenship. That gateway has moved. [P1.2] The danger is not future and abstract. A student who asks a chatbot for sources can receive confident, fabricated citations. A clip that sounds exactly like a principal announcing a closure can circulate before lunch. A political claim can reach millions with synthetic images attached.

[P1.5] Core status is what buys trained teachers, dedicated time, and real standards; everything else is a poster on the wall.
[P1.6] Core does not mean crowding out reading or arithmetic. It means protecting them. History is meaningless if a student cannot tell an archive from an AI forgery. Science is meaningless if a sleek video outweighs evidence.
