[P1.2] Payment processors and app stores are gateways, not merely customers choosing what to buy. A lawful market is not meaningfully accessible if its essential intermediaries can collectively treat legality and compliance as irrelevant.
[P1.3] The proper dividing line is demonstrable operational risk. Require reliable age controls, consent safeguards, fraud prevention, and compliance with applicable law. Investigate credible concerns and suspend services where justified. Those are reasons connected to actual conduct and harm.

[P1.6] The comparison is straightforward: exclusion protects a platform’s preferred image by imposing concentrated economic costs on people doing lawful work.
