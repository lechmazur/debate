Neurologically, minors are uniquely vulnerable: the prefrontal cortex that governs impulse control and long-term judgment does not fully mature until the mid-twenties. [P1.3] Yet markets adapt. Developers can still monetize through direct cosmetic purchases, battle passes with known rewards, or straightforward subscriptions. [P1.4] Default protections and strict spending caps are the minimal floor, not an overreach.
