Extending service to 4am or later directly solves this by dispersing departures across hours, preventing the chaotic street surges that trigger fights, vandalism, and impaired driving.

[P1.5] Earlier closings may trim alcohol sales but fail to address the transport gap that creates the conditions for both disorder and drunk driving. Transit extensions tackle the root cause through systematic crowd management and inclusive service, whereas closing times merely shift problems in time and space.
