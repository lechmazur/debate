[P1.3] The clearest benefit is preventing avoidable harm among otherwise lawful buyers. Point-of-sale screening cannot deliver that protection because it asks about disqualification, not competence.
[P1.4] A federal standard also makes those safeguards consistent across purchases. Changing sellers or crossing state lines should not remove the obligation to qualify.
