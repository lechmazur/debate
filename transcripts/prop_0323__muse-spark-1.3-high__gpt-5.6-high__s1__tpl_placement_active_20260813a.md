[P1.1] When every post wears its score on its forehead, people stop talking and start auditioning. [P1.2] The costs are personal and predictable. Teenagers delete photos that stall at low likes. Everyday users self-censor unpopular but sincere views. Creators chase outrage, shock, and panic because the meter rewards it instantly.
[P1.3] Comparison becomes constant because the numbers make hierarchy unavoidable. You cannot simply tell people to ignore the counts when the entire interface screams that counts are what matter.
[P1.4] The costs are civic too. A false claim with huge likes arrives pre-legitimized; most scrollers take popularity as proof. [P1.5] Substance loses to momentum.

[P1.8] That choice changes incentives without banning ambition. Outrage farmers lose the visible crowd they need to stampede others, while thoughtful posters gain room to breathe.
