When the state or a museum decides unilaterally how the crime is displayed, it repeats the original dispossession—taking from the community even the story of what was taken.
[P1.3] Second, epistemic quality. Descendants hold oral histories, kinship records, and burial knowledge that professional teams simply lack. [P1.5] Note what the motion actually says: substantial control, not absolute veto.
