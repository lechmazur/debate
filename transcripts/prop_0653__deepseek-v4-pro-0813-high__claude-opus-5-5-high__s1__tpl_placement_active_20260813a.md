[P1.1] The case for a ban is simple: these systems do not measure misconduct or poor work. They speculate about lawful future choices and latent traits, then let employers act on that speculation as if it were fact. That turns employment decisions into preemptive punishment.
[P1.2] A worker has every right to quit. If an AI scores her as a high flight risk, she may be denied training, mentorship, or a promotion long before she has made any decision. The employer is penalizing her for a freedom she has not exercised. Unionization likelihood is even more dangerous.
[P1.3] Labor law protects the right to organize, but a "unionization risk" score lets an employer weed out likely union supporters before they sign a card. The algorithm becomes a laundering device for anti-union discrimination.
