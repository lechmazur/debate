[P1.5] The proposition does not demand isolationism. It says scale back when the costs outweigh the benefits. That threshold is real and reachable. [P1.6] Leagues have preseason tours, exhibition matches, and media partnerships to build global reach. They do not need to sacrifice competitive integrity and local loyalty to chase it.
