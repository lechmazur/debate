[C1.2] Second, the pause is the wrong instrument even where baselines matter. The real choice is not “approve blind or stop everything”; it is what conditions attach to approval. [C1.3] That is targeted and enforceable. The common heritage principle calls for governed use with strong safeguards, not indefinite abstention.
[C1.5] Finally, uncertainty is a reason for adaptive management, not paralysis. Approve small, closely monitored projects where baselines are sufficient, with expansion contingent on performance. Irreversibility cuts for strict conditions, bonds, and no-go areas—not a universal stop. PRO’s standard is not precaution; it is a demand for perfect knowledge before any governance.
