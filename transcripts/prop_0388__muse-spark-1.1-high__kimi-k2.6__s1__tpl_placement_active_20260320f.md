[C1.5] The claim that voluntary parental controls fail because the business model "depends on them failing" is a conspiracy theory dressed as evidence. Platform-level tools on iOS, Android, PlayStation, and Xbox already let parents block purchases, require spending passwords, and restrict content by age. [C1.6] Finally, the category "gambling-like monetization" is hopelessly vague. If it covers any random reward, it sweeps in benign systems from Mario Kart Tour to digital trading cards. We should improve the parental tools that already exist, not flatten an accessible entertainment ecosystem with a regulatory sledgehammer.
