[P1.1] A safe-third-country rule is not a memorandum between governments; it is a decision about one person's life. The proposition sets three conditions — enforceable rights to work, to appeal, and to family unity. Each is necessary because each closes a specific hole through which transfer stops being protection and becomes export of harm.
[P1.2] Appeal first. Returning a claimant to a third country rests on a political judgment that the country is safe. If that judgment cannot be challenged on the merits, with suspensive effect where removal risks irreparable harm, the destination state has immunized itself from the very duty it invokes. Refoulement does not require a ticket to the persecutor; it requires sending someone where they will not be protected.
[P1.3] Effective appeal is what makes a designation more than a label.
[P1.4] Work rights second. "Safe" cannot mean a place where someone waits a year or more for a determination with no lawful income. Access to wage-earning work has always been part of what refugee protection means, and the Refugee Convention reflects that. A claimant barred from work is destitute, exploitable, or pushed onward — which destroys the orderly-processing rationale the rule is supposed to serve.
[P1.5] Family unity third. A transfer that splits a parent from a child or one spouse from the other is not neutral allocation of jurisdiction; it punishes people for asking for protection and breeds the very secondary movement such rules claim to prevent. Destination states write reunification law. They can build unity into any scheme they impose.
[P1.6] Two clarifications block the easiest escapes. "Only when" is a necessary condition: the proposition never claims these three are sufficient, so pointing to further requirements proves nothing. And "enforceable" is doing real work — paper promises without a remedy, including reinstatement of the claim in the destination state when the third country fails, are the standard device for laundering an unjust scheme. The test is not whether the rights appear in an agreement.
[P1.7] It is whether the person can make them stick.
