[P1.2] The equity case is also a business case. Platforms that open verification and reporting to everyone see measurable drops in fraud complaints. [P1.3] In other words, the expense of providing free safety is offset by savings from fewer lawsuits, fewer bans, and higher lifetime value per user.
[P1.4] Critics argue free safety will erode revenue, but the evidence points the other way. Moreover, emerging regulations such as the EU’s Digital Services Act push platforms toward baseline safety standards; making those standards free pre‑empts costly compliance later.
[P1.5] If dating apps treat safety as a premium add‑on, they signal that protection is a luxury. The proposition flips that signal: verification, blocking, and scam response become the default, because a platform that protects all its users protects its own future.
