[C1.2] If you mandate a cap on sugar, manufacturers substitute synthetic sweeteners, sugar alcohols, and highly refined bulking agents like maltodextrin. They game the metrics while degrading the actual nutritional profile.
[C1.3] The food remains ultra-processed, but now it is chemically altered in ways that carry their own metabolic risks. Mandates do not fix ultra-processed food; they just force it into a different, equally synthetic formulation.

[C1.5] The result? It drastically shifted consumer purchasing across all socioeconomic demographics and, crucially, forced the industry to voluntarily reformulate products just to avoid the black label.
[C1.6] Chile’s campaign achieved the exact supply-side changes my opponent praises, but through market incentives rather than bureaucratic micromanagement of food chemistry.
