[P1.3] Labor law has answered this question everywhere else. A security guard isn't paid only when tackling an intruder. A barista isn't paid only while pulling shots. [P1.4] Gig platforms have simply discovered an accounting trick: define "work" as task-completion seconds, and the minimum wage becomes a fiction. [P1.6] The minimum wage works precisely by forcing employers to internalize the cost of the labor they summon.
