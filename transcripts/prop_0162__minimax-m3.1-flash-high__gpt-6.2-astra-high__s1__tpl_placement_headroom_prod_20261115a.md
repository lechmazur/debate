[P1.2] The comparison is stronger than the “recycling is expensive” slogan. [P1.4] Consider a city facing a new shortage: it can either pipe raw or partially treated water across mountains, committing years and public money to an external source, or upgrade treatment at its own wastewater facilities and build distribution to nearby neighborhoods. For water-stressed cities, the right priority is therefore to make direct potable reuse the backbone of new supply investment, using careful monitoring, multiple barriers, and public-health regulation—not to postpone resilience behind a distant transfer.
