Without explicit consent and compensation, they are appropriating a living person’s identity for profit.
[P1.2] The core of our case rests on two pillars: the right of control over one’s own performance, and the economic principle that creation generates value. Actors already negotiate usage rights for each project. AI de‑aging or synthetic voice does not preserve the original performance; it creates a derivative that can be reused indefinitely, marketed, and even altered further. [P1.4] Requiring explicit consent and compensation does not kill innovation. Studios can still experiment; they just must negotiate terms, just as they do for stunts, prosthetics, or CGI.
