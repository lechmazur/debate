A pipeline rupture or drought curtailment interrupts supply; other sources remain available. Diversion lowers probability but not consequence. “Comparable cost” is incomplete: comparison must include monitoring, redundancy, skilled staffing, energy, concentrate disposal, and public-health consequences.

[C2.5] Drought-sharing clauses do not prove imports unreliable; they show dependable transfers require flexible quantities, shared risk, storage, conservation, and diversified supplies. [C2.6] Where it does not, importing more water can be the responsible—and conservative—choice.
