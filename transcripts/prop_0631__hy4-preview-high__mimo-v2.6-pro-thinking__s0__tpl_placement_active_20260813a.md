[P1.1] The Constitution roots copyright in "Authors." That word has meant human creators. A generative model is not an author; it is a statistical engine that recombines patterns from training data. If no human can show substantial creative control over the final expression, then the output is machine expression, and extending copyright to it would abandon the human authorship requirement at the core of U.S. law.
[P1.2] This proposition does not ban AI-assisted work. It simply demands evidence. A photographer who chooses lighting, angle, and timing and then edits the image has authored expression. So has an artist who paints over, crops, arranges, or otherwise transforms model output into a chosen whole.
[P1.3] But typing a generic prompt and accepting whatever the model emits is closer to commissioning a slot machine than writing a story. Copyright protects choices, not luck. A bare prompt is an instruction; the expressive elements are produced by the model, not the human.
[P1.4] The documentation rule is administrable and fair. It tracks ordinary authorship practice: drafts, edit histories, selection notes, layered files. It does not require perfect records, only enough to show the human shaped the expression rather than merely requested it. This filters out attempts to claim ownership over raw AI output while preserving protection for genuine human creation.
[P1.5] Without it, we invite a tragedy of the anticommons: thousands of near-identical AI images or texts owned by whoever generated them first, clogging licenses and litigation, without any new human creativity to justify the monopoly.
[P1.6] CON will call AI merely a tool. But tools execute; authors decide. When a machine supplies expressive choices, the human must show they supplied them too.
[P1.7] Rewarding undocumented AI output would shift copyright from protecting creativity to protecting compute and chance. The public domain would gain only stochastic filler, while licenses, litigation, and prices multiply. Keep copyright tied to human authorship: no documented substantial creative control, no copyright.
