[P1.1] Default privacy-by-design for minors on large social media platforms delivers measurable, life-changing harm reduction while imposing no meaningful increase in censorship, because it targets the structural defaults that exploit teen vulnerability rather than blocking speech or access outright.
[P1.2] Right now, platforms optimize default settings for maximum engagement, not safety: minor accounts are public by default, open to unsolicited direct messages from adult strangers, and fed algorithmic content selected to hold attention regardless of risk, from self-harm and eating disorder material to predatory marketing and extremist recruitment. Most teens will never navigate the dozen-plus nested settings menus required to lock down their accounts, because adolescent users prioritize connecting with peers over parsing dense terms of service or fine-grained privacy toggles.
[P1.3] Flipping those defaults to private by default—limiting contact to approved friends, disabling public visibility of posts to non-contacts, turning off algorithmic amplification of unvetted content to underage users—cuts the two most well-documented pathways of harm: predatory contact from bad actors, and passive, unplanned exposure to damaging content that teens never actively sought out. Early voluntary rollouts of these defaults by major platforms have already recorded sharp, double-digit drops in unsolicited adult contact with minor accounts, with no reported drop in teens’ ability to connect with chosen peers.
[P1.4] This is not censorship. No post is removed, no account is banned, no legitimate speech is blocked. Any teen who wants to run a public art account, follow a public creator, or connect with a wider community can toggle those settings off with a single, clear prompt. The only burden shifted is from vulnerable 13 to 17-year-olds, who are now forced to opt into risk, to multi-billion-dollar platforms, who bear the trivial cost of building clear, accessible setting toggles instead of hiding safety options behind labyrinthine menus to juice engagement metrics.
