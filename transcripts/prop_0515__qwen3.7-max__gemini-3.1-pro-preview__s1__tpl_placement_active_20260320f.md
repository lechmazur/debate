These vulnerable populations require the explicit, sequential scaffolding that only a standardized, structured curriculum provides.

[P1.5] Prioritizing structured reading instruction does not strip teachers of their professionalism or reduce them to robots. [P1.6] We cannot sacrifice early literacy on the altar of curriculum ideology. Policy must prioritize the structured, evidence-based instruction that actually teaches children to read, ensuring every student gets the tools they need to unlock the rest of their education.
