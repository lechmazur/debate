[C1.2] A district with a tenth of the demand needs roughly the same number of idle cars to hit the same wait time, so each trip there costs many times more to guarantee. The mandate doesn't repeal that arithmetic. It just picks who pays.
[C1.3] And who pays is the poor. [C1.5] A geographic wait-time rule delivers none of it.
[C1.6] The analogies break, too. Utilities are monopolies with fixed wires; robotaxis compete with buses, rail, ride-hail, and private cars.
