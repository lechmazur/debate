For orbital space tourism, the per-passenger footprint dwarfs even premium air travel. [P1.5] We need only decide whether, with so many preventable deaths and unmet needs, this is the best use of concentrated wealth. It is not.
[P1.6] The standard defense cites inspiration, spinoffs, or future colonies. Those benefits are speculative, diffuse, and obtainable through public science and satellites without selling joyrides.
