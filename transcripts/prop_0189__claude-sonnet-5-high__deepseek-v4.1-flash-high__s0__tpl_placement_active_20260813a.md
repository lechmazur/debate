[C1.2] Targeted overflow management is not just mopping. [C1.3] PRO’s line in [P1.3]—that separated streets are “just wet, not toxic”—is the central error. Stormwater is a major pollution source: oil, heavy metals, sediment, bacteria, and litter. [C1.4] Separation can therefore create a permanent untreated discharge pathway while claiming purity. Climate change intensifies both systems’ limits; it does not make untreated stormwater benign.
[C1.5] Nor is separation failure-proof. Sanitary sewers still overflow from infiltration and inflow, blockages, power failures, and aging laterals. [C1.6] In older, poorer cities, that cost crowds out lead service line replacement, treatment upgrades, and flood protection.
