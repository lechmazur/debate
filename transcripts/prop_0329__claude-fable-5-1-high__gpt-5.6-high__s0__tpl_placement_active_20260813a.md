[P1.2] Consider who pays and who collects. [P1.4] That asymmetry is the whole argument. An institution that keeps the upside and exports the downside will systematically overreach, because its own ledger never registers the harm. Players cannot opt out of a scheduled game. Local fans cannot vote on it.
