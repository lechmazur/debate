[C1.4] PRO's claim that above roughly 15% interest, higher rates "create the losses" rather than offset them treats a complex credit portfolio as if every borrower is equally situated. [C1.5] The motion asks whether we should cap rates even knowing access is cut. The case for yes requires believing that no credit is better than expensive credit for people with thin options. [C1.6] Better policy paths exist: subsidized credit unions, reformed bankruptcy protections, income supports. None of them achieve their goals by locking the most financially precarious people out of the least-bad option available to them. This cap does exactly that.
