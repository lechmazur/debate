[P1.1] Golden visas rest on a simple lie: that citizenship and residency in a democracy are commodities like any other, priced and sold to the highest bidder. Europe should stop pretending otherwise and end these schemes.
[P1.2] Start with what they actually buy. In Cyprus, a program that handed out passports for roughly two million euros in real estate was shut down after investigators found the buyer pool riddled with individuals facing serious corruption or fraud allegations elsewhere. Malta faced years of EU pressure and legal action over the same basic problem: an EU passport, with its visa-free access across the whole bloc, was for sale to anyone who could wire enough money, with due-diligence checks that were thin, outsourced, and easily gamed.
[P1.3] That is not a niche defect. It is the business model. The entire value proposition to buyers is "fast, discreet, minimal scrutiny" — and every one of those words is a security and rule-of-law problem.
[P1.4] Second, look at what these programs do to ordinary citizens. In Lisbon and Porto, golden-visa-driven property buying has been widely cited as a major contributor to housing prices climbing far faster than local wages, pushing residents out of neighborhoods they grew up in. A program justified as attracting "investment" mostly just inflates the price of the roof over your head, while the jobs and factories politicians promised rarely materialize, because the whole point for the buyer was passive, hands-off capital parked in real estate.
[P1.5] Third, consider the integrity cost. These programs create a two-tier system of belonging: citizenship earned through birth, integration, taxes, and civic life for most people, versus citizenship purchased in a single wire transfer for the wealthy. That corrodes the very idea that membership in a political community means something beyond money.
[P1.6] The counterargument will be revenue and investment. But revenue extracted by selling the integrity of your borders and your democratic membership is not a bargain — it is a subsidy for corruption, laundered through property markets, paid for by every renter priced out of their own city.
