[P1.3] Saturating youth-heavy broadcasts with betting messages is not neutral commerce; it is conditioning at the moment of peak emotional attachment.

[P1.6] Refusal draws a clear boundary without banning adult betting. When kids fill the stands and the screens, the league’s partners cannot include firms whose core product thrives on compulsion.
