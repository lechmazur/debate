[P1.5] Dating—where self-worth, time, and intimacy are on the line—deserves the same floor of honesty. A plain notice that AI is ranking and shaping visibility is enough; no source code, no endless explainers, just the fact that the deck is algorithmic.
[P1.6] Without that right, platforms can quietly optimize for retention over well-being and users remain unable to give informed consent. With it, the imbalance narrows. Knowledge is the minimal condition for dignity in a market that sells connection.
