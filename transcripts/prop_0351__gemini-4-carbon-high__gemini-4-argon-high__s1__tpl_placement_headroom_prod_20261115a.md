Asking a basic question suddenly requires booking calendar time or posting in a public channel, introducing friction and status anxiety precisely when beginners need low-stakes guidance. Juniors still clear their daily queues, creating the illusion of parity while starved of judgment, context, and sponsorship.
[P1.3] Crucially, this erosion runs far deeper than either side admits because both are rewarded for looking away. [P1.4] Remote-friendly firms insist digital tools suffice so they can cut lease costs and recruit cheaply, while hybrid employers declare the problem solved even when senior staff stay home and leave juniors on video calls anyway. [P1.5] When quarterly ticket counts look fine, everyone declares victory.
