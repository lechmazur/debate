A rural pharmacist who denies emergency contraception may cause a multi-hour drive and a missed treatment window—a burden that strikes those with the fewest options hardest. A physician opting out of end-of-life sedation can leave a suffering patient stranded if no one else steps in. These are not rare hypotheticals; they recur wherever refusal is disconnected from a duty to ensure alternatives.
