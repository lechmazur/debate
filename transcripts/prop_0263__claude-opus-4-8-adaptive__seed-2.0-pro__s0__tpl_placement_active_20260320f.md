[P1.1] When a studio releases a film in theaters and on disc, that film exists forever. You can find a 1940s movie, a 1970s TV episode, a forgotten indie picture. [P1.3] My path is simple. First, cultural preservation. These originals are not disposable widgets; they are the cultural record of our time. [P1.5] Second, fairness to the people who built it. Writers, directors, actors, and crew sign on expecting their work to live, to be seen, to build careers and earn residuals.
