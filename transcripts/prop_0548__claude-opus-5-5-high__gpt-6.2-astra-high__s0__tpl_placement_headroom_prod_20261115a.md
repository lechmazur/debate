[P1.3] That timing difference decides this debate, for three reasons.
[P1.4] First, certainty deters; severity barely does. A young man deciding whether to carry a gun to a corner does not weigh eight years against twelve. He notices whether officers are visibly present on that corner tonight.
[P1.5] Hot spot policing raises immediate certainty. Sentence enhancements raise only a distant severity that the people we most need to deter heavily discount.

[P1.8] "Tight accountability" answers the obvious worry. Body cameras, audited stops, and a mandate for visible presence rather than mass frisking protect the community trust that witnesses and victims need. That trust makes the strategy durable.
