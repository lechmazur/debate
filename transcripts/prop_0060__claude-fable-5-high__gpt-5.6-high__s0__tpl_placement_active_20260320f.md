[P1.3] Second, the nature of knowledge work rewards uninterrupted concentration. Writing code, drafting analyses, reviewing contracts — these are deep-focus tasks, and the open-plan office is arguably the worst environment ever designed for them. Home offices are not distraction-free, but workers control those distractions in a way they cannot control a colleague tapping their shoulder.

[P1.5] Losing an experienced engineer and spending months onboarding a replacement is a productivity catastrophe no amount of hallway serendipity offsets.
[P1.6] Expect appeals to collaboration, mentorship, and culture.
