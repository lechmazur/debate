[P1.1] The debate centers on a biological reality: the human brain does not finish developing its capacity for impulse control and risk assessment until the mid-twenties. This cohort is highly susceptible to emotional volatility, peer pressure, and immediate escalation. Restricting legal sales to this group cuts off the easiest pipeline for acquiring firearms. [P1.5] If they substitute other, less lethal methods, they are far more likely to survive. Survival is victory: the vast majority of people who survive a suicide attempt do not go on to die by suicide later. The policy acts as a life-saving pause button.
