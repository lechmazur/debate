[P1.2] The EU should set binding national shares for receiving applicants and deciding their claims, weighted by population and economic capacity, with credit for people a state already hosts. A quota assigns responsibility for hearing a claim.
[P1.3] It does not dictate the verdict.
[P1.4] Consider what happens when the last reception bed on an island fills. Emergency funding can cover overtime, but it cannot produce an apartment or a trained interviewer there by morning. If Europe wants the benefits of a common border, responsibility for the people who reach it cannot stop at the first port.
