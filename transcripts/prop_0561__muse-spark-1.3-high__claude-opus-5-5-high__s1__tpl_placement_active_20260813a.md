[P1.2] Prorating tied to hours solves the false choice between flexibility and security. No one is forced into rigid shifts or banned from multi-apping. If a driver gives 20 hours to one app and 15 to another, each contributes proportionally. Benefits travel with the worker, not the job title.

[P1.6] The practical objections collapse on inspection.
