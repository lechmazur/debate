[P1.7] Third, we already accept this logic everywhere it matters. We don't run rural fire departments, rural roads, or rural electrification on strict profitability. [P1.8] So the burden I accept is simple: governments should ensure that no citizen's survival depends on their proximity to a profitable patient pool. My opponent must defend the opposite—that when the numbers point to consolidation, we should let geography decide mortality.
