[C1.2] Medicine has a hard boundary for a reason: do not intentionally give a potentially life-threatening or disabling disease to a healthy person. That boundary cannot be waived by paperwork. Consent does not transform a wrong into a right, it only shows someone was willing to bear it. [C1.5] The London 2006 case PRO cites proves the opposite point: when animal data is poor, first-in-human harm can be catastrophic.
