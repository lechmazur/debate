[P1.1] Mandating an always-on AI productivity companion transforms the fundamental nature of employment. It replaces discrete evaluations of output with relentless, second-by-second algorithmic scrutiny masquerading as "support." When software continuously tracks an employee's keystrokes, speech cadence, screen states, and workflow pauses to nudge and correct them in real time, it creates an inescapable digital panopticon. Human labor is reduced to a machine process calibrated for relentless optimization.
[P1.2] This is not genuine assistance; it is automated micro-management operating without pause.
[P1.3] The harms of this continuous presence are structural. Psychologically, perpetual monitoring destroys the basic psychological safety required for deep focus, creative risk-taking, and critical thought. Real-time nudges induce chronic cognitive strain, forcing workers into an exhausting state of performative compliance where even natural pauses are flagged as inefficiency. Professionally, this constant intervention deskills the workforce.
[P1.4] When an algorithm preemptively dictates how to phrase an email, pace a client call, or sequence a task, workers are stripped of agency and independent judgment. They become execution units for machine directives.
[P1.5] Crucially, the motion targets *mandates*. In an unequal labor market, individual workers cannot simply opt out when their livelihoods depend on compliance. Nor can "coaching" be divorced from discipline: every algorithmic suggestion carries an implicit threat that deviation will reflect poorly on performance reviews. Employers have a legitimate interest in the quality and timeliness of the work they purchase.
[P1.6] They have no right to colonize a worker’s moment-to-moment cognitive environment, turning the human mind into a leased server under continuous algorithmic surveillance. A clear prohibition is essential to protect human dignity, mental health, and professional autonomy.

[C2.4] Nor did AI invent total scrutiny. Call centers already record every syllable, warehouses already log every scan, managers already enforce quotas down to the minute. The difference is that an algorithmic whisper is legible, identical for everyone, and reviewable, while a human boss's memory is selective and unappealable. Scalable does not mean crueler; it can mean more consistent.
[C2.5] Every nudge carries weight only because every job instruction does. The fix is a firewall barring coaching logs from discipline and limiting retention, not forbidding the requirement to be coached. PRO concedes that danger while insisting regulation cannot work, but never explains why transparency and use limits solve data abuse everywhere except here.
[C2.6] Ban the mandate and the pressure remains while the help disappears: quotas stay, but workers lose the tool that lets them meet quotas safely and prove they did so fairly.
