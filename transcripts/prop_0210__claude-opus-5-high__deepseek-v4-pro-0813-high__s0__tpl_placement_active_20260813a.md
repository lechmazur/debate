[C2.1] PRO treats my narrower reporting duties as a concession, but that narrowing is the whole argument. A rule tied to approved uses and regulator access targets the decisions that actually reach patients. The paroxetine case cuts the other way: off-label prescribing and regulator inaction drove the harm. A universal disclosure condition attached to adult market approval would not have changed the label, warning, or prescribing behavior that mattered; it would simply have released an ambiguous negative dataset without the interpretive controls clinicians need.
[C2.2] That is a spill, not a scalpel.
[C2.3] PRO’s noise reply ignores gatekeeping. Positive trials do not reach practice unfiltered; they are submitted, reviewed, summarized, and approved before shaping care. The mandate would give negative trials no equivalent filter. Meta-analysts may want every null, but the press, patients, courts, and payers do not read a null as “uninformative.” They read it as “the drug failed.” Underpowered or endpoint-mismatched failures would become courtroom exhibits and formulary cuts, even when regulators and competent analysts know they do not justify those conclusions.
[C2.4] On compliance, PRO’s “satisfy the strictest once” claim misses the real cost. Publication is non-rival, but legal and regulatory exposure is not. Countries disagree about redaction, patient-level privacy, timing, and what counts as reportable. A filing sufficient in one jurisdiction can be noncompliant or over-disclosing in another, and can be mined by litigants in a third.
[C2.5] The strictest interpretation becomes the global floor, and no single posting removes the parallel liability.
[C2.6] Finally, tiered timelines do not rescue small developers. They postpone, but do not reduce, the reputational and liability cost of a secondary negative finding, which can destroy a partnership or license for a one-product company. The mandate still concentrates its worst costs on the least-resilient firms while adding little beyond stronger confidential regulator access and targeted reporting duties.
