[P1.2] Proactive takedowns fail on their own terms. They require platforms to act as instant, opaque truth tribunals for inherently contestable claims. [P1.3] You don't stop a rumor by hiding it; you drive it underground where it radicalizes.

[P1.5] It respects user autonomy - readers see claim and correction together and decide.
[P1.6] It also works better operationally. Notes scale to millions of borderline political claims without requiring a central team to pre-judge truth in seconds.
