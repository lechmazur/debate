The result is less accurate intelligence about adversary capabilities, not more.
[C1.6] The wiser path is targeted end-use and military-specific controls, not a broad restriction on advanced equipment. The proposition asks us to accept certain major costs in exchange for an uncertain, porous delay. That is not strategic necessity; it is strategic self-harm.
