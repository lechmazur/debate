[P1.3] A fixed handoff requirement draws that line in a way that is enforceable and predictable. Mental-health risk is often quiet and indirect. A human professional is trained to notice patterns, ask follow-up questions, assess lethality, and coordinate care.
[P1.4] A chatbot can be programmed to output safety text, but it cannot bear responsibility for the judgment that the moment has passed.

[P1.7] This also answers the access objection.
