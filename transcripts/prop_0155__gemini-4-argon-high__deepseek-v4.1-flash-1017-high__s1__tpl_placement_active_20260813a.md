[P1.1] The difference between a free society and a surveillance state is whether the police must suspect you of a crime before they investigate you. [P1.2] Real-time surveillance must be prohibited because it destroys public anonymity and triggers dangerous street encounters. Worse, live scanning forces split-second policing on flawed data. Real-world feeds suffer from bad lighting, awkward angles, and motion.

[P1.5] Furthermore, post-incident use removes the hair-trigger urgency of a live alert. Detectives can treat a facial match as a preliminary lead rather than final proof, corroborating it with alibis, physical evidence, or witness statements before making an arrest.
