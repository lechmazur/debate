[C1.4] Treating these schools as inheritance institutions would gut the very policies—need‑blind admissions, generous aid, merit‑based recruitment—that produce those outcomes. Policy should therefore continue to view elite universities as engines of fairness, not as relics of privilege. The proposition’s push to tax endowments and ban legacy would cripple the aid that lets low‑income students attend, harming the very mobility it claims to champion.
