[P1.2] My case rests on three pillars.

[P1.6] Retreat removes the population before the failure, converting a potential disaster into a logistics problem solved on our timeline, not the storm's.
[P1.7] Third, opportunity. [P1.8] Note carefully: the motion says "on balance" and "prioritize." I am not banning every wall around a dense port. I am arguing that the default policy and the bulk of the money should fund orderly retreat, not a doomed fight to hold every shoreline.
