[C1.4] The proposed line—people with “no safe alternative shelter”—is unworkable on the ground. That ambiguity guarantees either arbitrary, unequal enforcement, or total abandonment of basic public space rules.
[C1.5] Most critically, eliminating routine enforcement removes the only consistent leverage to connect people to sanctioned, safe parking sites with sanitation, security, and on-site outreach to permanent housing.
