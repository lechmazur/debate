[P1.5] It preserves community ties that reduce reoffending. The evidence is overwhelming: cash bail doesn’t improve public safety; it just punishes poverty. For nonviolent offenses, supervised release and court reminders are fairer, cheaper, and more effective. Justice should not be for sale.
