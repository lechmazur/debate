[P1.1] Children do not suffer from a shortage of screens; they suffer from a deficit of physical, social, and creative presence. When public schools decide where to direct their limited after-school resources, they must prioritize what students cannot easily get elsewhere: team sports, tactile arts, and unstructured play.
[P1.2] First, physical and embodied activities are irreplaceable for developmental health. After sitting at desks and interacting through classroom devices for seven hours, children do not benefit from another two hours of screen-based enrichment. They need gross motor movement, sensory exploration, and the nuanced, face-to-face social friction that only physical collaboration provides. Unstructured play and team sports build emotional regulation, physical health, and conflict-resolution skills in ways purely digital environments fundamentally cannot replicate.
[P1.3] Second, public schools bear an equity mandate. Digital tools are scalable and relatively easy to access outside school; any child with a basic device or public internet can access coding tutorials or digital design software. But a child cannot download a soccer field, a pottery studio, musical instruments, or a safe, supervised playground. When public schools underfund physical programs to chase digital expansion, lower-income students are locked out of embodied enrichment entirely, while affluent families simply purchase private club sports, art studios, and dance lessons.
[P1.4] Third, resource allocation must respond to the realities of youth well-being. Expanding purely digital enrichment worsens the crises already confronting modern students: chronic sedentariness, screen fatigue, and social isolation. Arts, athletics, and free play serve as the vital antidote, anchoring children in real-world community, peer connection, and embodied confidence.
[P1.5] Before allocating funds to additional digital interfaces, public schools must first guarantee that every child has a field to run on, an instrument to hold, and a safe space to play without a screen.
