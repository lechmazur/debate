[P1.2] Relocation replaces a public argument about power with a public argument about interpretation, and museums remain places where communities can contest framing.
[P1.3] The proposition’s key phrase is “preservation with context.” A statue removed from a pedestal is not magically neutralized; it may become a museum artifact precisely because it has a powerful story.
