[C1.2] Most people cannot translate aims such as “inform me without making me anxious” into ranking parameters, nor predict the downstream effects of doing so. [C1.3] A teenager can select more sensational content; someone in a harmful spiral can request more of precisely what reinforces it. Platforms should bear duties of care rather than outsource them to a settings page.

[C1.5] Finally, recommendation has externalities. Individually chosen feeds can collectively deepen ideological isolation, accelerate rumors, and remove common informational ground.
