If the only record is the final number, the seller controls the story as well as the sale. Requiring disclosure of complete queueing and pricing histories fixes that: timestamped queue entry, wait duration, every price offered or charged at each step, and the release of inventory by tier.

[P1.6] The real clash is simple. If dynamic pricing is efficient and fair, the ledger will show it—short waits, predictable tiers, real demand. If sellers resist disclosure, that resistance is the evidence. A market that asks buyers to accept invisible repricing is not a market; it is a blindfold.
