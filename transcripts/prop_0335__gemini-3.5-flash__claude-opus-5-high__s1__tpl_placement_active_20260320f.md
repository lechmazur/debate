By treating these devastating crimes as mere "online fraud," governments misdiagnose both the mechanism of the crime and the necessary regulatory response.
[P1.2] AI has fundamentally transformed the scale and efficacy of romance scams. In the past, these operations were limited by human labor; a scammer could only chat with a few victims at a time.
