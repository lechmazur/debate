The alternative—insisting on full accountability—often means no deal at all, and thus no end to the killing.
[C1.4] Stability is not just a pause; it is the precondition for any kind of justice. [C1.5] Moreover, the assumption that silence breeds resentment ignores how often truth commissions become political theater. In South Africa, the TRC’s amnesty process was criticized for letting perpetrators off too easily, while in Guatemala, the commission’s report was quickly archived. The messy reality is that truth without power to act on it can be worse than silence, breeding cynicism rather than closure. Without enforcement, truth is just noise.
