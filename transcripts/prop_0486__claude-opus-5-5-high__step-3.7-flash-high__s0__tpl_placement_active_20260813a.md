Land-scarce cities have far less disruptive, proven ways to address burial land pressure: vertical columbaria, multi-level underground burial, and opt-in plot reuse with explicit family consent and compensation, none of which require scrapping the entire burial system for untested public alternatives.

PRO also glosses over the hidden resource costs of their preferred methods: large-scale alkaline hydrolysis requires millions of gallons of water annually, a non-starter in the water-stressed, land-scarce cities the motion targets, while human composting demands regulated facilities, transport, and monitoring that carry their own environmental and financial burdens.
[C1.4] The motion’s one-size-fits-all mandate ignores both the dignity of the dead and the needs of the living communities it claims to serve.
