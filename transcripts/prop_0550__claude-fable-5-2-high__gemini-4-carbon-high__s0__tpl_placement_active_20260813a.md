[P1.1] The question is not whether college graduates out-earn everyone else. It is where the next public dollar buys the most lifetime earnings, and for whom. On that question, apprenticeships win.
[P1.2] Start with who an expanded college subsidy actually reaches. Students who were certain to attend already do.
