[P1.1] Flagship benefits are not neutral cafeteria-plan items. A flagship is the benefit an employer uses to advertise its values. When that emblem is egg freezing, the message is: your eggs can wait; the job cannot. That message is honest only if starting a family earlier is already realistic at work.
[P1.2] Otherwise the company does not solve the work-family conflict; it moves the conflict into women's bodies and calendars, then calls that relocation empowerment.
[P1.3] The relevant choice is not freeze or nothing. It is whether an employee can have a child now—pregnancy, treatment, recovery, infancy, a sick child—without losing income, promotion, or status. Egg freezing addresses almost none of that; it is mainly insurance against postponement. Paid leave, fertility coverage, predictable schedules, caregiver protections, childcare, and supported returns address the conditions under which families begin.
[P1.4] If those supports are weak, the employer is applauding the one option that asks nothing of the workplace.
[P1.5] So the autonomy defense is thin. Adding a costly option to delay does not expand freedom when having children now remains professionally punishing. With patchwork leave, unreliable childcare, and managers rewarding constant availability, there are not two respected choices; there is one marketable choice and one expensive choice. The flagship recruits women under a promise the workplace does not keep, while giving the employer progressive credit without changing the norms that make early family formation risky.
[P1.6] It is also an unequal emblem. Egg freezing is most usable by employees with money, time, clinic access, and security. Those most exposed to weak caregiving support—hourly staff, lower-paid workers, single parents, people with complicated fertility—need help around birth and after, not a glossy symbol of delay. The unless clause demands honesty: egg freezing may be offered, but not put on the marquee unless strong leave, fertility care, childcare, and caregiver-safe careers make earlier family formation a real option.
