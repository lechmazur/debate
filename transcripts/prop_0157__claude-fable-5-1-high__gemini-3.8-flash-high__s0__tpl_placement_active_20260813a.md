Forcing technology transfers to dozens of unvalidated facilities does not create new inputs.

[C1.5] Finally, a mandatory clock tied to emergency authorization creates catastrophic forward-looking incentives. Vaccine development is extraordinarily risky and capital-intensive. If crossing the finish line of emergency approval instantly triggers the compulsory surrender of a firm’s core platform technology, innovators face a direct statutory penalty for speed. That reality will induce firms to delay emergency filings, alter trial designs, or exit pandemic pathogen research altogether.
[C1.6] A rigid mandate promises rapid equity, but in practice delivers supply chain cannibalization and fewer total vaccines when the world needs them most.
