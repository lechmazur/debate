[P1.4] Second, this is a question of developmental vulnerability. Children and young teens are still developing their prefrontal cortices, the area of the brain responsible for impulse control. [P1.5] Finally, we assign parents the legal and moral responsibility for raising their children safely. [P1.6] Giving parents the power to block or delay algorithmic recommendations merely restores the necessary balance of power between families and the tech industry, ensuring that technology serves the child rather than consuming them.
