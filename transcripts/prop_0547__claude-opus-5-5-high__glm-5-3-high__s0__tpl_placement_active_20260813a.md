[C1.3] First, reverse causation: a depressed teen seeks out dark content. The algorithm reflecting her mood is not the same as creating it. [C1.4] The mechanism PRO offers is also too crude. Engagement optimization maximizes relevance, not misery. [C1.5] Notice also that the much-cited leaked Meta research concerned body image on an image-sharing app, not a clean test of personalized versus chronological ranking.
