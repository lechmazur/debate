[P1.1] Picture a warehouse worker who gets a termination notice after a productivity score dips, or a nurse whose application is filtered out in eleven seconds by a résumé screener tuned on a decade of past hires. Both may have real legal claims — disability discrimination, disparate impact, breach of a contractual process. Neither will ever bring one, because neither knows what actually decided their case. That is the whole debate in miniature: we have already granted workers rights against arbitrary and discriminatory treatment, and we are quietly letting those rights expire through ignorance.
[P1.2] Disclosure is the enabling condition for every protection we already say we believe in. A worker cannot contest reasoning she cannot see, cannot request an accommodation from a system she doesn't know is scoring her, and cannot tell her lawyer what to subpoena. A human hiring manager leaves traces — an interview, a conversation, a colleague who noticed something off. An automated screen leaves nothing but silence.
[P1.3] So the effect of non-disclosure is not neutral; it is a one-way ratchet that makes rights weakest exactly where decisions are made fastest and at the largest scale.
[P1.4] Note how modest the obligation is. The motion asks for disclosure that a system meaningfully influenced the decision — not publication of source code, not model weights, not a right to veto. A sentence in a rejection email and a line in a termination record. If that sentence is genuinely commercially catastrophic, the honest reading is that the business model depends on people not knowing.
[P1.5] And disclosure disciplines the buyer. Right now a company can procure a screening tool, never audit it, and face no reputational or legal consequence because no one outside knows it exists. Make the use visible and the incentives flip: vendors get asked harder questions, HR departments validate before deploying, and bad tools die faster.
[P1.6] The alternative CON must defend is a system where the most consequential decisions in a person's working life are made by a process they are not permitted to know occurred.
