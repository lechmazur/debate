[P1.3] The cap model assumes all minutes are equal, but they are not. Thirty minutes of coding, collaborative play with friends, or a documentary watched with a parent differs fundamentally from thirty minutes of passive, isolated consumption designed to hijack attention. [P1.4] What shapes media's impact is context: what children consume, why, and who is present. [P1.5] Effective advice should shift from counting minutes to cultivating judgment. Parents need frameworks for asking: Is this content enriching or exploitative?
