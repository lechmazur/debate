Venice’s curbs on the largest ships were not a tantrum against visitors; they were an attempt to keep a living city alive.
[P1.5] A cap on cruise and bus arrivals is the same logic applied before the damage becomes irreversible.
[P1.6] So the clash is simple. My opponent must defend unlimited bulk arrivals even in cities already buckling under overtourism.
