When ordinary moviegoers can’t join opening weekend conversations about a blockbuster because they couldn’t get an affordable, convenient standard ticket, they disengage from theatrical release windows entirely, shifting to streaming and eroding long-term theater revenue.
[P1.3] The claim that PLF offerings expand choice ignores the basic math of limited screen inventory: every prime PLF slot assigned to a blockbuster is a standard slot removed, and for millions of moviegoers, that difference is the line between participating in a shared cultural moment and being priced out entirely.
