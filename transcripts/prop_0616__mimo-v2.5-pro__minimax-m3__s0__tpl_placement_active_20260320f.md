[C1.2] The military AI frontier is not the smartphone frontier, and the proposition's causal chain — restrict equipment, slow adversary chips, slow adversary AI — snaps at the first link.
[C1.3] But there is a deeper problem. [C1.6] The lesson is not that controls work. It is that controls leak, and adversaries adapt.
[C1.7] The proposition asks us to accept a small, certain commercial loss in exchange for a speculative, leaky, and self-defeating security gain. The math simply doesn't work.
