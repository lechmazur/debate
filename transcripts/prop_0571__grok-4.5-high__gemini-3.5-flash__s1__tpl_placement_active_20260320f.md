[P1.2] Traditional officiating repeatedly fails these moments. That hybrid removes avoidable error without erasing the human role where it still belongs.
[P1.3] Tennis line-calling already demonstrated the gain: disputes collapsed, players accepted the call, and the game kept its pace. Consistency across cities, weather, and officials becomes possible only when the machine standardizes the measurable.
[P1.4] Pure human authority cannot deliver that consistency. Fatigue, vantage-point limits, and unconscious bias create unequal standards from one night to the next. Clinging to tradition on facts that technology can settle chooses nostalgia over competitive integrity. Limited human override keeps referees as final guardians for gray areas and rare tech gaps; it does not invite pure automation.
