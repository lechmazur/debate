[P1.1] Sea level rise is not a temporary problem to be held back with concrete. Every dollar wealthy nations sink into building and rebuilding hard coastal defenses is a dollar wasted on a losing battle, while communities sleep behind walls that will fail catastrophically, without warning.
[P1.2] Hard defenses never win long term. Every major seawall system built in the last 50 years has required repeated, exponentially more expensive upgrades, or breached during extreme storms. When walls fail, they do not erode slowly: they give way overnight, trapping people who were told they were safe. There is no wall that can be built high enough, or maintained forever, to outrun multi-meter sea level rise this century.
[P1.3] Managed retreat is not abandonment. It is planned, compensated, voluntary relocation of homes, critical infrastructure, and entire neighbourhoods before disaster strikes. [P1.4] Wealthy nations have the unique ability to do this right now. Waiting will double the cost every decade, while millions more people settle into zones that will be uninhabitable within two generations.
[P1.5] This is not surrendering the coast. It is choosing to protect people instead of protecting concrete. Eventually the water will win. The only choice left is whether we leave ahead of time, or get carried out in the flood.
