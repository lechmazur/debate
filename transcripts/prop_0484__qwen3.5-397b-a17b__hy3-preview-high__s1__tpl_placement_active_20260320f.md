[P1.3] Current metrics fail because they measure power, not peril. Population counts bodies, not vulnerability. GDP measures wealth, not exposure. A billionaire can lose money and survive; a nation can lose land and cease to exist.
[P1.4] When negotiations prioritize economic output over physical survival, the system incentivizes delay. Major emitters calculate the cost of transition against the cost of damage. [P1.5] This proposal is not about privilege; it is about veto power over annihilation. In any domestic legal system, a party facing irreversible harm receives heightened protection. [P1.7] Furthermore, existential weighting sharpens ambition. When the most vulnerable hold the keys to consensus, lukewarm compromises become impossible. Negotiations shift from cost-sharing to survival-sharing. We accept weighted voting in financial institutions based on contribution; we should accept it in climate forums based on consequence.
