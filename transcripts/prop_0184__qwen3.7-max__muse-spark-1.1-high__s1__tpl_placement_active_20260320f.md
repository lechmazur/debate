[P1.5] They leak, waste millions of gallons of treated water, and harbor dangerous bacteria like Legionella. Chemical anti-corrosion treatments do absolutely nothing to fix structural water loss or biological risks.
[P1.6] By issuing debt to replace physical infrastructure, a municipality converts a perpetual, risky operational liability into a permanent asset that solves multiple systemic failures at once. Furthermore, physical replacement guarantees equitable protection; chemical treatments often fail first in marginalized neighborhoods with the oldest, most degraded plumbing. We cannot manage a modern water crisis with chemical bandaids. The only acceptable baseline for public health is the physical removal of the toxin.
