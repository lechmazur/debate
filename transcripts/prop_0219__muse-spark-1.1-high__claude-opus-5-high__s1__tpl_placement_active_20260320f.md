[P1.1] Pay gaps don't survive because employers consciously price a job differently by race or gender for the same posting. They survive because of information asymmetry and discretion. Without a posted range, candidates have to guess what to ask for, rely on insider networks, and negotiate blind. Research consistently shows women and Black and Latino workers have less access to that informal pay knowledge, are penalized more when they negotiate aggressively, and are anchored lower by prior salaries.
[P1.2] That discretion is where bias leaks in, even without intent.
[P1.3] A mandated range changes the default. The employer commits publicly to a floor and ceiling tied to the role, not the person. That floor prevents lowball offers that otherwise stick disproportionately to women and people of color. It gives every applicant the same anchor, so the negotiation starts from the job's value, not the candidate's perceived desperateness or previous underpayment.
[P1.4] It also makes it far harder to justify paying two people in the same title vastly different amounts without a documented reason, which is exactly how audits close gaps.
[P1.5] The supposed efficiency cost is thin and backwards. Hiring efficiency is hurt most by wasted screens, ghosted candidates, and late-stage offer collapse because pay expectations were misaligned. A range filters out mismatches on both sides early. Recruiters report fewer unqualified applicants expecting far above budget, and applicants don't burn hours on jobs they can't afford to take.
[P1.6] That's improved matching, not worse.
[P1.7] Will some employers post absurdly wide ranges? Some will try, but public scrutiny and competition quickly punish $50k-$200k postings. Even imperfect ranges beat no information.
[P1.8] The comparison is lopsided: a small, possibly positive effect on speed of match versus a structural correction to a gap that costs workers thousands a year for identical work. Transparency trades a little employer flexibility for a lot of fairness, and the labor market keeps matching.
