[P1.5] Second, asymmetric exploitation of developing minds. Adolescence is precisely the period of heightened reward sensitivity, weak impulse control, and acute social insecurity. [P1.6] Third, the ban is workable and narrow. Contextual advertising—showing sneaker ads on a sports site—remains fully legal.
