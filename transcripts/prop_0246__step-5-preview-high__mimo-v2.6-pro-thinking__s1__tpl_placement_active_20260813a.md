[C1.2] Chronological feeds have a brutal volume bias. Content appears in the order it is posted, so the people who show up in your feed are the ones who post constantly: the obsessed, the professional provocateur, the harassment campaign running twenty accounts. Ranking systems at least weight toward what a given user engages with. Chronological order weights toward whoever shouts most often.
[C1.3] The worst actors already treat posting volume as a weapon—spam floods, rapid-fire replies, coordinated raids. A strict timeline converts their stamina directly into visibility.

[C1.7] Chronological default would not have made social media kinder. It would have made it faster, louder, and more hospitable to whoever is willing to post the most.
