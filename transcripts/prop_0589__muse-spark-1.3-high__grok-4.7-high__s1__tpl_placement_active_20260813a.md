[P1.1] Gun violence is not spread evenly across a city. A very small number of blocks and a very small network of young men at extreme risk drive most shootings, and most shootings are retaliatory. That structure decides the best tool.
[P1.2] Additional patrol officers mostly arrive after shots are fired, take reports, and leave. They cannot live inside the feud. Civilian interrupters can. Hired because they come from the same streets and carry credibility no uniform can buy, they learn of an insult, a robbery, a shooting, and get to the friends and cousins before the revenge shooting happens.
[P1.3] Hospital-based workers meet victims at the bedside when motivation to retaliate is hottest and offer safety planning, money, jobs, relocation. This is prevention aimed precisely at the next trigger-pull, not general presence hoping to deter.
[P1.4] That precision is why the dollars go further. A single additional police officer costs a city a full salary plus overtime, benefits, pension, academy training, equipment, supervision, and liability — easily well into six figures every year for decades. A civilian outreach team costs a fraction of that and focuses entirely on the handful of people most likely to shoot or be shot. One team working a hotspot can mediate dozens of conflicts a year that otherwise end in emergency rooms.
[P1.5] Per shooting prevented, targeted mediation is simply cheaper than buying citywide coverage one expensive badge at a time.
[P1.6] Experience bears this out. Where cities have allowed programs like Cure Violence, Advance Peace, and hospital intervention to operate steadily, evaluations have repeatedly found large, double-digit drops in shootings in the zones served, while nearby comparison areas stayed violent. Richmond, California’s turnaround from a homicide capital is the famous example. The binding constraint has rarely been effectiveness; it has been permission — short-term grants, bans from hospitals and jails, political vetoes when a program touches someone controversial.
[P1.7] If reducing shootings per dollar is the test, hire credibility, not just authority.
