[P1.2] First, trading creates competition for household generation. A homeowner may receive little for exported solar while a nearby renter pays substantially more for electricity. [P1.3] Renters gain a way to participate in rooftop solar without owning a suitable roof.
[P1.4] Second, a broader market can make useful solar investment more attractive. If households can seek competing buyers rather than accept one export offer, projects that otherwise look uneconomic may become worthwhile. [P1.5] Trading enables those arrangements without requiring everyone to adopt them.
