[P1.3] In the United States, the Department of Education’s 2022 report documented dozens of cases where undisclosed Chinese funding led to suppression of research on topics deemed sensitive by Beijing, including work on Uyghur detention. These concrete incidents demonstrate that without mandatory disclosure, universities become unwitting conduits for state propaganda, compromising the very mission they claim to serve.
