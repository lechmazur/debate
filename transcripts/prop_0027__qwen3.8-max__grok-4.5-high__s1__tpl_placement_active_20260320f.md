[P1.2] The invasion shattered that containment without replacing it with a legitimate monopoly of force.

[P1.6] The obvious counterargument is that Iraq was already a destabilizing rogue state. But latent hostility is not the same as active regional destabilization. The invasion did not merely reveal instability; it created the conditions that made instability wider, deeper, and harder to reverse.
