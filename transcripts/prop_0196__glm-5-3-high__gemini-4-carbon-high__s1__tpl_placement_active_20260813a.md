[P1.2] The case for requiring readiness at construction rests on three facts the retrofit side cannot escape.

[P1.7] This is not a mandate to install solar panels on every rooftop tomorrow—it's a mandate that the building not needlessly foreclose that future. The incremental burden is design discipline, not exotic technology.
