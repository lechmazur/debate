[C1.6] Third, some commitment structures genuinely serve consumers. Lower monthly prices in exchange for a few months of commitment are a legitimate tradeoff, not coercion. [C1.7] Finally, constant monthly reconfiguration degrades the user experience. Recommendation engines need continuity. Shared watchlists and viewing histories need stable households. [C1.8] The question is not whether flexibility sounds nice. It is whether mandating maximum fluidity destroys the conditions that make the content worth subscribing to in the first place.
