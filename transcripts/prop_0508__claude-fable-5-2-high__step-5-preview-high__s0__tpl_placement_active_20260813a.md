[C1.3] Durability matters just as much. Tariff commitments are enforceable, treaty-locked, and reciprocal. [C1.6] Exporting it to blocs with divergent legal traditions invites backlash that can kill broader agendas.
[C1.7] PRO also overstates the value. Digital services already cross borders with a click; the real bottlenecks are data governance and consumer protection, not professional credentials.
