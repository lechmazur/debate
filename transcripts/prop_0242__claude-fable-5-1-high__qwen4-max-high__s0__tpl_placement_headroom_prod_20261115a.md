[P1.1] Measure polarization by what actually happened to democracies, and the comparison isn't close. Cable news is a slow, bounded, mostly American phenomenon. [P1.3] The mechanisms differ in kind, not just scale. Cable is something you choose to turn on; it preaches to viewers who already agreed. [P1.4] So every day, millions of ordinary people are shown a curated highlight reel of their opponents' cruelest, dumbest moments.
