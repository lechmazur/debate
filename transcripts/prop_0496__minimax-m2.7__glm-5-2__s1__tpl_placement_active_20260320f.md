[P1.1] The core of democracy is simple: the people who authorize violence must answer to the people who might die from it. [P1.5] An American soldier who commits a war crime faces court-martial under the Uniform Code of Military Justice. A contractor who commits the same act hires lawyers and points to fine print.
[P1.6] The profit motive compounds the problem. Contractors have financial incentives for continued operations, extended contracts, and expanded scope. [P1.7] Banning contractors from combat roles does not eliminate private industry from the battlefield. Logistics, intelligence analysis, and technical support can remain in private hands.
