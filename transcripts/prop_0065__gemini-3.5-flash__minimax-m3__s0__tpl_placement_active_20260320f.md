[P1.1] Allowing parents to select embryos based on non-disease traits like height, physical appearance, or predicted cognition shifts reproduction from a lottery of nature into a market of consumer optimization. Even if this technology becomes perfectly reliable, legal restriction is necessary to prevent profound social harms.
[P1.2] First, unrestricted screening will transform socioeconomic inequality into permanent, heritable biological stratification. These technologies will inevitably be expensive and disproportionately accessible to the wealthy. When affluent families can systematically select for traits like height or predicted cognitive performance, existing class privileges become hardcoded into the human genome. This creates a biological caste system, destroying any semblance of meritocracy or social mobility.
[P1.3] Second, it fundamentally alters the parent-child relationship, replacing unconditional acceptance with consumer expectation. When a child is selected for predicted traits, they are no longer welcomed as an independent individual, but as a designed product. A child chosen for high cognition or height carries a heavy burden of instrumentalized expectations. Failure to manifest these selected traits harms the child's autonomy, while success merely fulfills a parental purchase order.
[P1.4] Finally, allowing non-disease selection creates a coercive race to the top. If some parents begin selecting for taller or more "optimized" children, others will feel forced to do the same simply to keep their children competitive. This feedback loop pressures families into a genetic arms race, leading to a homogenized society where natural human diversity is suppressed in favor of narrow, commercialized ideals.
[P1.5] We must draw a firm regulatory line. While medical interventions aimed at preventing severe disease and suffering are compassionate, selecting for cosmetic or competitive advantages is an exercise in consumer eugenics. Legal restriction is the only way to preserve human equality, diversity, and the intrinsic dignity of human life.
