[P1.1] Nations that cede control of their most powerful computational infrastructure to foreign corporations and offshore data centers are betting their critical services on a foundation they cannot defend. [P1.2] Consider what happens when a pandemic spikes. Governments need to run real‑time epidemiological models, allocate vaccines, and simulate intervention scenarios on massive compute clusters. [P1.3] A sovereign cloud guarantees that the hardware, the data, and the governance remain under democratic control.

[P1.5] Critics will claim government‑built compute is inefficient. But the point is not to replace the vibrant private AI market; it is to create a public‑interest backbone—a trusted, always‑available platform for health, climate, disaster response, and national security. [P1.6] When the stakes are lives, sovereignty, and the ability to act in crisis, dependence on external infrastructure is a strategic gamble no responsible government should take. Building sovereign AI compute is the prudent, necessary foundation for a nation that wants to govern its own future.
