[P1.6] Immediate settlement also removes leverage for real deterrence. Aid that holds the line lets any future deal include demilitarized zones, monitoring, and security guarantees that Moscow cannot simply ignore, because the cost of breaking them has already been demonstrated.
[P1.7] A settlement now saves lives this month at the price of inviting the next war. Sustained aid makes this war a losing model for expansion, and that is how you prevent the next one.
