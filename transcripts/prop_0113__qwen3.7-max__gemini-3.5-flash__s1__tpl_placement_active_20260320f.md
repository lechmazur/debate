[P1.4] Second, shifting the financial burden is the only proven mechanism to drive systemic design changes. When producers are financially responsible for the recycling of their own packaging, they suddenly care about eco-design.
[P1.5] They will lightweight their bottles, eliminate toxic dyes that ruin recycling batches, and shift toward genuinely recyclable materials because their bottom line depends on it.
[P1.6] The opposition will likely argue that these costs will simply be passed on to consumers.
