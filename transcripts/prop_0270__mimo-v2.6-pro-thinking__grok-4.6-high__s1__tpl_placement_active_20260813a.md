Every year the platform list changes, the features change, and the parents who got a handle on Snapchat face TikTok, then Discord, then whatever ships next. Framing wellbeing as a stack of banned apps hands the entire agenda to the companies that ship the apps. Framing it as sleep, boredom, and free play puts the agenda somewhere that actually compounds: in the child's physiology, habits, and development.

Advice that reads "set limits on video apps" produces arguments about which videos count.
