[P1.2] End-to-end encryption ensures that only communicating parties hold the keys. Forcing providers to build exceptional access—whether via master keys or client-side scanning—creates a catastrophic central point of failure. If a backdoor exists, hostile states, ransomware syndicates, and predatory hackers will eventually exploit it.
[P1.3] That systemic vulnerability exposes critical infrastructure, banking systems, government secrets, and the private data and locations of millions of innocent children to the worst actors on earth.
