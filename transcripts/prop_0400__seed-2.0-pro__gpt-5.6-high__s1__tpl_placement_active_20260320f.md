A system can process ordinary cases automatically while guaranteeing notice, reasons, correction, rapid escalation, interim relief, and independent appeal. They do not waste scarce attention on cases where the rule and record are uncontested.

A reviewer cannot invent an exception the law forbids, may never learn the missing fact, and can rubber-stamp or discriminate. Calling that person a “decision-maker with actual authority” merely smuggles in a different right: broad discretionary power to waive rules. That power must itself be lawfully defined and reviewable; once it is, exceptional cases can be routed to it without giving everyone a categorical entitlement to manual adjudication.
[C5.3] The decisive test is not whether government symbolically “sees” you, but whether it reliably fixes wrongful outcomes. PRO mandates the actor, not the remedy, while consuming the human capacity needed for the hardest cases.
