[P1.1] Poor indoor air is a silent drain on public health and economic productivity. [P1.2] First, the status quo is failing. [P1.3] Voluntary guidelines have proven insufficient because they allow building owners to prioritize short-term cost savings over occupant health.
[P1.4] Second, the cost of inaction is quantifiable. Enforceable standards act as a preventative public health measure, similar to how we mandate sanitation in restaurants or safety barriers in construction.
[P1.5] We do not leave "cleanliness" up to the discretion of individual business owners; we recognize that collective standards are necessary to protect the public.
