[P1.1] The leftover tariffs inside functioning trade blocs are small change. [P1.2] Mutual-recognition deals for digital services and professional credentials should therefore come before another round of tariff cuts. Recognition turns formal membership into usable access. When members accept each other’s cybersecurity certifications, electronic signatures, or cloud standards, a firm sells once and complies once. [P1.6] Prioritize does not mean abandon goods. It means sequence the work to the binding constraint. The last generation of integration lowered tariffs. The next one trusts each other’s rules and credentials.
