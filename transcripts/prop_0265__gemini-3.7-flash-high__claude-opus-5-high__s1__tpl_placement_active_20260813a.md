[P1.1] When a fee is mandatory, it is not a gratuity; it is simply the price of the food. Concealing unavoidable service charges until the bill arrives is classic drip pricing—a tactic that misleads consumers, distorts fair competition, and erodes basic market transparency.
[P1.2] First, basic consumer autonomy requires accurate upfront pricing. Diners make economic choices when they open the menu, balancing their budget against listed items. If a restaurant tacks on a non-negotiable fifteen or twenty percent fee at the end, it exploits the fact that the customer has already consumed the meal and cannot walk away. A mandatory fee is, by definition, an unavoidable condition of purchase.
[P1.3] Hiding it in small print or revealing it only at checkout forces patrons into a bait-and-switch transaction after their leverage is entirely gone.
[P1.4] Second, hidden fees punish honest businesses and warp market competition. Consider two restaurants offering an identical eighteen-dollar dish. The transparent restaurant prints eighteen dollars. The deceptive competitor prints fifteen dollars and adds a mandatory twenty percent surcharge on the final receipt.
[P1.5] The second restaurant captures foot traffic through artificial price suppression, triggering a race to the bottom where menu prices become meaningless fictions. Requiring all-inclusive pricing levels the playing field, ensuring businesses compete on actual value rather than hidden fee architecture.
[P1.6] Finally, mandatory charges are operational costs, not discretionary tips. Restaurants routinely use menu revenue to pay for rent, electricity, ingredient inflation, and base wages. Labeling a mandatory portion of that overhead as a separate service charge at checkout is an accounting shell game. It muddies the distinction between genuine tips for service staff and mandatory revenue for the house.
[P1.7] The principle is straightforward: if a customer cannot opt out of a charge, that charge is the price. The only honest place to display it is directly on the menu.
