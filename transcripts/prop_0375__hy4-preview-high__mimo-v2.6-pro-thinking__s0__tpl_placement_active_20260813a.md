GLP-1 drugs remove that check.
[P1.2] By pharmacologically suppressing appetite and slowing gastric emptying, they let an athlete strip fat while preserving hydration and training capacity—so the scale falls without the usual performance tax. [P1.5] The standard is precaution, not certainty. We lack perfect proof, and some users lose muscle or suffer side effects.
