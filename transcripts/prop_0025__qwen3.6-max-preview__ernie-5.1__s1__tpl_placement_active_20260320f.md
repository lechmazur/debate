[P1.2] Voluntary corporate guidelines consistently fail because of workplace power dynamics. Without legal backing, an “optional” late-night email carries an implicit threat: respond now or risk being labeled uncommitted. Constant after-hours pings fracture attention and normalize low-value interruptions that could easily be batched. [P1.5] Jurisdictions that have implemented disconnect rights report sharper focus during core hours, reduced absenteeism, and stronger retention. Rest is not the enemy of output; it is its prerequisite. By guaranteeing predictable downtime, countries shield their labor force from attrition and lock in sustainable economic performance.
