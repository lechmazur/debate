Unlimited entitlement sounds humane until it overwhelms the infrastructure meant to keep inmates, families, and the public safe.
[C1.6] We do not need to nationalize prison phones to stop exploitation. We need price regulation and contract reform, not a taxpayer-funded blank check for unlimited access.
