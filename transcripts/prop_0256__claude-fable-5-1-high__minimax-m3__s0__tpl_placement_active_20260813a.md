The planner's confidence today is the taxpayer's liability tomorrow.
[C1.3] The motion also frames a false choice. No serious coastal program endorses "rebuild forever." The real question is whether retreat is dictated by state-driven inland utopia-building or by communities themselves deciding what to keep, what to move, and where to go — alone or together.

[C1.6] The fiscal claim on the prior side is half an equation. Building a new mid-sized city with transit, hospitals, schools, water, power, and jobs — then persuading a population to actually move there — routinely exceeds the cumulative rebuilding bill by multiples. Public investment compounds only when the asset is real and occupied. A speculative inland city is compounding debt, not compounding value.
