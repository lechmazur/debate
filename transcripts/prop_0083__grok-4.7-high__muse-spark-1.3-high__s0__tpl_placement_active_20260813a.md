[P1.1] Dating is a search for a real person, not a product demo. An AI-generated face or AI-drafted messages sell an identity the user does not fully own. Platforms should treat that as deception unless it is clearly disclosed, because a profile exists so another person can form accurate expectations about whom they might meet.
[P1.2] A photo is the first evidence of appearance, age, and presence. Generated images can smooth skin, invent features, or assemble someone who does not exist. The viewer cannot tell, so attraction forms around a fabrication. Meeting later becomes a reveal, not a confirmation—the structure of a bait-and-switch, cruelty optional.
[P1.3] Conversation help is subtler and just as costly. Wit, warmth, timing, and curiosity are how people judge fit. If a model supplies those qualities, the other user bonds with a script. Evenings, vulnerability, and hope go to a voice that disappears offline or when the assistant is switched off.
[P1.4] Disclosure does not ban the tool. It lets the recipient decide whether they are flirting with a person, a prompt, or both.
[P1.5] Platforms already police fake profiles, stolen photos, and scam accounts because trust is the product. Undisclosed AI sits on that same line: it makes the recipient believe something materially false about identity or authorship. Labels such as “photo generated by AI” and “messages assisted by AI” are cheap and legible. Silence is the deception.
[P1.6] Without that rule, everyone must assume the charming face and the perfect reply might be synthetic, and honest users are punished for competing against invisible enhancement. Platforms should not levy that tax on truth. Treat undisclosed AI photos and AI conversation help as deceptive, and require clear disclosure. Anything less asks people to fall for a version of someone who is not quite there.

[C1.5] Judge deception at the point that actually matters: does the person who shows up resemble the photos, and can they sustain the connection the messages promised? If yes, there was no bait-and-switch. Do not turn everyday self-presentation into fraud for failing to file paperwork.
