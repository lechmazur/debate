[P1.1] A 62-year-old grandmother opens Instagram after dinner to look at photos of her new grandchild. There is no legitimate public or user benefit to forcing political content on people by default; the only parties that gain are platforms profiting from engagement and bad-faith actors exploiting algorithmic amplification to spread falsehoods.
[P1.4] For user autonomy, public health of online discourse, and basic respect for user choice, social media platforms must end default political content recommendation.
