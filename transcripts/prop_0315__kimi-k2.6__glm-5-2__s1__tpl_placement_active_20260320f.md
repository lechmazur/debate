[P1.2] Transparency is an integrity mechanism, not a spectacle. Publishing referee audio and detailed explanations exposes sloppy process without exposing officials to gratuitous personal abuse. [P1.3] Either outcome serves the sport better than managed silence.
[P1.4] Fuller disclosure also respects the intelligence of a modern audience. A referee calmly explaining why contact was incidental rather than penal transforms an opaque delay into an educational moment that deepens appreciation for the game’s complexity.
[P1.5] The status quo is not a neutral default. It is an active choice to prioritize bureaucratic comfort over competitive honesty.
