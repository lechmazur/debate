[P1.1] A fair workplace judges people by what they actually do, not by what an algorithm guesses they might do next year. Banning AI predictions of an individual worker’s future productivity, attrition, or unionization draws a vital boundary between legitimate management and corporate pre-crime.
[P1.2] Consider what happens when a company scores individual flight risk or future output. Predictive models cannot read minds; they rely on behavioral and demographic proxies: altered badge-swipe times, medical leave, slower message replies, or commute changes. When an algorithm flags a caregiver or an employee managing a chronic illness as a "flight risk" or predicts a productivity dip, managers inevitably pull back. They withhold promotions, training, and raises.
[P1.3] The prediction becomes a self-fulfilling prophecy, punishing workers for choices they have not made based on opaque correlations they cannot contest.
[P1.4] The danger of predicting individual unionization likelihood is even starker. There is no benign use for scoring whether a specific employee will organize. Its only commercial purpose is preemptive suppression: isolating, transferring, or quietly cutting potential organizers before a union drive ever gains legal protection. After-the-fact labor laws cannot police this, because black-box scores let companies launder illegal retaliation behind routine scheduling or restructuring decisions.
[P1.5] Moreover, generating these individual forecasts requires an invasive surveillance dragnet, tracking keystrokes, physical movements, vocal tone, and workplace friendships. No worker should have every glance and pause mined to feed a vulnerability score.
[P1.6] Crucially, this ban does not blind management. Companies remain completely free to evaluate individuals on their actual, historical work, and to use aggregate, team-level analytics to identify burned-out departments or broken workflows. When a warehouse or office has high turnover, the solution is to fix pay and management across the board, not to deploy predictive AI to target, sideline, or silence individual workers.
