[P1.4] The promised payoff is less abuse. That payoff is smaller than the sales pitch. People harass under their real names every day. Coordinated campaigns, workplace retaliation, and state-aligned pile-ons become easier when the target is named and searchable.

Platforms already have blunt instruments against abuse—blocks, rate limits, spam detection, content rules—none of which require publishing a legal name. Forced identifiability is not those tools.
[P1.7] It is a spotlight. For dissidents, whistleblowers, and the already endangered, the spotlight is the harm. The residual drop in trolling does not pay for it.
