[P1.2] First, the directive is the last reliable expression of autonomy. When she wrote it, she could weigh dignity, suffering, dependence, and burden. Dementia later impairs judgment, but it does not retroactively make that earlier decision inauthentic. If we discard it merely because the person now seems content or accepts food, we let the disease’s symptoms override her settled values.
[P1.3] A woman may have refused artificial feeding after watching a parent die with advanced dementia. Her later mouth-opening for a spoon is not clear evidence that her values changed; it may be a disease-driven reflex.
