The lightweight materials and life support tweaks developed for these joy rides have negligible, slow-moving spillover benefits compared to direct investment in climate adaptation or poverty alleviation.
[P1.4] Worse, the industry already draws tens of billions in public subsidies that could instead expand off-grid solar access to 200 million people without electricity, or fund climate-resilient farming for 50 million smallholders. Ethics demands we prioritize urgent, collective needs over exclusive, pointless excess. When people are starving and the planet is burning, space tourism is not justifiable.
