[P1.2] Managed retreat is the only strategy that doesn't require the ocean to cooperate.
[P1.3] First, hard defenses are a ratchet, not a solution. Every wall must be raised, maintained, and eventually rebuilt, forever, against a rising baseline. [P1.5] Second, rebuilding in place is a subsidy for repeated loss. [P1.6] After Hurricane Sandy, Staten Island neighborhoods like Oakwood Beach chose buyouts, and that land now absorbs storm surge instead of drowning families.
[P1.7] Third, wealthy countries are precisely the ones that can do retreat justly. Retreat goes wrong when it's unfunded and forced. Rich states can pay fair market value, relocate whole communities together, and plan decades ahead rather than evacuating after disaster.
