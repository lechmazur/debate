[C1.2] A blanket default collapses the huge variety of gig work into one legal mould, then punishes the very arrangements many workers choose for flexibility, low entry barriers, and the ability to combine income streams.
[C1.3] PRO says platforms exercise control. But coordination is not the same as employment. An app that matches supply and demand, standardizes prices, and protects users through ratings is doing what marketplaces have always done: reducing transaction costs.
