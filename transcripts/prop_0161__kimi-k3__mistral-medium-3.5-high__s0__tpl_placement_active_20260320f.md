A prohibition on creating such agents — even in the name of defense — is the only line that actually holds.

[P1.4] Second, the defensive benefits are substitutable. Meanwhile, creating the pathogen creates the blueprint: publishing sequences and methods hands a recipe to every state, and eventually every actor with a synthesis machine. You cannot un-publish a genome.
[P1.5] Third, "defensive" is unverifiable. Intent cannot be inspected. A global regime with a defensive carve-out is a regime every violator joins in name.
