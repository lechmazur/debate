[P1.1] Safety on dating apps is not a premium add-on: it is a non-negotiable baseline for a service that facilitates intimate human connection. When apps reserve identity verification, robust block tools, and scam response for paying users, they create a two-tiered system where the most vulnerable bear the highest risk of harm, and the entire platform’s trust erodes for everyone.
[P1.2] Consider older adults, who are disproportionately targeted by romance scams per FTC data. Many live on fixed incomes and cannot afford premium subscriptions. Right now, a scammer with a fake ID and a fabricated military backstory can match with a 70-year-old widow on a free tier, and the app will not offer her the ID check that would expose the fraud, or priority scam response that would freeze the scammer’s account before he drains her savings. The FTC recorded $1.3 billion in romance scam losses in 2022, the vast majority of which originate on dating platforms.
[P1.3] That loss is not a collateral cost of doing business—it is a direct result of apps prioritizing subscription revenue over user safety.
[P1.4] This two-tiered model also harms paying users. When scammers and harassers operate unimpeded on free tiers, they drive away honest users, depress subscription rates, and create a toxic environment that makes even paying users less safe. Paywalled block tools mean harassers can create new accounts to target victims after being blocked, a problem that disappears when block tools are free and unlimited. Universal ID verification reduces catfishing and fake profiles across the entire platform, improving match quality for everyone.
[P1.5] The cost objection is a red herring. Top dating apps generate billions in annual subscription revenue—Tinder alone brought in over $2 billion in 2023. The cost of rolling out these safety features to all users is a rounding error compared to that revenue, with a clear return on investment in higher user retention and less reputational damage from viral harm stories. There is no legitimate business case for paywalling safety, only a choice to prioritize profit over people.
