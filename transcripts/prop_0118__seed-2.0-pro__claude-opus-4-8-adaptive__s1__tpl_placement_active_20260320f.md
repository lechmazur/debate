[P1.1] Means tested school meals fail, first and foremost, because they humiliate children. A kid should never have to carry a different coloured tray, get pulled aside at the register, or watch their friends notice they qualified for support. That shame is not a minor side effect. It is baked into the design.
[P1.2] Large numbers of eligible children do not even apply for free meals, just to avoid that stigma. The targeted system fails the exact people it is supposed to help.
[P1.3] Second, targeted systems waste enormous money on administration. [P1.4] Strip all that overhead away, and most of the supposed extra cost of universal meals vanishes.
[P1.5] Finally, hunger does not check form deadlines. There is the quiet child whose father lost his job last week and has not reapplied yet. There is the middle class kid whose mother worked a double shift and forgot to pack lunch. No child learns well on an empty stomach.
[P1.6] This is basic school infrastructure, same as desks, same as drinking fountains. We do not make children prove their parents are poor before they can use the tap. We should not do it for lunch.
