[C1.1] The proposition imagines a neat, orderly corridor: people apply at embassies, receive a document, board a commercial flight. That picture conceals three problems that make this proposal counterproductive.
[C1.2] Consider the demand problem first. An application window open at every EU embassy in Africa, the Middle East, and Central Asia does not redirect existing migration flows—it expands them by orders of magnitude. Every person in any fragile or authoritarian state now has a plausible legal route to pursue. The pool of potential applicants is not the tens of thousands who reach EU shores annually; it encompasses hundreds of millions living under governments that fall short of liberal democratic standards.
[C1.3] The proposal gives no credible mechanism for setting limits without either overwhelming European processing capacity or making the visa so restrictive it helps almost no one. That dilemma goes unaddressed, and it is fatal.
[C1.4] Then there is the operational fiction. EU consular presence is thinnest precisely where persecution is worst. High-repression states—Eritrea, North Korea, large parts of Afghanistan and Syria at their worst—often have no accessible EU diplomatic footprint. Where embassies do exist, they operate under surveillance by the very regimes applicants are fleeing.
[C1.5] A Belarusian dissident applying at the EU mission in Minsk is not protected—they are exposed. The proposal offers no account of who conducts specialized asylum interviews at scale, how staff are trained, or how documents are verified when host governments have every incentive to corrupt the process.
[C1.6] Finally, security screening is not better abroad—it is worse. Security databases covering non-EU nationals are least reliable for the countries generating the highest volumes of applicants. Biometric matching fails when there are no prior records to check. A consular appointment cannot substitute for the extended review genuine refugee determination requires.
[C1.7] The PRO's "control by design" becomes a theoretical aspiration with no operational foundation.
[C1.8] This is not a lifesaving corridor—it is an unworkable promise that expands demand while providing no real mechanism to manage what follows.
