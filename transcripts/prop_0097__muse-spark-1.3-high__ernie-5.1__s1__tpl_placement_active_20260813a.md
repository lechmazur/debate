[P1.3] When a single tool lets three people do the work of five, the fifth job rarely disappears with a press release.
[P1.4] Labor markets do not give advance warning, they give pink slips. Companies experiment quietly, freeze entry-level hiring, squeeze more output from smaller teams, then cut suddenly when the tools mature. Skills atrophy, mortgages slip, and mid-career workers discover retraining takes years, not weeks.
[P1.5] That lag is why preparation must come first. Effective retraining cannot be built overnight; instructors, curricula, employer links, and childcare all take time. Basic income support keeps families afloat and demand alive while people adjust.
[P1.6] These are low-regret investments. Even if AI displaces more slowly than feared, workers still face churn, downgrading, and painful transitions.
