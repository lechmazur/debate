[P1.2] Consider the mechanism at work. When a government plans to liquidate a sovereign wealth fund for immediate spending, or authorizes infrastructure that locks in decades of emissions, or changes pension obligations in ways that shift costs forward forty years, the costs fall on people who cannot organize, lobby, or vote against them. [P1.3] The democratic process systematically underrepresents the interests of those who will inherit the consequences.

The interests of future citizens deserve analogous protection.
[P1.5] A commissioner with veto authority over a defined category of major long-term decisions is no more undemocratic than a supreme court striking down legislation or a central bank raising interest rates against the government's wishes.
