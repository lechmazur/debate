It is a predictable, preventable hazard that employers already have every incentive to ignore, and voluntary guidelines have failed completely for decades. [P1.2] That is an employer choosing to keep production running instead of pulling people into shade. [P1.4] That is not a free labor market. That is coercion. That is a failure of basic labor protection that only government mandate can fix.
