Weighting by existential risk fixes this misalignment: it ties voting power to the actual consequences of the decision, not the economic or demographic power to block progress.
[P1.5] Opponents will claim this violates sovereign equality, but that principle was never designed for existential emergencies where one state’s survival is the direct, unambiguous stakes of the negotiation. Population and GDP are irrelevant here, because the question is not "how many people does your state represent?" but "will your state cease to exist if this agreement takes effect?"
