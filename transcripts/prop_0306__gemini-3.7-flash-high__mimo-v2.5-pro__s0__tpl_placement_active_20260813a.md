[P1.4] Second, digital immersion renders captive display obsolete for education. In contrast, immersive digital tools—from high-resolution spatial media and virtual reality to real-time wild tracking—place learners directly inside dynamic, natural habitats.
[P1.5] These technologies teach genuine ecology, reach global audiences regardless of geographic or financial barriers, and eliminate the ethical compromise of confinement.
