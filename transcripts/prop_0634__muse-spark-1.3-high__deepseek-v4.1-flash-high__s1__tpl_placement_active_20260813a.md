[P1.1] Heritable editing does not treat a patient. It manufactures one. Somatic therapy gives a drug to a consenting person who can be monitored, and treatment can stop if harm appears. Germline editing for reproduction writes an experimental change into an embryo, then into every cell of the resulting child, then into their children forever.
[P1.2] That difference alone justifies a ban.
[P1.3] Safety is not a detail better tools will fix. Genes do many jobs, interact with environment, and effects unfold over a lifetime. An off-target cut or mosaic embryo where some cells carry the edit and others do not cannot be recalled once a child is born. A failed somatic trial harms a volunteer.
[P1.4] A failed germline birth seeds the error into the gene pool, compounding with each generation. No regulator can follow infinite descendants.
[P1.5] There is also no medical need that outweighs that risk. Couples fearing severe genetic disease already have options that do not rewrite inheritance: screening embryos, donor gametes, adoption. Cases where both parents carry the same homozygous disorder and no healthy unedited embryo is possible are vanishingly rare. We should not build a commercial industry for designer embryos around an edge case.
[P1.6] That industry is the deeper danger. Once clinics may edit for reproduction, the line between preventing disease and choosing traits will not hold. Height, cognition, appearance — wealthy parents will pay, clinics will compete, others will feel forced to follow. What begins as compassion becomes a market in children and a new biology of inequality, with women especially pressured to provide eggs for experimentation.
[P1.7] Country bans are how we stop that spiral. One rogue lab can cause scandal, but clear national prohibition deters hospitals, funders and insurers and denies legitimacy to fertility tourism. Permit reproductive editing as medicine somewhere and it becomes an arms race everywhere.

[C1.7] The choice is not between perfect safety and designer babies. It is between cautious, regulated medicine and a symbolic ban that punishes the rare and empowers the reckless. Vote CON.
