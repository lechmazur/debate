[P1.3] Treat nightlife like any other high-volume public event. Late transit and visible safety support turn a spike into a flow. [P1.4] Kitchen staff and bartenders get home. Crowds leave over two hours instead of twenty minutes. The peak that overloads police and emergency rooms is the simultaneous exit, not the extra hour of music.
[P1.5] Clock and license restrictions punish the legal rooms that already have cameras, door staff, and a paper trail. [P1.6] It is a function of whether people can leave without being stranded.
