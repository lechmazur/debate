[P1.4] Second, the proposition preserves everything legitimate about employer interests. [P1.6] Third, unlimited surveillance is bad management dressed as rigor. Keystroke counts reward performative typing, not thinking; workers learn to jiggle mice rather than solve problems. [P1.7] Consent-based, limited monitoring filters out precisely the systems that destroy trust while measuring nothing.
