[C1.3] The distributional consequences are ugly. A labor pathway from a drought-hit region may admit engineers and nurses, stripping the places that need them most. [C1.7] Settle minimum standards first: triggers, non-refoulement, review, burden-sharing. Then expand. Acting first and defining later is not compassion; it is protection by lottery.
