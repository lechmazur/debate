[P1.1] Criminal punishment should do more than give wrongdoing an unpleasant consequence. Its most important aim should be to reduce the likelihood that the person punished harms someone again. [P1.5] They should shape those restrictions, because the public needs safer conduct afterward—not merely a completed sentence.
