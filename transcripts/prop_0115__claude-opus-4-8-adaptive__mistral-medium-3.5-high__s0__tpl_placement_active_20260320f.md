[P1.6] Third, fair penalties and reporting. Late fees and the consequences for your credit record should follow consistent rules, not a patchwork that lets a "feature" punish you harder than a "card" legally could.
[P1.7] The opposition will say BNPL is often interest-free and good for access. Fine—then light-touch rules cost responsible providers nothing, because the protections only bite where harm exists. Interest-free does not mean consequence-free.
[P1.8] The question is not whether BNPL is convenient. It is whether convenience should buy an exemption from the rules that protect borrowers. It should not. Treat the loan like a loan.
