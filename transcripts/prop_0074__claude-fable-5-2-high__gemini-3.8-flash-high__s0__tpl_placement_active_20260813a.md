[C1.2] First, modern endowments are not liquid pools of ancestral loot. Compelling universities to reallocate these funds toward targeted reparations or municipal development breaches fiduciary duties and starves their primary public mission: educating current and future generations of students across all backgrounds.
[C1.3] Second, privatizing systemic justice creates an arbitrary lottery. Universities cannot act as surrogate states, and pretending they can only lets the sovereign public off the hook for systemic remedies.
[C1.4] Finally, historical acknowledgment is not a decorative prelude to a cash settlement; it is the primary, indispensable duty of an academic institution. Reducing moral reckoning to a financial transaction treats justice like an indulgence—a payout that allows an institution to declare its moral balance sheet settled. Real accountability lies in permanent, honest education, not in converting universities into privatized reparations boards.
