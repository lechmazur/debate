[C1.3] The harms that remain are concentrated poverty, weak schools, housing segregation, unequal credit, and biased policing. [C1.5] A direct program would require a bureaucracy to certify ancestry, rank suffering, and decide who counts—inviting fraud, backlash, and divisions that weaken the political coalition needed for broad redistribution.
