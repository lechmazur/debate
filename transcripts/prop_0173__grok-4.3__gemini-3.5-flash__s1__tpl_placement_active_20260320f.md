Once built, it captures 70–80 percent of the air market on comparable routes, delivering emission cuts and time savings at a marginal cost per avoided ton of CO₂ well below the price of waiting for viable electric planes.
[P1.5] The decisive comparison is therefore not whether both options can reduce emissions, but which one actually removes flights and shortens trips at scale within a decade. High-speed rail demonstrably does.
