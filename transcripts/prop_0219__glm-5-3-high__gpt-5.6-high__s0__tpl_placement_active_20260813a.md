[P1.3] Range disclosure extends that logic: it gives every candidate the same anchor, before any negotiation happens, before any self-selection filters anyone out. [P1.6] And the counterfactual matters. Matching today already operates through opaque bands, recruiter games, and rejected offers after weeks of interviews. The system was never frictionless.
