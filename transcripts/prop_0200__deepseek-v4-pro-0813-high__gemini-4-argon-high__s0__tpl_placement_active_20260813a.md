[P1.1] Lunar resource extraction will not wait for a treaty. [P1.2] Legal recognition is what makes enforceable conditions possible. These duties can be implemented through domestic authorization and bilateral coordination now, without waiting for universal consensus.
[P1.4] A full treaty would be valuable, but making legality hostage to the slowest global negotiation is self-defeating. The proposition does not block a future treaty; it creates a governed baseline before the treaty arrives. That baseline is better than the alternative: uncertain legality, opaque state-backed activity, and no binding transparency or debris obligations at all.
[P1.5] Recognize the activity conditionally, attach the duties, and the first generation of lunar operators has an incentive to act responsibly.
