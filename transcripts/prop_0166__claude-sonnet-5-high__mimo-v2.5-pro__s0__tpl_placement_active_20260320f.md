Ban de-extinction and you don't protect that pipeline — you starve it.
[P1.4] Third, there's an ecological payoff CON can't dismiss as sentimental. That's not competing with endangered species protection; it's climate mitigation with conservation as a side effect.
[P1.5] Finally, humans drove these species extinct through direct action — overhunting, habitat destruction. If restoration is possible, we have an obligation to attempt it, and government legitimacy and oversight is exactly what prevents this from becoming reckless private bioengineering with no ecological safeguards.
