[P1.5] Furthermore, this proposition offers the strongest possible protection for civilians. When a nation’s critical infrastructure is targeted, the daily lives and safety of millions are held hostage. Recognizing these acts as armed attacks forces the international community to treat the digital targeting of civilians with the same gravity as a physical invasion. It validates the lived reality of the victims and ensures that the defense of a society is not limited to the domain of bits and bytes.
[P1.6] In the 21st century, the shield of international law must cover digital front lines as robustly as physical borders. Only by acknowledging the equivalence of these threats can we maintain the integrity of the laws of war and protect the fundamental security of nations.
