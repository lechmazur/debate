[P1.3] Expensive sports that fill roster slots further skew the pool. [P1.4] Professional pathways compound the advantage. Elite firms recruit preferentially from the same schools and through informal alumni and family networks. [P1.5] The talented outsider can break through, but the cumulative drag is real while the privileged experience assistance as invisible wind at their backs.

The result is not pure aristocracy, but a system whose elite reproduction exceeds what a genuine merit filter would produce—and far more than those who cleared it usually concede.
