[P1.1] The motion is not a demand to abolish every rating. It is a demand to stop a dossier that follows you. The systems at issue take conduct from one setting—a gig shift, a lease dispute, a school misconduct flag, a social-media flare-up—and score it in a portable form that reappears in later decisions about jobs, platforms, schools, and housing. Calling this efficiency or trust does not answer the harm: a portable score is not consent that anyone meaningfully gave.
[P1.2] That portability, not feedback itself, is what needs a legal off-switch.
[P1.3] Picture a delivery worker whose account is automatically restricted after an identity-check glitch. Because the same vendor sells reliability and fraud signals across platforms, she soon cannot drive, deliver, or rent through platforms that rely on the same score. No human explains the rule; no appeal restores access; and the "reputation" was never hers to contest. This is not rare malfunction around the edges; it is the basic structure of reputation systems that treat past data as permanent destiny.
[P1.4] Such systems collapse contexts. A person may be difficult online yet reliable with money, or had one bad month years ago but stable now. Housing tools routinely ingest non-housing signals, so a payment long resolved can still veto a lease. A single score erases that difference and hardens inequality.
[P1.5] Because participation in work, education, and housing is not optional, people cannot meaningfully consent; the terms of service are not democratic authorization. Efficiency is not enough to justify a judgment that follows you everywhere, cannot be inspected, and offers no redemption.
[P1.6] A legal right to disconnect does not require forgetting the past. It keeps sector-specific, contestable evidence—pay stubs, transcripts, references, a verified rental ledger—available, while barring the inherited, cross-domain algorithmic score from automatically deciding your future. That is the minimal condition for second chances, due process, and equal access to the essentials of life.
