[P1.1] Home care should be the default for elder care whenever it is genuinely possible, and the reasons are not sentimental — they are medical, economic, and moral.
[P1.2] Start with what older people themselves want. Survey after survey across wealthy countries shows large majorities of older adults saying they want to age in their own homes and communities. In fact, the risks run the other way.
[P1.3] Institutional living concentrates frail people together, which is why nursing homes became the deadliest sites of the pandemic in country after country.
