[P1.3] Treating cohabitation as suspicious gets the causation backwards. [P1.4] Marriage, which is harder to exit than either, deserves at least the same realism.

[P1.7] So the standard I'd ask the judges to hold: does cohabitation reflect people taking their relationship more seriously, or less?
