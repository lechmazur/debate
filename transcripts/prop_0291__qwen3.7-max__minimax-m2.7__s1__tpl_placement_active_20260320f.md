[P1.6] We are not advocating for draconian surveillance, but for structural guardrails. Giving parents un-bypassable OS-level tools ensures that when a guardian sets a boundary to protect their child's sleep, focus, or psychological well-being, the tech industry cannot quietly hand the teenager a digital crowbar to break it.
