And when the market fails to fund digitization, the loss is irreversible—paper decays, custodial institutions close, and links to names and kinship are permanently severed.
[P1.5] The question is ultimately whether we accept a model where the wealthy can afford their ancestors and the rest navigate silence, or we treat access to history as a public right. We choose the latter.
