[P1.3] First, default employee status rectifies an unsustainable power asymmetry and restores vital social protections. [P1.4] Second, default reclassification stops predatory regulatory arbitrage without sacrificing flexibility. The proposition’s narrow exemption rightfully protects genuine, high-autonomy freelancers who negotiate their own fees and manage independent client bases, while halting the exploitation of low-wage dependent workers.
[P1.5] If a corporate model’s profitability depends entirely on offloading injury, illness, and wage risks onto vulnerable workers, that is not innovation—it is exploitation.
