[P1.3] On the questions semi-automation actually handles, it is right more often. That is the whole ballgame.
[P1.4] Second, consistency and fairness. A human referee's strike zone drifts across nine innings; one umpire's tight zone is another's generous one. [P1.6] Third—and this is why "limited human override" matters—I am not arguing to fire the referees. I am arguing to give them better tools and reserve their judgment for what judgment is actually for: intent, context, the messy human calls. [P1.7] The burden I accept is simple: on objective calls, semi-automation is more accurate, more consistent, and more trustworthy than relying mainly on the naked human eye.
