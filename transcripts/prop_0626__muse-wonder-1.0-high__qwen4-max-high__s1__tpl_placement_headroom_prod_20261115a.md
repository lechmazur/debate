[P1.1] States should ban weapons that decide, on their own, who lives and who dies. Once a system can select and engage targets without meaningful human control, the decision to kill is no longer a human decision — and that breaks the three conditions that make lethal force legitimate: judgment, restraint, and accountability.
[P1.2] First, autonomous weapons cannot reliably perform the judgments the laws of war require. Distinguishing a combatant from a civilian surrendering, wounded, holding a child, or coerced into fighting is not a pattern-matching exercise. It demands interpreting intent, context, and proportionality in situations where sensors are degraded, adversaries deceive, and training data never matches the next battlefield. A human commander can pause, doubt, and abort.
[P1.3] A fully autonomous system optimized to engage will act on classifications at machine speed, turning ambiguity into irreversible killing.
[P1.4] Second, autonomy creates an accountability vacuum. If a drone swarm strikes a school bus it misclassifies, who is responsible? The commander who deployed a system whose target choices were unforeseeable, the programmer who wrote code, or the manufacturer who sold it? When no person meaningfully chose that target, punishment becomes scapegoating and compensation becomes an apology without agency.
[P1.5] That gap invites states to launder killings through machines and erodes deterrence against war crimes.
[P1.6] Third, these weapons lower the threshold for war and accelerate escalation. Leaders can risk forces they do not have to bring home in coffins, adversaries must respond faster and automate in turn, and autonomous systems proliferate to militias and terrorists far more easily than crewed platforms. Competition shifts from human deliberation to algorithmic speed, where a sensor error or spoofed signal can trigger exchanges no leader intended.
[P1.7] Banning deployment draws a clear, verifiable line before these systems normalize. Keep humans meaningfully in control of lethal selection, or do not field the weapon.
