[C1.5] Sanitation and noise are legitimate concerns, but they are already handled through street-cleaning contracts, noise ordinances, and license reviews. [C1.6] The motion says "some venues," but its "only when" is universal for those venues and covers all three plans. It cannot distinguish a quiet cocktail bar from a stadium-sized club; it either overburdens the first or writes hollow paperwork for the second.
[C1.7] Cities should not trade later hours for a promise to solve municipal infrastructure. They should permit later hours where safety and nuisance standards are met, then use targeted, evidence-based conditions and city-led services. A nightlife policy should be a dial, not a lock.
