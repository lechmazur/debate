[P1.1] Influencer marketing makes surgery look like skincare. [P1.2] That matters because minors are not small adults. Adolescence is peak social comparison, identity instability, and susceptibility to parasocial persuasion. [P1.3] These are medical interventions with infection, scarring, nerve damage, filler complications, anesthesia risk, and psychological regret. In a clinic you get informed consent. [P1.5] Age-gating when an audience is likely to include minors is proportionate and feasible. We already gate alcohol, lottery scratchcards, and explicit content on the same platforms. [P1.6] Opposing this means defending a system where medical profit can exploit developmental vulnerability without disclosure. Warnings and gating do not ban speech for adults; they keep medical marketing honest where children are actually watching. Choice means informed adult choice.
