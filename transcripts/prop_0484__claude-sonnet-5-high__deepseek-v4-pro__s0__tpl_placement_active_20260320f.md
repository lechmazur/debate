[P1.1] Climate negotiations currently allocate influence through population and GDP - the same metrics that determine power everywhere else in the international system. That is precisely the problem. Tuvalu, Kiribati, the Marshall Islands: these states face not economic disruption but literal territorial erasure. Sea level rise does not care that Tuvalu has eleven thousand people and no GDP to speak of.
[P1.2] Yet in the room where decisions get made, that same smallness renders them nearly voiceless, while the states most responsible for emissions - the ones with the population and GDP to dominate voting weight - face inconvenience, not annihilation.
[P1.3] This is a mismatch between who bears the stakes and who holds the power, and it is the central design flaw of the current system. Weighting votes to population or GDP rewards exactly the actors whose economic model created the crisis, while muting the actors for whom the crisis is not abstract but civilizational.
[P1.4] Proportionality demands that voice track consequence. We already accept this principle elsewhere: a patient facing a fatal diagnosis gets more say over their own treatment than a distant relative with no skin in the game, regardless of who's richer or more numerous. Existential risk weighting simply imports that logic into climate governance. A state that will physically cease to exist if warming crosses 1.5 degrees has a categorically different stake than a state that will merely see reduced coastline or economic cost.
[P1.5] Treating those stakes as equivalent - or worse, letting the low-stakes actor outvote the high-stakes one - is not neutral procedure, it's a substantive judgment that survival matters less than GDP.
[P1.6] Weighted voting also corrects a specific failure mode we've already seen: watered-down targets, non-binding pledges, financing promises that go unmet, all pushed through by coalitions of states who will still have functioning economies at three degrees of warming. Give existential-risk states real voting weight, and the negotiated outcome must actually satisfy the people it will kill or spare. That's not sentimentality. That's the whole point of giving anyone a vote at all.
