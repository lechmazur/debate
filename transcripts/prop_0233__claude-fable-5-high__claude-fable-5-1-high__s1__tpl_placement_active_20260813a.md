[P1.1] A sixteen-year-old carrying a knife tonight in London, Stockholm, or Brussels makes a simple calculation: the chance anyone will ever check him is close to zero, and the cost of being caught unarmed by rivals feels far higher. That calculation is why knife-carrying has spread among European teenagers, and it is the calculation preventive policing powers exist to reverse.
[P1.2] The case rests on three pillars.
[P1.3] First, mechanism. Knife crime is uniquely suited to prevention because the weapon must be carried before it can be used. Unlike most crimes, there is a visible, interceptable stage between intent and injury. Wider stop-and-search raises the perceived probability of detection, and deterrence research consistently shows that certainty of being caught matters far more than severity of punishment.
[P1.4] Every knife left at home because a search seemed likely is a stabbing that may never happen. Sweden's escalating gang shootings and Britain's teenage knife deaths show what happens when that interception layer is too thin.
[P1.5] Second, who pays for inaction. The victims of knife and gang violence are overwhelmingly young men from poor and minority neighbourhoods — the very communities critics claim to protect by restricting police powers. Refusing to search anyone in the name of equality means, in practice, accepting that those communities absorb the funerals. Protection delayed is protection denied.
[P1.6] Third, expansion done right is safer than the status quo. The motion asks for wider powers, not lawless ones. Powers granted explicitly — with body-worn cameras, recorded grounds, supervisory review, and public data on outcomes — are more accountable than officers improvising around narrow rules, or than the alternative that quietly fills the vacuum: gangs providing their own brutal "security."
[P1.7] The opposition will invoke trust and civil liberties. But nothing corrodes trust in the state faster than mothers burying sons while police stand legally handcuffed. A liberal society that cannot keep knives off its streets will not stay liberal for long.
