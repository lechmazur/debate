[C1.3] Take away that subsidy and you do not automatically redirect the money to the under-served child—you risk losing the infrastructure and the income that supports them.
[C1.4] The PRO side assumes elite subsidization is a gift to the already-privileged. [C1.6] Finally, aspiration matters. When a community invests in its best young athletes, it tells every child in that town that excellence is worth reaching for. Recreational access is the floor. Competitive investment raises the ceiling, and a higher ceiling lifts the floor for everyone.
