The harm is concentrated, and it is not neatly short-term: eviction forces children to switch schools, workers to lose jobs, families to leave support networks, and often pushes people from transit-rich neighborhoods to car-dependent peripheries where their costs rise. “Right to return” rarely returns most of them.

[C1.6] The proposition asks us to knowingly impose avoidable harm. Municipalities exist to protect the people who live in them now. We should reject.
