[P1.4] Those ask the target to survive the stampede and then clean up. They do not slow a system built to outrun moderation. [P1.5] Doing more does not mean censoring opinion. It means targeted friction on the pile-on, not the speech. Platforms can slow the mass distribution of high-velocity quote-post clusters and context-stripped clip farms, cap recommended amplification when a post is being swarmed, require lightweight context or source links before a clip is monetized or recommended, rate-limit dogpile notifications, and give smaller creators a default shield mode. The objection is preserved; only the rocket fuel is removed.
[P1.6] The clash is therefore simple: we defend cooling the engineered accelerant while expression continues; the other side must defend keeping the current accelerant and calling the resulting mob merely “engagement.” If platforms can cut even part of that foreseeable harm with small, ordinary frictions and still refuse to act, the motion stands.
