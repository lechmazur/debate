[P1.3] Three arguments carry this debate.
[P1.4] First, the harm is severe, targeted, and growing. Sexually explicit deepfakes overwhelmingly target women and girls; they destroy reputations, careers, and mental health, and the tools to make them are now free and instant. [P1.5] Second, existing remedies structurally fail. Criminal statutes depend on prosecutorial discretion, and these cases are routinely deprioritized. Fabricated sexual imagery of an identifiable, non-consenting person, passed off as their body. [P1.8] A well-drafted statute requires realistic depiction of an identifiable person without consent, with carve-outs for clearly labeled satire and matters of legitimate public concern.
