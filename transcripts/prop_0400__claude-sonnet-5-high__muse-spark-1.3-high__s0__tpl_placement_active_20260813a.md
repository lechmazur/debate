[P4.3] A2: No, sixty seconds does not satisfy the right, and CON is right that we need a test — but the test is straightforward and already exists in administrative law: a reviewer must engage the specific evidence the claimant submitted and produce reasons tied to that evidence, reviewable by a court for being boilerplate or non-responsive. A rubber-stamp affirmance that ignores submitted rebuttal evidence fails that test and the decision gets vacated. That's enforceable, and it's exactly the mechanism CON's own "stated reasons" proposal needs — except CON's version has no one with the power to actually change the outcome when reasons fail.
[P4.4] Someone must re-decide. That's the human.
