Technocracy produces outputs; democracy produces authorizations. Only authorized rule survives contact with the governed.
[P1.2] Start with the failure mode. [P1.5] Once you weigh incommensurable values, you are doing politics, not engineering. The question is who is entitled to make those trade-offs, and the only non-arbitrary answer is the people who live with the consequences.
