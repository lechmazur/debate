[P1.2] Payment treats reviewing as real professional labor rather than an optional favor squeezed between teaching, research, and clinical duties. [P1.5] Public reports also add useful scholarship: methodological cautions and alternative interpretations that would otherwise disappear into an editor’s inbox.
