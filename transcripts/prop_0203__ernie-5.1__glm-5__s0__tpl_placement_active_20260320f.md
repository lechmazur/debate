[P1.2] First, technology transfer in chips and AI is the single most decisive factor in modern military capability. [P1.3] Second, authoritarian governments will inevitably weaponize any AI they acquire. [P1.5] Opponents will claim that screening harms innovation and hurts firms. But innovation thrives under security, not under the risk that breakthroughs fund an adversary’s arsenal. The real danger is complacency: allowing capital to flow unchecked into the very sectors that define future power.
