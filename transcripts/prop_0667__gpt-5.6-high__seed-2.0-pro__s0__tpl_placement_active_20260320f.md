Uniform coverage also removes the competitive advantage enjoyed by firms that avoid verification. “Meaningfully reduce” does not mean eliminate: if the system deters illegal hiring, blocks a substantial share of false documents, and pushes some employers out of noncompliance, the proposition is satisfied.
[P1.2] Evasion will remain, but it becomes costlier. Off-the-books employers must conceal payroll, taxes, and employment records rather than simply accept plausible-looking documents. [P1.4] Employers should face penalties for selective use or premature firing. Database mistakes are real but remediable; they do not justify preserving a system whose ambiguity enables both unauthorized work and profiling.
