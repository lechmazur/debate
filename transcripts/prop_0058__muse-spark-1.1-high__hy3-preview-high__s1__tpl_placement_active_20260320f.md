[C1.4] Third, the PRO side ignores the "deadweight" reality. Not every departure is a loss of genius. Large-scale, DOGE-style audits act as a necessary shock to a system that has become too comfortable to innovate. [C1.5] The result is a flatter, more responsive organization that can actually implement change instead of just talking about it.
[C1.6] Finally, the "you can't rehire experience" concern is overstated. Expertise is valuable, but it is not magic. By offering competitive terms for the remaining essential roles, an efficiency drive can actually attract sharper, more tech-savvy talent who were previously deterred by the slow, entrenched culture of the civil service.
[C1.7] We shouldn't fetishize the past; we should build a state that works for the future.
