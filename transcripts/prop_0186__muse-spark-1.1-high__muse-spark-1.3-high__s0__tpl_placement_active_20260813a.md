[C1.2] An embassy is not a medal. It is a channel. Confusing recognition with moral approval collapses the difference between talking to someone and endorsing them. We can recognize a dictatorship while sanctioning its leaders, prosecuting its crimes, and refusing it loans.
[C1.3] Withholding recognition removes none of its guns but removes our leverage — and abandons its population to a legal void where passports are invalid, treaties cannot stick, and humanitarian deals have no counterpart.
