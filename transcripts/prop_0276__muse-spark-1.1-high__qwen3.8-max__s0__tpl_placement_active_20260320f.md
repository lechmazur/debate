[C1.1] The fastest way to lose this debate is to treat “automatic” as a synonym for “fair.” The proposition promises effortless justice, but it builds a payout machine on the one thing air travel cannot reliably provide: a clean, instant judgment of what caused a delay.
[C1.2] Airline operations are networks, not vending machines. A missed connection in Chicago may trace to a late inbound plane, but that plane may have been delayed by weather two legs earlier, a crew timing out because of a diversion, or an air-traffic slot lost during a ground stop. Maintenance can be routine and foreseeable, or it can be a bird strike discovered at the gate. European-style rules are often cited, but they have not eliminated disputes; they have shifted them into cause-code fights and higher fares.
[C1.3] Knowing who was on board is not the same as knowing who is legally responsible. If the law demands automatic compensation whenever a delay is labeled controllable, airlines will code defensively, cancel marginal flights earlier, pad schedules, and move complexity into classifications passengers cannot see.
[C1.4] Automatic cash payouts are also a blunt and regressive remedy. They do not compensate actual loss; they pay a fixed amount to whoever fits the trigger. A well-off frequent flyer delayed thirty minutes in an airport lounge and a single parent stranded overnight with children may receive the same arbitrary sum, while true costs remain unmeasured. The bill does not vanish.
[C1.5] It is spread through higher fares, thinner routes, and fewer low-cost options, hurting travelers least able to absorb them.
[C1.6] The real target should be complaint-heavy indifference, not automatic compensation. The problem is not adjudication itself; it is airlines making passengers beg for what is already owed. Require fast refunds, rebooking, meals, hotels, and clear disclosures. Penalize chronic controllable failures.
[C1.7] Preserve a simple claims track for disputed cases. Airlines should owe care, correction, and enforceable minimums, not a mandatory algorithmic bounty.
