[P2.1] "Fix the gate" sounds pragmatic, but it ignores who's holding the wrench. The gatekeeper's revenue depends on the gate staying open. Every golden-visa program in Europe has been "reformed" at least once—tightened rules, new due-diligence contractors, published guidelines—and the scandals kept coming, because a program whose business model is speed and discretion will always lobby, erode, or outsource its way back to that model. Reform isn't the opposite of the disease; it's the disease's remission stage.
[P2.2] The "visibility" argument inverts reality. An applicant who wants to hide doesn't volunteer a money trail; they hire intermediaries to construct one. That's precisely what the due-diligence firms were caught missing. And the comparison fails: capital that can't buy a Schengen passport isn't the same capital that flees to Dubai.
[P2.3] What these buyers want—free movement, EU legal cover, a respectable flag—exists nowhere else. Abolish the product and its value evaporates. Dubai gets the money that was always dirty; Europe stops manufacturing respectability for it.
[P2.4] Portugal actually proves my case, not my opponent's. The property route survived a decade of documented damage before it was cut, and only under sustained political pressure. That's not reform working; that's reform arriving a housing crisis late. Meanwhile the "productive" fund routes remain, with their own transparency problems.
[P2.5] The honesty argument is the weakest of all. Skilled-migration routes select for what a person can contribute; investor routes select for what a person already has. Calling that "the same trade" flattens the distinction between earning membership and purchasing it—and it's exactly that distinction Europeans across the political spectrum keep rejecting.
[P2.6] Finally, "unilateral exit" misses the direction of travel. Portugal, Ireland, the Netherlands, and the Netherlands again—states are leaving, not joining. The race is to the exits. The only question is whether Europe ends this trade cleanly or lets each scandal write the ending.
