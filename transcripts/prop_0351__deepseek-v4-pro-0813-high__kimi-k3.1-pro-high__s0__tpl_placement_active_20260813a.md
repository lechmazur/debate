[P1.1] Early-career growth is apprenticeship, not information transfer. A junior learns judgment by overhearing a senior manager handle a hard client call, watching which slide gets cut in a final meeting, absorbing how a colleague pushes back without burning the relationship. Working from home converts those small learning moments into scheduled, sanitized exchanges, so juniors complete tasks while falling behind in the tacit skills promotion requires.
[P1.2] That is the central illusion. Employers measure tickets closed, messages answered, tasks delivered. Remote juniors often hit those marks, so employers conclude remote is working. But missing development appears later, when the junior cannot run a meeting, anticipate a client’s real objection, make a judgment call without a script, or lacks a sponsor pulling them into a stretch role.
[P1.3] By then the cost has compounded.
[P1.4] Feedback thins. In person, a senior catches a spreadsheet error or corrects a framing mistake in a hallway conversation before it becomes habit. Remotely, feedback lands later in one-to-ones or written comments, usually narrowed to the deliverable. Juniors also lose calibration: watching senior styles teaches what too slow, too aggressive, and good enough look like.
[P1.5] That cannot be downloaded.
[P1.6] Visibility drives promotion, and remote juniors lose it silently. Promotions are won in unplanned moments: the junior who stays steady in a crisis, asks a sharp question after a meeting, or gets pulled into the problem because they were there. Remote juniors must manufacture evidence of potential while office juniors leak it constantly. Over five years the gap in sponsorship, cross-team trust, and stretch assignments is enormous.
[P1.7] The harm is understated because both sides confuse current productivity with development. Workers like the flexibility and feel busy; employers see stable output. But a junior productive today and under-skilled tomorrow is the hidden cost. Remote work in early years is not neutral; it removes the apprenticeship engine, and the bill arrives after the promotion window closes.
