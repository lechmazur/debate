[P1.1] The right to disconnect is not a ban on urgency. It is a default rule: after agreed hours, knowledge workers are not required to monitor or answer work messages, with narrow exceptions for genuine emergencies and scheduled on-call. That default is the central issue, because it changes who bears the cost of coordination.
[P1.2] The welfare gain is direct and widespread. A late-night message does not cost five minutes; it reactivates the task, delays sleep, and keeps the worker on standby. Over months, that chronic availability erodes recovery, autonomy, family time, and mental health. Knowledge workers cannot “leave work at work” when work follows them home in their pocket.
[P1.3] A legal default restores a boundary that individual workers cannot enforce alone: if one person answers, everyone else looks less committed, and the norm ratchets upward. The law solves that collective-action problem.
[P1.4] The productivity cost is real but limited and transitional. Knowledge output is not hours online; it is judgment, concentration, and error avoidance. Fatigued workers produce more mistakes, worse decisions, and slower recovery the next day. Protected off-hours improve next-morning focus and reduce burnout-driven turnover, which is expensive in exactly these industries.
[P1.5] Firms adapt with asynchronous handoffs, clearer priorities, and on-call rotations. A software team does not need every engineer watching a channel at midnight; it needs one scheduled responder. If something is truly urgent, the exception applies.
[P1.6] Weigh that comparison. The welfare benefit is immediate, diffuse, and compounding. The productivity damage is concentrated in coordination inconvenience and can be managed by design. The proposition does not require zero cost; it requires welfare gains to exceed productivity damage.
[P1.7] Opponents must show a large, persistent output loss, not merely that managers lose instant access. Most after-hours messages are not emergencies, and most response delays do not damage output. The right to disconnect protects the recovery that makes good knowledge work possible.
