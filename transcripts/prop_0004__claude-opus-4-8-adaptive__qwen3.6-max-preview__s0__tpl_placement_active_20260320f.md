[P1.2] We are organizing the modern workforce around an obsolete clock, and the proposition simply updates the clock.
[P1.3] My case rests on three pillars.
[P1.4] First, the productivity offset is real, not wishful. Output holds or rises while hours fall. That's why pay needn't drop: the firm isn't buying fewer units of value, it's buying the same value more efficiently.
[P1.5] Second, why the law, and not just voluntary adoption? Because a coordination problem traps us. [P1.6] The "standard" workweek is already a legal construct; we are choosing which standard.
[P1.7] Third, the human and social dividend.
