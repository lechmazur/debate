[C1.3] The question is not ban versus price, but which mix serves a city best. Forcing one blunt instrument is policy theater, not planning.
[C1.4] The second problem is who an outright ban actually punishes. Consider a disabled driver who cannot use transit or cycle. A parent dropping a sick child at a central clinic. A tradesperson with tools.

[C1.7] You are removing access. Shoppers reroute to suburban malls. Restaurants lose footfall. Property values fall, and the people who suffer most are the workers and small businesses who depend on that footfall.
