[C1.3] The "demonstrated improvements" guardrail does not solve this. [C1.5] Nor is enrollment funding merely paying for seats. It pays for capacity, access, research, and public service. [C1.6] The burden is therefore PRO's. They must defend a lagging, gameable, demographically contaminated private-earnings test as the arbiter of public higher education.
