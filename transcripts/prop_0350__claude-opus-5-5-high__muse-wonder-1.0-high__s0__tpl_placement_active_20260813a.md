[C1.2] Priority should therefore go to serious behavior policy — not the tired poster PRO attacks, but funded, repeated support: counselling in primary care, structured weight-management programmes, cooking and budgeting skills in schools, workplace and digital coaching, and help with self-monitoring, sleep, and activity. [C1.3] Environmental restrictions promise effortless wins, but they are blunt and easily outrun. Tax one drink and consumers switch to juice, milkshakes, alcohol, or larger discounted packs.
