[P1.1] English is not a neutral bridge between equals; it is a gate that keeps most of humanity on the outside. When scientific publishing, top-tier business education, and the core infrastructure of the internet operate overwhelmingly in one language, they do not simply connect the world—they concentrate the power to define what counts as knowledge, who counts as an expert, and which problems get solved first. The harm is structural, not incidental.
[P1.2] In science, English dominance creates a double extraction. Researchers from non-anglophone countries must pay the cognitive and financial tax of writing in a second language, then often face higher rejection rates and lower citation visibility even when their work is sound. Meanwhile, entire knowledge systems—oral traditions, indigenous ecological classifications, locally grounded medical practices—remain invisible to the global literature because they exist outside English-language journals. When a community’s insights must be translated into English to be taken seriously, the gatekeepers are anglophone institutions, and the research agenda is set by their priorities.
[P1.3] The result is not a universal science but a provincial science wearing a cosmopolitan mask.
[P1.4] The same logic governs business and the internet. Global platforms are built around English-first interfaces, English search algorithms, and English content moderation rules, which systematically disadvantage speakers of other languages in visibility, commerce, and even safety. A small business in Lagos or Lima competes on terrain tilted toward native English speakers, while the value created in local languages is scraped, translated, and monetized by anglophone corporations without reciprocal benefit.
[P1.5] Yes, English enables some cross-border collaboration. But the dominance privileges a minority of native speakers, accelerates the erosion of linguistic diversity, and subordinates non-anglophone epistemologies to a single cultural frame. A world where knowledge flows only through one linguistic filter is poorer, not richer, and the harm is built into the architecture.
