[P1.1] A nuclear-armed Iran would make every regional war more likely, more dangerous, and less controllable. [P1.5] Finally, the risk of regional war was real but bounded, and it must be compared with inaction. The operation used no ground troops and was not designed to collapse the Iranian state.
