[P1.1] Aviation emissions are overwhelmingly driven by a hyper-mobile elite, yet our current approach to taxing air travel punishes the working class. Shifting to a progressive frequent-flyer levy—where the tax rate rises with each additional flight a person takes annually—is the only economically efficient and socially just way to curb aviation emissions.
[P1.2] First, a progressive levy directly targets the true source of the problem. A tiny fraction of the global population takes the vast majority of flights. Flat ticket taxes or fuel duties fail as environmental policies because they do not deter high-income frequent flyers, who easily absorb a flat fee. A progressive levy solves this by escalating the financial friction.
[P1.3] Your first flight of the year—perhaps to visit family or take an annual holiday—remains lightly taxed. But your fifth, tenth, or twentieth flight faces an exponentially higher premium. This structure suppresses high-carbon discretionary travel—like weekend luxury getaways or constant business trips that could easily be video calls—without outright banning travel.
[P1.4] Second, the current flat-tax model is inherently regressive. Under the status quo, a low-income worker flying home to see a sick relative pays the exact same carbon penalty rate as an executive flying to a vacation home for the fourth time this year. A frequent-flyer levy protects essential and occasional travel while shifting the burden of decarbonization onto those who actually generate the bulk of the emissions. It treats a baseline level of mobility as a normal good, but taxes excessive aviation as luxury consumption.
[P1.5] Finally, this progressive structure generates substantial revenue from those with the greatest ability to pay. These funds can be aggressively reinvested into sustainable aviation fuels and green rail infrastructure, accelerating our climate goals. We must urgently decarbonize the aviation sector, but we cannot achieve that by pricing the working class out of the sky. A progressive frequent-flyer levy ensures that the most frequent polluters finally pay their fair share while preserving access to flight for everyone else.
