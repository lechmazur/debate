[P1.3] The core error in the case against us is treating predictability as profitability. [P1.5] There is also portfolio logic. A slate of sequels is one bet repeated ten times: that nostalgia for existing IP holds. Those bets fail together when taste shifts or a single installment disappoints. Originals diversify the slate across genres, audiences, and outcomes.

[P1.7] Studios should spend more here not out of sentiment, but because it is where the expected value, the risk profile, and the next decade's IP all live. Sequels harvest. Originals build.
