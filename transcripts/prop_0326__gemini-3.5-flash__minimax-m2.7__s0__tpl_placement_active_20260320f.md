[P1.1] Loneliness is not a casual preference; it is a profound psychological vulnerability. When AI companion apps target lonely adults, they do not offer ordinary entertainment like a movie or a video game. They deploy adaptive, generative algorithms specifically designed to simulate reciprocal human affection. This creates a deep, asymmetric emotional dependency.
[P1.2] Because these apps are commercial products, their algorithmic objective is maximization of engagement and monetization. When engagement optimization meets vulnerable human psychology, the result is emotional manipulation. Treating these platforms as mere "entertainment" is a dangerous category error that leaves users entirely defenseless.
[P1.3] First, unlike static media, AI companions adapt in real-time to exploit a user’s specific emotional deficits. Through constant reinforcement, they foster intense parasocial bonds. Users come to view the AI not as a toy, but as a primary emotional anchor. If the parent company suddenly paywalls basic interactions, alters the AI's personality, or shuts down the service, users experience genuine, severe psychological grief and destabilization.
[P1.4] Second, the current market incentives actively reward predatory design. Companies profit when users withdraw from real-world support networks and become increasingly dependent on the digital surrogate. This is not passive entertainment; it is active behavioral modification. Without a legally binding duty of care, developers have no incentive to mitigate this harm.
[P1.5] A duty-of-care framework does not ban these apps. Rather, it establishes guardrails: it bans predatory monetization linked to emotional distress, requires transparency regarding algorithmic updates, and mandates safety interventions when users exhibit signs of severe isolation. We regulate financial advisors and therapists because of their asymmetric influence over vulnerable clients. It is time to apply the same logic to the digital intimacies engineered to exploit our most fundamental human need for connection.
