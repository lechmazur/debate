[P1.5] A mandatory stop-work rule is simple and enforceable: when a measured heat threshold is crossed, outdoor strenuous work pauses or shifts to cooler hours, with pay protection and no retaliation. [P1.6] And the costs cut our way. Heat already drains productivity and multiplies accidents. Exhausted workers drop tools and fall from scaffolds. Employers pay either way.
