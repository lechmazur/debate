[P1.1] Rewilding abandoned farmland beats most active reforestation on the metric that decides this motion: carbon and biodiversity per dollar. The land is already leaving production. Succession does the rest unpaid.
[P1.2] Active reforestation buys a forest—seedlings, nurseries, site preparation, crews, fencing, weeding, and replanting after die-off. Those costs often run into the thousands per hectare, and much of the stock never becomes a stand. Rewilding spends on the margin: a fence against cattle, light invasive control, often nothing. These are not prime acres pulled from profit.
[P1.3] They are hill pastures and tired farms the market has already quit, still close to seed sources on Europe’s mountain and Mediterranean margins and on abandoned North American farmland. Trees arrive without a contract.
[P1.4] Carbon follows. Retired fields hold seed banks, nearby woods, and soils that rebuild organic matter once tillage stops. The eastern United States reforested after nineteenth-century abandonment largely without planting crews; that regrowth was a real sink. Secondary growth will not beat a poplar crop in year five, but it holds carbon in mixed wood and often in soil.
[P1.5] Most programs deliver even-aged, narrow mixes, site disturbance that can vent soil carbon, and failures or harvests that return wood to the air. Early growth is not carbon retained per dollar.
[P1.6] Biodiversity is harsher still. Succession is messy on purpose—grass, shrub, pioneer, gap, edge, then dead wood—and that disorder is habitat. Insects, birds, fungi, and mammals track structure and mixture, not rows of stems. A planting contract buys survival rates.
[P1.7] It does not buy the jumble rewilding gets for being cheap.
[P1.8] The best unharvested restoration can be excellent. Most programs are under-tended plantings with thin ambition. Rewilding’s edge is leverage: more hectares, held longer, richer assemblages, for every dollar otherwise spent on labor and seedlings. Where abandonment is the trend and seed can still travel, passive recovery is the higher-yield investment.
