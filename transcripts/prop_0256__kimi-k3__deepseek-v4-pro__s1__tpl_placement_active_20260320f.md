[P1.4] Planned inland cities flip this logic. Well-sited new towns concentrate demand, jobs, and modern infrastructure in one decisive investment.
[P1.5] The justice case cuts the same way. Rebuild subsidies flow overwhelmingly to property owners and to places with the wealth to navigate federal programs; renters and the poor are bought out late, cheaply, or not at all. [P1.6] The CON side must defend an open-ended commitment: pay again after every storm, at rising prices, for assets guaranteed to erode. We propose paying once for assets that appreciate.
