[P1.2] The crucial distinction is lawful adult content versus harmful conduct. Adult creators may be sex educators, performers, writers, or producers of consensual fiction. Their work can be legitimate without being suitable for every storefront. But discomfort is not a lawful ground for exclusion.

[P1.4] Blanket exclusions distort the market. Independent creators lose income while larger incumbents can survive controversy or diversify their brands. [P1.5] Operational concerns have better answers than blanket bans. [P1.6] Yet voluntary refusal to associate cannot, by itself, transform lawful conduct into prohibited conduct. Reputation risk may shape marketing; it should not determine who is allowed to work.
