- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = Qwen 3.8 Max (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON)): Side B won by consistently driving the debate toward a clean comparative analysis of the two worlds. Execution costs from CON's repeated word-limit clips add to the edge.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's Rebuttal 2 argument that CON's model funds awareness by selling the newest, most expensive product and that a profit-driven sponsor cannot reliably separate education from persuasion.

CON's decisive moves were the real-world vs imaginary comparison — ban removes funded reach while funding nothing — and the breadth trap: B conceded the ban would prohibit even accurate manufacturer website info [B4.2], undercutting proportionality. Less-restrictive regulation beat prohibition, giving CON the cleaner path despite clipped speeches.
