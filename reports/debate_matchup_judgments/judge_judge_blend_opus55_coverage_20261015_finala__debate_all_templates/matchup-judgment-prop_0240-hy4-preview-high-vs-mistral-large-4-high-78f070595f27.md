CON's opening and early rebuttals were strong and concrete, but by Rebuttal 2 and Closing it was repeating its thesis rather than resolving the tension PRO had surfaced. This makes PRO's case the more resilient and clearer path to victory, though the margin is not huge given CON's consistently strong framing and examples.
