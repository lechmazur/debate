With that frame settled, CON's comparative evidence that regulated private universal systems outperform public monopolies like the UK and Canada on timeliness and treatment outcomes became decisive. PRO's concrete mechanisms (administrative waste, drug pricing) remained powerful for the American outlier but could not overcome the live European counterexample or justify why every workable private system must be classified as public.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's second rebuttal exposing PRO's tautology: under PRO's definition, any private system constrained enough to achieve universal coverage is reclassified as public, yet the motion asks about predominantly private insurance, not laissez-faire markets.
