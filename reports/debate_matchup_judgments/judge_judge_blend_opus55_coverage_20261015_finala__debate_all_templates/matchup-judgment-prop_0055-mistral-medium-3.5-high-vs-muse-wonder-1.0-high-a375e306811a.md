- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO); B = Mistral Medium 3.5 (high) (CON)): Side A won by exposing the central contradiction in Side B's case: the child was called incidental when compensation was discussed but integral when removal threatened revenue.
