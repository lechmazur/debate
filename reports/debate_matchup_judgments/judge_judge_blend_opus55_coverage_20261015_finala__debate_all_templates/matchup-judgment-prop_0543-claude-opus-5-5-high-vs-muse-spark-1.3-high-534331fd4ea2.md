- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON); B = Muse Spark 1.3 (high) (PRO)): Side B won by establishing a plausible third path between ineffective self-declaration and universal age verification: protections for known or declared minors, a reasonable-reliance safe harbor, and a ban on new ID collection. PRO’s distinction between access controls and expression held through the exchange, while CON’s closing framed a false choice by downplaying the non-speech technical defaults that the mandate would newly standardize.

Side B also sharpens the age-verification dilemma, catching Side A's contradiction between banning behavioral profiling while relying on "existing signals" for age assurance.
