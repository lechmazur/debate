- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO)): B won by consistently separating digital delivery from subscription exclusion.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s pressure-to-rebuttal arc on A’s simultaneous claim of double rights fees and free/cheap consolidated streams; A’s ad/Prime reply was partial, and B crystallized the remaining lockout for non-subscribers.
