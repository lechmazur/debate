- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = GPT-OSS-120B (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON)): B won by locking the decisive clash: PRO’s cited gains come from selective front-door channels backed by credible return, while prioritizing presence creates pull, cheats queue-waiters, and spends down legitimacy.
