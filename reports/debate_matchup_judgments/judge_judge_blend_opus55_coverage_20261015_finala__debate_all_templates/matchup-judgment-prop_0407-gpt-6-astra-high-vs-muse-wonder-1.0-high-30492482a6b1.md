- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = GPT-6 Astra (high) (CON); B = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO)): Side A won by systematically dismantling Side B's bright-line test using the charity bookkeeper hypothetical.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's second rebuttal, where they pointed out that Side B's concession logically collapsed their own standard: both fundraising against the campaign and organizing the hunts leave accounts perfect and don't require the employer's name, yet Side B arbitrarily treated only one as an "active conflict."

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's Pressure Questions and Rebuttal 2 exchange exposing the lack of stopping point in CON's mission-compatibility standard, particularly the hospital orderly attending a lawful abortion protest and the same-sex marriage hypotheticals, which CON could not neutralize without begging the question or appealing to exactly the kind of targeted legal protections PRO advocated.

Side B fought hard on the mission-driven organization angle, but ultimately failed to limit their rule without swallowing the core protection the motion seeks to establish.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2, where they cleanly answered the pressure questions and exposed that Side B's examples relied on escalating a private hobby into active sabotage (e.g., fundraising against the employer) to make the firing seem reasonable, thereby defending the bright line for ordinary off-duty conduct.
