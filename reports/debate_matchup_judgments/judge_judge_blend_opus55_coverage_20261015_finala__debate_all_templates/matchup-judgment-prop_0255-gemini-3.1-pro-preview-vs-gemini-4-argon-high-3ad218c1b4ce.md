- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (CON); B = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO)): A won by making the proposition’s absolute wording the decisive clash: a blanket ban on brokers archiving any deleted/non-public traces would outlaw the cross-platform aggregation that enterprise fraud and identity systems actually use. CON's absolutist reading of the proposition never fully overcame the asymmetry that their solution subjected abuse survivors, teenagers, and ordinary citizens to irrevocable commercial exposure.
