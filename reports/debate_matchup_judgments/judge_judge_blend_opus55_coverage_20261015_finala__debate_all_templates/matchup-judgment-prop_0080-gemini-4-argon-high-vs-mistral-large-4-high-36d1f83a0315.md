### Judge Notes

- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = Mistral Large 4 (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (CON)): PRO built a clean normative syllogism—revenue sharing crosses into employment, therefore formal bargaining is owed—but it stayed largely assertion-driven and never fully absorbed CON's structural attacks. CON also directly refuted PRO's attempt to blame roster cuts on revenue sharing itself by distinguishing variable NIL distribution from mandatory union wage floors. PRO's rebuttals were competent but repeated the "employment threshold" frame rather than answering the economic capture and legal fragmentation problems with comparable specificity. CON's closing crystallized the clash more cleanly, leaving PRO's path to victory weakened on both legal feasibility and the cross-subsidization tradeoff.
