- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO); B = MiniMax-M3 (CON)): Side A won by showing that vendor-by-vendor punishment leaves abusive buyers free to switch suppliers, whereas a buyer-level ban removes access to legal engineering hubs, support, and continuous updates. Its talent argument also blurred a ban on sales to dictatorships into elimination of commercial vulnerability research generally. Side A directly answered the classification challenge and offered stronger weighing between imperfect degradation and knowingly maintaining authoritarian access. Side B also incurred costs from a clipped rebuttal and brittle claims about NSO's collapse and multiple export programs, while Side A's closing contained two incorrect reference labels.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's strongest exchange exposed that Side B's targeted accountability sanctions individual vendors only after abuse while admitting contracts continue through replacements. Side B answered that sanctions create cost and preserve leverage, but never established that this leverage prevents abuse or outweighs cutting off top-tier subscriptions and updates upfront.
- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO); B = MiniMax-M3 (CON)): A won by keeping the motion's buyer-side harm central and turning B's own NSO account into a fatal concession: targeted sanctions left the same autocratic buyers free to switch vendors while "contracts kept flowing." A then weighed continuous commercial updates, unique turnkey/VC supply, and unpatched-OS blowback more cleanly than B's relocation-and-leverage story, which never showed net reduction in dissident/diplomat targeting. B stayed competitive on talent-pool risk and audit-trail loss but lost definitional and alternative force after the NSO contradiction and a clipped R1.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's NSO trap (A2.1/A4.4): B's flagship "targeted accountability" example itself shows buyers simply hire the next legal vendor, so the alternative never stops the regime-side abuse the motion targets.
- **Muse Spark 1.1 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO); B = MiniMax-M3 (CON)): PRO won the central relocation debate.
