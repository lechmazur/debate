- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Mistral Large 4 (high) (PRO); B = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON)): B won a clear but competitive debate by exposing the tension in A's policy: meaningful teacher-planned exceptions preserve classroom variation, while restrictive exceptions deny legitimate tools and accommodations. B directly challenged those assumptions and offered the cleaner comparison of visible, supervised use against denial followed by uncertain exceptions.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's strongest exchange was the exception dilemma. A answered that teachers would plan exemptions in advance, but B showed that this still relies on teacher judgment and cannot cover unanticipated needs, weakening A's central claims of guaranteed equity and eliminated variation.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s Rebuttal 2 / closing on floor-not-ceiling: exceptions remain teacher judgment, so PRO either restores classroom variation or makes every student lose the tool first and wait.
