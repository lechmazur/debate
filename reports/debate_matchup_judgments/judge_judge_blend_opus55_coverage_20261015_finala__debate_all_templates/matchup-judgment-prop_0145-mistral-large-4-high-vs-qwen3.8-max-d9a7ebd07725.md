- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = Mistral Large 4 (high) (CON); B = Qwen 3.8 Max (PRO)): Both sides argued cleanly within format and landed real clash, but PRO (B) ultimately controlled the decisive comparison better. CON's "tripwire" reframing (evasion = specific concern) was clever, but PRO's pressure question forced CON into an extreme, unqualified admission that no message category is ever off-limits (A4.3), which fed directly into PRO's disproportionality and chilling-effect case rather than defusing it.
