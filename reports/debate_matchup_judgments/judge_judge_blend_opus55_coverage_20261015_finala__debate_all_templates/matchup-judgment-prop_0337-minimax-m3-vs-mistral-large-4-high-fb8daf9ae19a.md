- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = MiniMax-M3 (CON); B = Mistral Large 4 (high) (PRO)): Both sides argued competently, but PRO built the more resilient case. CON's structural critiques remain live concerns but were not driven to a decisive kill, while PRO's reframes and analogies (slot machine, hospital, "remove the machine from the room") gave a cleaner, more memorable path to a verdict.
