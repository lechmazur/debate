Side B never successfully reconciled this flaw.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 1 and 2 cleanly dismantled Side B's housing argument by pointing out that day-trippers have zero housing impact, and pivoting to overnight guests directly accelerates STR conversions.
