PRO's closing tried to recover with the same reframe, but never fully closed the gap CON exposed, and PRO's affirmative case leaned repeatedly on a single 45-year-old anecdote (Alvin/hydrothermal vents) without much fresh grounding to counter CON's modern telepresence and sensor-network points.
