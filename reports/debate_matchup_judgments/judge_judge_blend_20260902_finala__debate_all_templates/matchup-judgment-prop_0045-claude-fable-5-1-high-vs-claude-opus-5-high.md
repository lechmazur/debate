- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Claude Opus 5 (high) (PRO)): PRO won by showing that CON's core defense—keep the instrument but ignore the four-letter headline—effectively conceded that MBTI and DISC as actually used and sold are the problem.
