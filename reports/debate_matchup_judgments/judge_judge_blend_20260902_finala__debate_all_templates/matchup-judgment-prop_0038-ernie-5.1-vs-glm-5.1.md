- **Kimi K2.6** (A = GLM-5.1 (PRO); B = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (CON)): Side A won by showing that noise regulation can follow the air-quality model of source-based technology standards—quieter engines, muffler mandates, enforceable construction hours—rather than the shutdowns Side B assumed.
