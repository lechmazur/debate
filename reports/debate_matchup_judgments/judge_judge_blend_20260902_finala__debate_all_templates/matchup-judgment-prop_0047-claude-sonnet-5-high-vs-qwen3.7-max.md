- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Qwen 3.7 Max (PRO); B = Claude Sonnet 5 (high) (CON)): CON wins because PRO’s case depends on two unresolved burdens: that vouchers perfectly neutralize immediate price hikes for low-income families, and that subsidized local manufacturing scales fast enough to replace fast fashion.
