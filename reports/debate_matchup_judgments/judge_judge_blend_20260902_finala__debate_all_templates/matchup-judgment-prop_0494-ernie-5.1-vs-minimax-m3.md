### Judge Notes

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s most decisive exchange was answering the fee-chasing objection by distinguishing piece-rate payment from quality-tied compensation, while also turning the reputation point: unpaid anonymous review already lacks meaningful accountability.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (CON); B = MiniMax-M3 (PRO)): Side B won by decisively dismantling Side A's defense of the status quo. Additionally, Side A relied on brittle fake precision (a highly specific 15-20% budget overrun stat) in Rebuttal 2, lowering its grounding score.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's Rebuttal 1 point that "Reputation only constrains what is visible," which completely neutralized Side A's opening claim that the current anonymous system aligns motives with reputation.

Side B raised valid points about anonymity and incentive alignment but undermined its grounding with brittle, unsourced precision (“15–20% budget overruns,” vague “long-run data”) in a closed-book format. B’s closing also reiterated earlier claims without advancing the comparison, leaving A’s corrigibility frame as the decisive path to victory.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s Rebuttal 2 directly answering the pressure questions: it dismantled the inevitability of de-anonymization by distinguishing softened tone from substantive rigor, and neutralized the fee-chasing worry by proposing editor-gated, quality-tied honoraria.
