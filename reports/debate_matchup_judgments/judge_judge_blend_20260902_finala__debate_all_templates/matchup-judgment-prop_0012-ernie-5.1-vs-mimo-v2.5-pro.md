- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (PRO); B = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (CON)): Side A won by making the decisive issue consent and final authority over ancestors, then showing that Side B's model leaves institutions with an indefinite veto whenever they can claim more research value.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's limiting-principle attack was decisive: B answered with "demonstrable purpose" judged by institutions, which A persuasively framed as circular and effectively endless retention.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's first rebuttal, which turned the Kennewick Man example against Side B, exposed the forensic reference argument as a red herring, and leveraged Side B's own concession that nations already negotiate research agreements.
- **Qwen 3.6 Max Preview** (A = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (PRO)): PRO wins by decisively controlling the central clash over decision-making authority. PRO's pressure questions successfully exposed that CON's proposed limiting principle ("demonstrable purpose" judged by institutions) is functionally circular and provides no actual stopping rule against indefinite delay. PRO effectively reframed the collaboration dynamic, arguing convincingly that repatriation mandates create the leverage necessary for genuine partnership, and used the NAGPRA precedent to neutralize CON's claims that science would suffer. CON's case was structurally competent but relied on a weak limiting principle, a stretched forensics argument, and a closing that grew repetitive and contained awkward reference-ID artifacts. PRO's sharper rhetorical framing, cleaner progression across rounds, and superior weighing of institutional power versus community sovereignty provide the clearer, more persuasive path to victory.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's systematic dismantling of CON's limiting principle. After CON offered "demonstrable purpose assessed by institutions" as a threshold, PRO correctly identified it as circular: the possessor deciding when they are finished possessing. PRO crystallized this in the closing by noting that no researcher ever voluntarily declares a collection "exhausted," turning CON's standard into a "perpetual license." This exchange resolved the core mechanism clash in PRO's favor and made CON's model practically indefensible.
