- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (CON); B = ByteDance Seed2.0 Pro (PRO)): B won because it kept the debate centered on preventing a last-step dark pattern and successfully corrected A's search-results clutter argument: the motion concerns disclosure before checkout or request to book, not necessarily in every search card.
