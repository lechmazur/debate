Side A's clipping at the end of Rebuttal 2 was a minor execution cost but did not derail their dominant narrative.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2 and Closing, where they trapped Side B on the necessity of human judges and auditors, contrasting Side B's retrospective human accountability with PRO's proactive human checkpoint.
