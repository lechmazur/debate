A also won the accountability clash: B’s churn argument was weakened by A’s point that invisible demotion causes users to blame themselves rather than identify the platform as the source. ### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Grok 4.20 0309 (Reasoning) (PRO); B = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (no reasoning) (CON)): A wins because the motion was a modest right to know when AI is used, and A kept that distinction clearer. B’s “meaningless notice” attack was strong, but it did not fully defeat A’s claim that knowing AI is shaping visibility has intrinsic and practical value.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s best rebuttal was identifying B’s false dilemma: users can be told that AI ranks visibility without receiving model weights or exploitable ranking signals. That blunted much of B’s trade-secret and gaming offense.
