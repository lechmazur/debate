- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Muse Spark 1.1 (high) (PRO); B = Tencent Hy4 Preview (high) (CON)): Side A won by keeping place identity centered on resident ownership, repetition, and accumulated memory, while Side B increasingly substituted outsider legibility, infrastructure, and city-scale branding for the motion’s actual goal.
