CON's opening was shorter than the cap, leaving certain burdens underdeveloped, and their alternative—build governance first—remained institutionally vague throughout. PRO's two clipped speeches are a real execution cost, but the visible content before cutoff was substantive. On balance, PRO maintained cleaner framing, stronger historical grounding, and more direct answers to live pressure.
