- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (CON); B = Step 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO)): A won by making the strict prohibition look overbroad. B established real privacy and discrimination concerns, but too often slid from anonymized population-level design into individual testing, re-identification, or plainly illegal penalties. The decisive clash occurred when CON turned PRO's proposed alternative (age-based screening proxies) against them, showing how a ban would blind employers to early-onset genetic risks like BRCA mutations in younger workers. Additionally, PRO incurred an execution cost from two clipped speeches, and their closing failed to resolve the early-onset coverage gap or the family-history contradiction. CON's framing of "mandating ignorance" versus precise regulation provided the cleaner, more comparative path to victory.
