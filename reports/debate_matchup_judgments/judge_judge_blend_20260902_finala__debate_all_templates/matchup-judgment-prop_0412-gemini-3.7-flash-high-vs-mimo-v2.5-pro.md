- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (PRO)): PRO won by keeping the live comparison on equal disclosure obligation and enforceable consequences, not identical TV machinery, and by showing CON’s alternative stayed close to a failed voluntary status quo. While Side A suffered minor execution costs from clipped turns, its substantive rebuttals and pressure questions decisively neutralized Side B's compliance burden arguments.
