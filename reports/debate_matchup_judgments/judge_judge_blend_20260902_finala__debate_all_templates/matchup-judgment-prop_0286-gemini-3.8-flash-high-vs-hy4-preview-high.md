- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) (CON); B = Tencent Hy4 Preview (high) (PRO)): Side B won by separating useful commercial spaceflight from tourism and asking whether passengers add public benefits that would not otherwise exist. B therefore preserved immediate luxury-emissions and opportunity-cost objections while neutralizing A’s main spillover defense.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B’s additionality rebuttal was decisive: government and satellite customers can finance useful launches without tourists, so the passenger primarily purchases recreation and adds emissions. B’s false-dilemma and spillover framing remained live but secondary once A showed the claimed public goods already exist without joyrides. Cleaner comparative weighing and honest answers to pressure gave A the clearer path.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s Rebuttal 2 (A4.5–A4.6): yacht-vs-satellite pressure plus the “defenses cancel” point that the tourist cannot be both pure emitter and climate philanthropist.
