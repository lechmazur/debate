- **Kimi K2.6** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON); B = GPT-5.6 Sol (high) (PRO)): PRO wins because it successfully reframed the central clash: the issue is not merely auditing existing corporate models, but ensuring independent research capacity to train alternatives, test scaling claims, and pursue safety research without corporate permission.
