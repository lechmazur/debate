- **Grok 4.3** (A = Gemini 3.5 Flash (CON); B = GPT-5.5 (high) (PRO)): Side B (PRO) built the stronger case by consistently sharpening the distinction between conduct/competence evaluation and compelled belief affirmation, directly answering pressure questions with clear limiting principles, and crystallizing the autonomy and viewpoint-neutrality stakes in closing.
