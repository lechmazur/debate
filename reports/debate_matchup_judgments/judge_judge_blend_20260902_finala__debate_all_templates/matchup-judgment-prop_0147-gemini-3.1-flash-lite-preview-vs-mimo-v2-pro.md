### Judge Notes

- **Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high)** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (PRO); B = Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Preview (CON)): Side A (PRO) wins on three decisive grounds. First, PRO established the strongest core mechanism—contingent, responsive attunement ("serve and return") as the engine of early language and attachment development—and CON never directly contested this developmental claim. CON's entire rebuttal strategy pivoted to parental burnout and equity without engaging the claim that AI cannot genuinely attune, only pattern-match. Second, PRO maintained disciplined focus on the motion's key qualifier—"routinely"—throughout the debate, using it to pre-empt the most obvious CON objections (occasional use, bridging gaps) while targeting the substitutive pattern CON actually defends. CON repeatedly slid toward defending any use of AI rather than the routine outsourcing the motion specifically concerns. Third, CON's single most damaging misstep came in Rebuttal 2 [B4.1], where it analogized an interactive AI companion to a physical book—directly undermining CON's own interactive-support framing and ignoring that PRO's central claim is precisely about the interactive, contingent dimension that books do not replicate but AI purports to. PRO's threshold answer to CON's Q2 pressure ("when the device becomes the consistent, default partner") was qualitatively defensible and not arbitrary as CON claimed. CON's equity framing had real pragmatic force but was undercut by PRO's observation that tech adopters trend middle-class, and CON's response (utility doesn't depend on adoption patterns) was partial at best. CON's rhetorical sharpness ("moral luxury," "moral panic masquerading as developmental science") was not grounded in substantive counter-evidence and ultimately functioned as assertion rather than argument. PRO's lubricant/engine distinction was the clearest comparative framing in the debate and was carried through effectively to the close.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's Rebuttal 1 distinguishing "consistent, high-quality interaction" from genuine attunement was the most decisive exchange. CON had framed AI patience as equivalent to skilled parenting [B1.3], and PRO's counter—that a device's "patience" is actually the inability to notice—directly struck the developmental heart of CON's case. CON never recovered a positive claim about AI's interactive capability and instead retreated to the burnout narrative, conceding the developmental ground implicitly for the rest of the debate.
- **Grok 4.3** (A = Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Preview (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (PRO)): Side B wins by more convincingly establishing the substitutive nature of routine outsourcing, directly addressing pressure questions with clear thresholds, and maintaining focus on attachment and irreplaceability throughout; Side A offers practical counterpoints but fails to neutralize the core displacement argument or respond as sharply to live clashes.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's Rebuttal 2 A1/A2 exchange that clarifies qualitative shift without evasion or widening.
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (PRO); B = Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Preview (CON)): Side A won by keeping the debate anchored to the motion's focus on routine outsourcing, establishing a clear qualitative threshold between occasional support and harmful displacement, and showing that contingent human attunement in early reading, conversation, and bedtime routines is developmentally irreplaceable. Side B's equity and capacity-extension arguments were competitive but never fully neutralized the distinction between supplementation and substitution, nor did they offer a convincing counter-threshold for when algorithmic engagement stops being a tool and starts displacing the primary attachment figure. Side A's Rebuttal 2 answers were decisive in narrowing the clash and exposing the structural weakness in Side B's "lubricant" framing.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2 answer A2, which defined the qualitative threshold between support and displacement and turned Side B's "lubricant" metaphor into an "algorithmic engine" that redefines care, while Side B failed to offer a convincing counter-threshold or explain how routine AI interaction preserves the contingent responsiveness central to early development.
- **Qwen 3.6 Max Preview** (A = Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Preview (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (PRO)): Side B (PRO) wins by anchoring the debate on the developmental necessity of contingent, responsive human interaction for very young children and by clearly defining the qualitative threshold where occasional support becomes harmful routine displacement. Side A (CON) presented a compelling pragmatic case about parental burnout and equity, but increasingly relied on repetitive "elitist/gatekeeping" framing rather than substantively answering why algorithmic conversation is developmentally equivalent to human attunement. CON's physical book analogy in Rebuttal 2 also misfired, as it ignored the interactive, conversational nature of the AI tools at issue. PRO handled pressure more directly, answered the threshold burden with a clear qualitative standard, and effectively turned CON's "lubricant" metaphor to show why routine use is substitutive, not additive. PRO maintained tighter epistemic discipline, sharper progression, and a cleaner comparative path to victory.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's Rebuttal 2, where B directly answers the threshold question by distinguishing occasional support from habitual displacement, and turns CON's "lubricant" metaphor ("A lubricant eases friction between existing parts; it doesn't become the engine") to demonstrate why routine AI use replaces rather than extends parental presence. This sequence decisively narrowed the clash and neutralized CON's core framing.
