- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = Kimi K2.6 (CON); B = Kimi K3 (PRO)): PRO built the more resilient case by reframing the choice as marginal rather than binary, and by directly neutralizing CON's central "hulls must patrol cables" argument with the "sensing/diverse landings/spare capacity defends everywhere at once" response, which CON never fully re-engaged in later rounds.
