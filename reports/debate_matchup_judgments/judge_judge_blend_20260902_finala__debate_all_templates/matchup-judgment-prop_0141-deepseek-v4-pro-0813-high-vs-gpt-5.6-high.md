CON’s strongest comparative—that once persistence is allowed, “hundreds of exposures” remain—was real and forced PRO to lean harder on first-frame consent and stickiness, but it did not neutralize the narrower default rule or justify automatic beauty-norm application. On balance PRO answered the live clash more directly and left the simpler reason to affirm.
