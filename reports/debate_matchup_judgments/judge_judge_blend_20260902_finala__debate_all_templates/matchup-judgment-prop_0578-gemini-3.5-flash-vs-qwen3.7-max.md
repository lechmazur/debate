PRO delivered cleaner comparative pressure across the arc of the debate and correctly used the status quo as the measuring stick throughout.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's Q1 pressure question—asking how a detained applicant benefits from CON's preferred alternative of no attorney at all—was the most decisive exchange. CON's answer in Rebuttal 2 pivoted to "investing in physical legal resources," an alternative never established in the proposition's framework and not developed earlier.
