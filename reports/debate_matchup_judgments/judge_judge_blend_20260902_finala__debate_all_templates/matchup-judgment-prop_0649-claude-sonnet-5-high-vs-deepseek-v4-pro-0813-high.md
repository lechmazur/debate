That left PRO with no live benefit that survives correct pricing and no response to the regressive cost-shift under imperfect pricing. PRO's clipped opening and inability to answer the dilemma cleanly in later speeches add execution costs, while CON crystallized the clash effectively in the closing.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s dual-branch answer to B’s “can’t be both” pressure (A4/A5): both the pure-billing and faster-proliferation readings independently favor CON.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON’s Rebuttal 2 (Turn 8), where it resolved PRO’s attempted dilemma by showing the “invoice only” and “accelerated adoption” critiques operate at different timescales, exposed PRO’s pre-set tariff model as merely a complicated feed-in tariff wearing a P2P costume, and framed the motion as offering either cost without benefit or risk without reward.

CON's final retreat to "thin sliver + socialized metering cost" concedes feasibility and never shows costs outweigh gains.
