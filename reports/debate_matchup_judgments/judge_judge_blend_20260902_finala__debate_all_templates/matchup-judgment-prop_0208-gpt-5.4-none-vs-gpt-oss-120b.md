Neither side dominated, but PRO's coupling of equity with the specific proposal (need-based aid pools + stackable pathways) was more actionable than CON's alternative of unspecified systemic reform. Two format clips for Side B carry a modest execution cost, and the somewhat brittle specifics PRO introduced in Rebuttal 2 (50%, 15%) register as mild epistemic overreach but were directly responsive to pressure rather than gratuitous. On balance, PRO maintained a tighter through-line from opening to close while CON's strongest argumentative moment arrived too late to flip the accumulated advantage.
