CON's "hidden tax on housing" framing was rhetorically effective but became repetitive across later rounds without adding new analytical force. PRO's closing was clipped, a real execution cost, but it had already synthesized the key concession point before the cut. On balance, PRO's case was more internally consistent, better adapted to pressure, and left the cleaner reason to vote its way.
