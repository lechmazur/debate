- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5 (high) (CON); B = Step 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO)): A won by narrowing the debate to what dynamic pricing adds beyond loading zones and enforcement, then repeatedly showing that B’s strongest examples depended on those non-pricing tools.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s second rebuttal was decisive: it exposed B’s late 12-18 percent pricing-alone claim as unsupported, turned B’s “nearby lower-priced legal spot” answer into a concession that legal supply does the work, and reframed dynamic pricing as relocation or extraction rather than a true behavioral signal.
