- **Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (PRO); B = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (CON)): Side A built the stronger cumulative case and came out ahead on the two decisive clashes. On parts pairing: PRO introduced the concept early and it anchored the debate. CON's [B4.1–4.2] made the sharpest attack of the whole debate—arguing that parts pairing is a firmware lock, not a distribution problem, so a distribution-focused law is a "shell." But PRO's closing [A5.1–5.2] correctly answered this by pointing back to the proposition's own language: "parts, tools, and documentation" — "tools" naturally includes software authentication tools. CON's attempt to narrow "tools" to physical parts only was an unwarranted restriction of plain meaning, and PRO earned this reframe. The clipping of [B2] is a real execution cost for CON, as their first rebuttal was cut before the conclusion. PRO's Q1 pressure question directly exposed the gap in CON's informal-sector defense, and PRO answered CON's own questions with the A1:/A2: structure cleanly. CON's "catalog" line was rhetorically effective but was directly answered and did not remain live at close.
