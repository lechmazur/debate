- **Qwen 3.7 Max** (A = Step 3.7 Flash (high) (CON); B = Muse Spark 1.1 (high) (PRO)): Side B won decisively by dismantling Side A's primary economic objection. Furthermore, Side B provided a concrete, auditable mechanism for preventing wage theft (defining the charge as wages on a paystub), whereas Side A's defense of cash tips ignored the realities of credit card tips, tip-outs, and unreported skimming. Side B's rhetoric was also sharper and more memorable, while Side A suffered minor execution costs from clipped turns.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's Rebuttal 2, where they directly answered Side A's pressure questions by clarifying that replacing a tip with an included charge does not mathematically double-count labor costs, and by proposing a specific legal mechanism (itemizing the charge as wages on payroll) to make it auditable and prosecutable as wage theft.
