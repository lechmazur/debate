  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's second rebuttal (A4) decisively neutralizes Side B's argument that 'an entrant faces the same costs' by highlighting that entrants do not bear the originator's research failures, thus dismantling B's claim that the market will perfectly distinguish between monopoly rent and genuine cost.

CON’s grounds dilemma landed repeatedly—if breach is only optional permission, delay and politics remain; if it is pre-decided justification, leverage arrives before copyability, supply continuity, and net benefit are known. PRO’s residual “is there a supplier?” test conceded residual judgment, which CON used to argue the reform either underdelivers or still gambles on anticipatory launch and supply effects.
