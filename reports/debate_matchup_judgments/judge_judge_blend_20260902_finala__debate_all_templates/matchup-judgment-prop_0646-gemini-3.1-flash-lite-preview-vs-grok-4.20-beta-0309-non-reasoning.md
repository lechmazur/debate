PRO's pressure questions were sharper and more precise, but CON's A1/A2 answers in B4 were adequate, while PRO never provided adequate A-labelled responses to CON's counter-pressure: dismissing variance as illusion in A4.1 and pivoting to an arsonist analogy in A4.2 are not satisfying answers to the specific challenges raised. On balance, CON leaves the more important burdens live.
