- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) (CON); B = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (PRO)): A made the debate live by pressing settler entrenchment and the two-state process as an alibi, but B carried the exchange.
