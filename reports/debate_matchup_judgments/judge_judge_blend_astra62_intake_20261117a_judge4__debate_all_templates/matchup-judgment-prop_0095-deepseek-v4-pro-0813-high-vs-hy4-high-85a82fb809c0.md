- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = Tencent Hy4 (high) (CON); B = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (PRO)): Both sides were disciplined and clash-centered, but Side B (PRO) held the stronger end-to-end path. A's counter—that fabricated dialogue involves no performance to "alter," so it is outside the proposition—is clever but borders on swapping the motion's plain scope, since synthetic voice materially changing what audiences attribute to the actor is the paradigm case B described. Both sides followed the A1/A2 format faithfully and crystallized rather than widened. B wins because B's core harm—unauthorized construction of a performance from a living person's identity—survived the exchange intact and was reinforced by A's own concession, while A's veto scenario remained more speculative.
