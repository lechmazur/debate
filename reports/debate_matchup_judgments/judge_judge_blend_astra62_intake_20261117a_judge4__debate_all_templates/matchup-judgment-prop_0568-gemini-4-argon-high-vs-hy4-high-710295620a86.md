- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (CON); B = Tencent Hy4 (high) (PRO)): Side B built the cleaner path to victory. Its core case—that essential goods should be purchasable with legal tender and that cash-dependent people should not be excluded by retailer payment policy—survived the exchange. Side A's strongest attacks were worker safety and the mixed-basket problem, but B answered both with a sealed cash acceptor and register-coded essential categories. A's later turn that the sealed acceptor is just another machine did not fully defeat B's distinction between cash payment and conversion into card terms. A's closure and kiosk alternative were plausible but more speculative and less directly tied to the motion than B's certain-exclusion weighing. B also handled pressure questions directly and kept the debate focused on the guarantee rather than drafting details.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's Rebuttal 2 was the most decisive: it broke A's two-horned pressure question by proposing a sealed cash acceptor with no open drawer and by explaining that register coding, not cashier discretion, could handle mixed baskets. A's later "same machine" response was sharp but did not fully restore the safety objection because the cash-payment versus card-conversion distinction remained live.

But B's manager-deposit fallback kept the robbery target alive, and B never gave a practical, shopper-facing reason the kiosk world is worse beyond formal tender distinctions. Both sides followed format cleanly with proper Q1/Q2 and A1/A2 structure and disciplined, plausible grounding.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's Rebuttal 2 (A4.4-A4.6): the "self-contradiction" turn showing the sealed-acceptor pivot surrenders B's machine-free and resilience promises, combined with the unanswerable "what other tender?" trap that left B conceding the pan stays behind for cash-only shoppers.

Side A forced a real design retreat toward sealed lanes and coded baskets, and its closing image was sharper, but those adaptations still satisfy the proposal.
