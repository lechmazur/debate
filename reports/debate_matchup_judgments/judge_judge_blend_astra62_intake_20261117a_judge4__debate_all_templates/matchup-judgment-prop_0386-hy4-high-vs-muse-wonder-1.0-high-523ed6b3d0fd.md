- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = Tencent Hy4 (high) (CON); B = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO)): PRO narrowly won by turning CON's own access examples into cases the proposed condition already blocks, and by narrowing the live question to whether refusing in a genuinely seamless next-room case imposes any concrete patient burden.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's rebuttal that if the colleague is sick, the slot vanishes, or records fail to transfer, then the condition forbids refusal — meaning CON's own examples prove the guardrail rather than defeat it — was the most decisive exchange.
- **Gemini 4 Carbon (high)** (A = Tencent Hy4 (high) (CON); B = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO)): Side B (PRO) wins a high-level, competitive debate by exposing a decisive tension in Side A's attempt to allow upstream scheduling accommodations while banning all same-day step-asides. Side B’s upstream-assignment distinction was sharp and original, but it relied too heavily on administrative idealization and left the strongest intuitive problem unanswered: why forbid a seamless, patient-protective step-aside at the bedside.
