- **Kimi K3.1 Pro (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.2 (high) (CON); B = Gemini 4 Carbon (high) (PRO)): Side A won a close, high-quality debate by turning B’s implementation concessions into a comparative burden: the legal design mandate does the protective work, while the parental toggle mainly serves already-engaged households and faces an anonymity-versus-enforceability dilemma.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's response to A's universal opt-in default was decisive: if adults can restore feeds in one tap without age proof, children can too, so durable protection still requires a parent-controlled lock.
