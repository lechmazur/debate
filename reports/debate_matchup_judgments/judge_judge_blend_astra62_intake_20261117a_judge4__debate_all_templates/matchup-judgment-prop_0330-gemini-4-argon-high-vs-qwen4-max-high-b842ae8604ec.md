- **Claude Opus 5.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (CON); B = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO)): PRO (B) built a clear risk-based case resting on three asymmetries: a captive audience that cannot opt out, children watching at scale, and pitches timed to peak arousal and fused with live play.
