- **Claude Opus 5.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Carbon (high) (PRO); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (CON)): PRO wins clearly. PRO answered each point directly. Verification is an ordinary dispatch or outreach task. Hazardous acts like idling and dumping stay enforceable. Sanctions cannot be a lever when there is nowhere safe to steer. The pivotal moment came in CON's A1. CON conceded that outreach or dispatch must confirm and offer a fitting space before any sanction bites. That is functionally the motion's own conditional protection. PRO's closing exploited this cleanly, showing the administrability objection collapses under CON's own workflow. CON's closing tried to claim PRO had adopted CON's policy. But the motion already permits sanctions once a safe alternative exists, so the motion does not "delete" the last step. The "lever" argument also stayed weak in the core scenario where shelter is full. CON also took two clipping penalties, losing the ends of both its opening and its second rebuttal. CON's best surviving point was the night-shift worker and the windshield-flyer jab. It was real but narrow, and PRO answered it reasonably.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's closing turned CON's A1 concession, "only after a genuine, fitting placement is refused does any backstop bite," into proof that the administrability objection fails. Under CON's own sequencing, no sanction occurs when no safe space is confirmed, which is exactly what the motion protects. That concession left CON's "lever" with nothing to push toward when shelters are full.
- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (CON); B = Gemini 4 Carbon (high) (PRO)): B won the decisive clash. A's strongest line was that "no safe alternative shelter" is an unadministrable 2 a.m. trigger, but B repeatedly turned it: A's own "steer-first, sanction-last" workflow (A4.1) requires outreach to confirm an open, fitting space before any citation issues, which is functionally the same verification A called impossible. A also incurred execution costs with two clipped turns, losing the ends of its opening and second rebuttal, though its visible argumentation was coherent and persistent.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: The pivotal exchange was B4.1/A4.1/B5.1: B demanded to know how A can sanction without verifying an open, suitable space — the exact check A called impossible. A's answer conceded that dispatch must confirm a fitting placement before any citation, and B's closing hammered that this concedes the motion's substance while A merely refuses to write it into law.
