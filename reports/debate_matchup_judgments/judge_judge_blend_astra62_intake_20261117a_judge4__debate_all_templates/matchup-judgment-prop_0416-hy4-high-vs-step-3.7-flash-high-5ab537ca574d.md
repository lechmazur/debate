- **GPT-6 Astra (high)** (A = Step 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO); B = Tencent Hy4 (high) (CON)): B supplied the stronger comparison: claim-linked disclosures address misleading advertising at first contact, while personalized counseling precedes payment.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s strongest rebuttal targeted A’s decision to count non-users as failures: that metric mixes biological success with whether patients later use their eggs. Side B gave a clear fairness case and answered pressure directly, but its chosen metric became vulnerable when it counted non-users as failures, and its closing did not fully engage A’s claim-linked alternative.
