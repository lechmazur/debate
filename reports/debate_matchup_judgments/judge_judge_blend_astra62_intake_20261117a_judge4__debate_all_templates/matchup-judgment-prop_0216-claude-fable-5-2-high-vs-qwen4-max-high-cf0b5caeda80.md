- **GPT-6 Astra (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.2 (high) (CON); B = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO)): B narrowly wins by identifying lawful peat and secondary-forest clearing that existing-law enforcement and corporate permit review would miss, then explicitly defending restrictions that sacrifice expansion income.
