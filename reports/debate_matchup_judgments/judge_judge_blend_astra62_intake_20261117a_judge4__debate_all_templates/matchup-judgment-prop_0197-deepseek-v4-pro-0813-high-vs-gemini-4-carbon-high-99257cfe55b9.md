- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Carbon (high) (CON); B = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (PRO)): Side A (CON) won by extracting and exploiting three concessions. Second, A exposed an internal tension in B's case: B argued a low-altitude carve-out "normalizes the weapon," yet B's own design preserves hit-to-kill validation via suborbital missile-defense tests and an emergency exception. Third, the ratification asymmetry—rivals locked out of affordable orbital validation while adversaries perfect identical interceptors suborbitally—was never squarely answered; B's reply that states "aren't barred from developing defensive interceptors" ignored the cost-barrier point, leaving A's "paper rules stop zero debris" live through the closing. B's strongest remaining ground was the comparative that imperfect, attributable verification beats an open right to test, and that the unregulated present is the real danger—genuine but weakened by the ratification and scope problems. B also drifted from the motion as written by conceding an emergency exception, which A tied neatly to the "all kinetic tests" wording. Both sides followed format (Q1/Q2, A1/A2), answered pressure honestly, and stayed grounded in plausible background knowledge without fake sourcing. A's rebuttals were sharper, more adaptive, and progressively narrowed the dispute; B's later speeches largely restated the "enforceable first cut" frame without resolving the asymmetry or the altitude-graduation weighing.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: The decisive exchange was A's Rebuttal 2 [A4.4]: using B's own concessions—perigee geometry [B4.1] and "high-altitude breakups" as the cascade source [B4.6]—to show the total ban rests not on debris physics but on weapon non-proliferation, which B simultaneously permits through suborbital interceptors and unverifiable emergency exceptions, dismantling the internal logic of B's case.

- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON); B = Gemini 4 Carbon (high) (PRO)): B (PRO) won by converting A's core objections into strengths.
