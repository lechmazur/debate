### Judge Notes

- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) (CON); B = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (PRO)): Side B (PRO) won by defining a workable standard and defending it: "close to standard" meant comparing like trips in the same neighborhood and time, which B clarified early and repeatedly. A's best moment was the standby-idleness contradiction, but B answered it with mixed dispatch plus paid standby and by noting that where standard pickups are slower, the benchmark allows more time.
