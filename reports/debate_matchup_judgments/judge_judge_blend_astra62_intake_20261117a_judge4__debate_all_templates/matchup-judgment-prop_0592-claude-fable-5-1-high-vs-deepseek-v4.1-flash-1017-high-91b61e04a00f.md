- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (high) (PRO); B = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON)): Side B won a clear but competitive debate by showing that faster adoption is not necessarily faster effective action when dissenting states control crucial banks, ports, registries, and borders. B also offered a concrete alternative and a relevant implementation precedent, then weighed recurring leakage against temporary negotiating delay more sharply.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B’s second rebuttal was decisive: it answered that under-enforcement already exists but argued ownership worsens after an imposed vote, used the failed migrant-relocation scheme as a direct implementation analogy, and turned A’s concessions about slow enforcement and budget unanimity into comparative offense.
