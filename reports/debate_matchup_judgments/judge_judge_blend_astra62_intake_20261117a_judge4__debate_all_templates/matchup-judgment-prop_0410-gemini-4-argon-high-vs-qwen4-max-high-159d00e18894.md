### Judge Notes

- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (CON)): PRO built the cleaner path on the motion. PRO also effectively exposed CON's policy line as being about how a child arrives rather than how they are raised: CON conceded same-sex parents could raise even high-need foster children but still assumed developmental harm for planned donor/surrogacy cases without demonstrating a replicated deficit.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's second rebuttal and closing: CON's donor/surrogacy line collapsed into a universal restriction that does no work on orientation, and the foster concession plus headcount analogy left no live, measurable child-outcome deficit unique to same-sex parents.
