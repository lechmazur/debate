### Judge Notes

- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = Qwen 4 Max (high) (CON); B = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (PRO)): Side B (PRO) built the stronger path to victory. B successfully defended the proposition's core-function test against Side A's central attack that the gate either self-defines religion or forces state grading of theology. B's entrustment answer offered a plausible third option: the institution designates what it relies on for formation and worship, and courts check that factual reliance without evaluating theology. B also undercut A's redundancy challenge with the hotel-agent example, showing that equal standing can defeat an exemption even when access is preserved, and with the press example showing the test protects religious editorial functions despite commercial form. A's critique was sharp and persistent, especially in turning B's own examples into evidence for a burden-and-tailoring test, but A could not fully defeat B's final framing that exemptions should track displaced religious authority rather than raw burden. The narrow clash favored B, and B answered pressure questions more cleanly than A.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's Rebuttal 2 hotel-agent answer was the most decisive exchange: even though an outside agent could deliver identical room access, B refused the exemption because the couple is entitled to book like everyone else rather than have a religious test administered efficiently. That directly undercut A's claim that the hotel case was resolved by narrow tailoring, and showed the core/commercial gate adds real protective work.
- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (PRO); B = Qwen 4 Max (high) (CON)): This was an exceptionally even, high-level debate. Side A mounted a credible defense of the core-function gate: the compliance-impact test, the entrustment answer to the grading-religion dilemma, and the hotel-agent hypothetical that exposed a real divergence between categorical rules and balancing. But Side B sustained the sharpest strategic arc of the debate — the redundancy argument — from opening ("their case shows some exemptions should fail, not that exemption should exist only inside a judge's definition of worship") through closing ("the word 'core' has still never decided a case"). B repeatedly reabsorbed A's rulings as covert applications of sincerity-burden-tailoring, answered A's vacancy pressure by redefining the injury as the religious test itself, and honestly conceding the tenant case removed A's strongest emotional ground while making CON more credible. A's entrustment answer was a genuine third option, but B's rejoinder — that it leaves the boundary either self-defined or judge-audited — was never fully shattered, and A underdeveloped the "categorical rules settle what balancing leaves to judicial variance" weighing that would have best answered redundancy. B's rhetorical control ("worship in aprons," "my test, wearing the word 'core'," "religion, safely indoors") made the clash consistently legible. Narrow win to B on strategy and clash control; A's case remains largely intact, hence the slim margin.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: The hotel-agent exchange across A4.1, B4.3-B4.4, and the closings. A forced B to either grant an exemption routing same-sex bookings through a seamless agent (undermining equal standing) or redefine the compelling interest as booking directly (importing A's categorical principle). B's response — that the injury is disqualification by religious test and enforcing against the landlord is the only means that restores equal standing — held, but A's closing line about "a religious test administered so efficiently it never costs them a dollar" kept the pressure alive to the end. This exchange decided how close the final call would be.
- **Muse Wonder 1.0 (high)** (A = Qwen 4 Max (high) (CON); B = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (PRO)): PRO sustained a workable functional test: protect decisions an institution actually entrusts for teaching, worship, or religious formation, and enforce equal treatment for housing, hotel bookings, and ordinary service roles. CON's dilemma was sharp, but PRO answered that judges verify factual entrustment rather than grade theology. The hotel-agent exchange showed the core-function gate doing work beyond access-based tailoring, and PRO cleanly distinguished a press choosing its message from refusing to sell an existing title. CON proved some exemptions should fail, but not that the core/commercial boundary is redundant.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's Rebuttal 2 was decisive: the hotel-agent hypothetical distinguished categorical equal booking for a non-core function from narrow tailoring based only on access, while the press answer separated core editorial selection from discriminatory sale of existing books.

### Judge Notes

- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (CON); B = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO)): Side B gave the cleaner route to a vote: it tied exemptions to the justification for religious autonomy, showed the main commercial cases should be refused, and reframed Side A's alternative as a discretionary advance-licensing scheme rather than a readable rule. Side A's mutual-aid examples were genuinely sharp, particularly the symmetry between a funded ticket trainee and a funded lab trainee, and that exposed tension in B's "motive vs function" line. But A did not convert that into a stronger governing rule; its balancing test still relied on agency discretion, and B had already defused the advertising example as ordinary speech protection and forced A to supply ad hoc stopping points like the landlord answer. B won the rule-vs-renewable-licence comparison.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's rebuttal was most effective when it neutralized A's ticket-advertising example as an ordinary speech burden rather than a religious exemption and pressed A to specify who grants its advance exemption and where its test stops. That shifted the clash from whether a discriminator's intent matters to whether the rule can be known in advance, where B held the more persuasive ground.
- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO); B = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (CON)): Both sides were strong, but B edged the decisive clash. B's trainee symmetry — a garage trainee collecting tickets versus a congregation-funded lab trainee, both additional places created for mutual aid — exposed an instability in A's function test: A exempted the garage by pointing to the community-level purpose ("alms by another route") but excluded the hospital trainee by pointing to role-level duties ("simply a hire"), then in closing could only say "if the agency can distinguish" — conceding the stated test does not do the distinguishing work. B bit the bullet coherently (both qualify; no displacement; advance notice) and supplied concrete answers to A's pressure: a public agency with named-practice, expiry-dated advance permissions (A1) and a principled floor refusing the landlord via no status-based denial of publicly offered housing (A2). A landed real hits — neutralizing B's ticket-advertisement example as ordinary speech law, extracting the "defined exemption" vagueness, and framing B's refusals as function judgments — but B's closing rebuttal that scrutinizing activity is not the same as requiring "core religious function" blunted the concession claim, and B's advance-notice scheme answered A's ex ante certainty argument. A's sharper rhetoric kept it close; B's more consistent line under sustained pressure decides it.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B4.4–B4.7 and B5.1–B5.3: B placed the garage trainee and the congregation-funded lab trainee side by side, showing A had classified one as charity and the other as ordinary hiring on grounds (worksite, institutional context) that A's own function test cannot supply — the contradiction A never cleanly resolved.
- **Muse Wonder 1.0 (high)** (A = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (CON); B = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO)): Side B framed a clear rule protecting ordinary workers, but Side A showed a narrower alternative not dependent on ranking an activity as a core religious function: advance, specific, time-limited permission where the place is genuinely additional and no incumbent is displaced.
