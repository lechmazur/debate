### Judge Notes

- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) (CON)): PRO (A) won by defending a qualified cross-recognition model that genuinely satisfies the motion's "more than currently do" while answering due-process concerns head-on, and by exposing CON's false dichotomy between rubber-stamping everything and recognizing nothing. CON's strongest line — that PRO's procedural safeguards gut the practical impact at scale — landed, but never neutralized PRO's closing weighing: CON told platforms when to stop, never why to stop one case earlier at a ban they can verify. CON also dodged PRO's Q2 pressure (answering a cross-service harassment scenario with a same-service blocking remedy), while PRO answered both pressure questions directly, honestly conceding residual error risk. CON's rhetoric was sharper ("digital exile," "corporate cartel," "concierge court"), but relied on overconfident specifics (Sony/Valve behavior, "millions of disciplinary actions each month") and partially strawmanned the motion's concession clause as requiring weakened due process.

CON's Rebuttal 2 counter (boutique justice cannot scale) was forceful but conceded the verifiable cases rather than answering why those bans should stay home.
- **Muse Wonder 1.0 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) (CON); B = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (PRO)): PRO built the cleaner live route: recognize only well-supported, reliably linked, proportionate bans, with receiving-platform review and reversals traveling too, while honestly accepting residual error costs. CON never defeated the checkable case, conceded the admission-and-recording example, and its blocking alternative did not solve identifying a harasser before a renewed threat on a new service.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's Rebuttal 2 was decisive: it answered automation and incentives directly, distinguished routing from banning, and showed CON's choice between rubber-stamping everything and recognizing nothing ignores serious cases worth reviewing.
- **Qwen 3.8 Max 0902** (A = GPT-6.2 Astra (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) (CON)): A won by defending a workable, limited cross-ecosystem recognition model and by answering due-process and scale pressure without overclaiming.
