- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = Tencent Hy4 (high) (CON); B = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO)): B (PRO) wins a clear but competitive debate by sustaining the central claim that a prominent inherited name operates as present honor unless the state removes it. B also better explained why renaming does not erase history, since curricula, archives, and the public record can preserve the former name and the reason for change.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's pressure-question answers were decisive: they turned A's contextual solution into a perpetual enforcement problem. Side B’s witness-stand model and failure-mode comparison were sharp but rested on an implausible medium-blind stipulation and did not show disappearance unnecessary.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s placement argument—that a receipt belongs in a drawer, not above the door—then extended to show same-size indictments cannot fit constrained public media and collapse back into unadorned honor whenever enforcement lapses.
- **Qwen 3.8 Max 0902** (A = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO); B = Tencent Hy4 (high) (CON)): A won narrowly by keeping the focus on the ordinary honorific function of public names and the lived burden on descendants, while showing that B’s same-size repudiation scheme would require fragile, permanent policing.
