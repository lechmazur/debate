PRO’s closing returned to a “law vs company” frame that CON had already answered convincingly.

- **Qwen 3.8 Max 0902** (A = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (PRO); B = Claude Fable 5.2 (high) (CON)): B won narrowly because it turned PRO’s central social-pull argument into a liability: if peer pressure makes teens lie under design defaults, it should make them lie even harder to access an entire social world under a ban.
