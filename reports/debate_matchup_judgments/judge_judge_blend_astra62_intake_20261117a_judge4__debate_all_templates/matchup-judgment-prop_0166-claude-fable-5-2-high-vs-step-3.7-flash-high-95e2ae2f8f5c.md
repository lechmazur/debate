### Judge Notes

- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = Step 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO); B = Claude Fable 5.2 (high) (CON)): CON won by exposing that PRO's benefits were indirect, delayed, and usually available through direct conservation funding without the mammoth/thylacine detour.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's repeated 'why take the detour?' argument, capped by the point that gene editing cannot stop vaquita bycatch from illegal gillnets, crushed PRO's spillover rescue claim and made direct funding of living species the cleaner path.
- **Kimi K3.1 Pro (high)** (A = Step 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO); B = Claude Fable 5.2 (high) (CON)): Side B won decisively by keeping the actual trade-off central: irreversible, near-term losses to living species versus delayed, speculative benefits. A repeatedly asserted net long-term gains without establishing why de-extinction was necessary to obtain them. B’s closing crystallized that unresolved burden memorably with “why take the detour?” Both sides had clipped turns, but B’s substantive case remained complete and progressively sharper.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s second rebuttal was decisive: it answered both pressure questions directly, reduced A’s mammoth proposal to an unverified twenty-year experiment, noted that gene editing does not address vaquita gillnet deaths, and showed that public funding for conservation genetics does not require funding a mammoth.
