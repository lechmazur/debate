CON's opening raised plausible concerns, including delay, evasion and fiscal burden. However, its first rebuttal contradicted itself by calling the ban symbolic while also predicting wipers because no ransom would be paid. PRO's pressure questions exposed that contradiction, and CON's answers then conceded the core mechanism. CON's second rebuttal and closing went further and argued affirmatively for the ban, saying revenue shrinks, the financing loop breaks and attacks collapse. That effectively abandoned the CON side. PRO answered CON's questions directly with A1/A2 and crystallized the concession in its closing. The result is a near-blowout.

PRO's closing then turned that concession into the decisive reason to vote PRO: permission to pay adds nothing to the victim's recovery and only adds revenue to the attacker.
