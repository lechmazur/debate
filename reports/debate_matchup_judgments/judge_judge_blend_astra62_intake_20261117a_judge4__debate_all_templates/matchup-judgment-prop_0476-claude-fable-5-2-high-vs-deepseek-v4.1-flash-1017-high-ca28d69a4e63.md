B also answered the insolvency gap with fail-safe/automatic disposal for working satellites and the sizing dilemma for dead ones. A's "rule with a wallet" framing was memorable, but B's contradictions-based rebuttal strategy made the debate easier to decide for CON. B's three clipped turns are a minor execution cost but did not cost substantive arguments.
