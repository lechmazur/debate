- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Kimi K3.1 Pro (high) (PRO); B = Kimi K3 (CON)): Side A won by maintaining the crucial distinction between preserving history and continuing civic honor. It directly limited the motion to defining legacies, classified Churchill consistently, and showed why plaques or counter-monuments do not necessarily neutralize an honored pedestal.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s strongest response was that unlawful topplings of Lincoln or Grant do not prove a deliberative “primarily associated” standard unworkable.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's A1/A2 answers and closings turning A's "vote to retain does not change what should happen" against A's opening "ongoing choice"/endorsement claim, paired with the Churchill classification as adopting deed-not-whole-man.
