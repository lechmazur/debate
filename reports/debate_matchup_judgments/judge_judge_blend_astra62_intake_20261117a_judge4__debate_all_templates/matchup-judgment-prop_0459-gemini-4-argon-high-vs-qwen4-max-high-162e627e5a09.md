### Judge Notes

- **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high)** (A = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (CON)): B wins on the cleaner comparative case. The decisive live issue was timing and cost: B showed that clean tech is largely a durable stock, so cheap imports bank decarbonization now, while localization creates a self-inflicted bottleneck before domestic capacity exists. A's insurance case was rhetorically strong and invoked real chokepoint risk, but it never fully answered B's deployment-clock objection. A's shift to allied specialization also blunted the localization claim. B used A's own admission that export controls were narrow and reversible to argue the threat was less than a full embargo and that private offtake/diversification was already occurring.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's most decisive exchange was turning A's narrow/reversible licensing concession into evidence that a full supplier cutoff is self-destructive, then concluding that localization imposes present deployment losses to hedge against a proven bluff.
- **Grok 4.7 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (CON); B = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO)): B wins a close, high-level clash by making the vote easier: a second source has to exist before a cutoff, and observed post-signal diversification is late, panic-priced insurance rather than proof that insurance is unnecessary. A's stock-versus-flow case and cumulative-emissions worry stayed live and were the best reasons to vote no, but A overread narrow licensing as a proven bluff and treated allied specialization as abandoning a motion that never required autarky. B answered the sharper pressure more directly and weighed a modest early premium against a plan that works only if the monopoly stays restrained.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's rebuttal that continued installations during graphite and gallium controls were a warning shot, not survival of a cutoff, and that private scrambling for non-Chinese supply borrows the very second source B wants funded before the crisis.
- **Muse Wonder 1.0 (high)** (A = Qwen 4 Max (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (CON)): Side B made the cleaner comparison: localization imposes a certain deployment and emissions cost now, years before new plants open, to hedge a weaker conditional risk because installed clean tech keeps running and exporters harm themselves by cutting supply. B also answered the financing and substitution pressure by arguing diversification and new chemistries can proceed in parallel without throttling current imports, and effectively exposed A's shift from national localization to allied specialization as diluting the motion.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's Rebuttal 2 turned A's own admissions—that graphite controls were narrow and reversible and that buyers privately contracted non-Chinese supply—into proof that real embargoes are self-destructive and market diversification occurs without domestic-content walls.
