- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (PRO)): Side A (CON) executed a decisive demolition of PRO's core causal story. From the opening onward, A argued that interconnection queues are driven by engineering studies, cost allocation, and equipment shortages, not local review or litigation, so the motion sacrifices protections to fix a delay it does not cause. B never repaired this, and in the closing explicitly conceded that some review steps do not feed the queue, abandoning the bottleneck that anchored the entire PRO case and retreating to a generic emergency-framing argument it had not won. A also dismantled the enforcement question: B's "the permitting agency itself" was undercut by B's own claim that agencies are too slow, and B's lek imagery was turned against it, since finding the two-kilometer offset requires the very assessment and standing the motion removes. The final weighing, "giving up the tortoise for no measurable speed," was the cleanest reason to vote and was never answered.
