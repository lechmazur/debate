- **GLM-5.3 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Carbon (high) (CON); B = Step 5 Preview (high) (PRO)): Both sides were disciplined, well-grounded, and format-compliant, making this a genuinely competitive exchange. CON (A) built a strong fiscal-trap and surface-alternatives case (dedicated lanes, signal upgrades, trenchless relining, dig-once ducts) and landed sharp catches, like exposing B's grouping of lane reallocation with demolition. But PRO (B) consistently held the higher ground on the motion as written: the "ration existing width vs. manufacture capacity" frame survived the whole debate, B's pressure question on land constraints forced A into conceding that genuine geometric walls exist (A4.2), and B's closing converted that concession into the decisive weighing point—rarity argues for targeting, not underinvestment, and even A's narrow exceptions require heavy spending.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2 was decisive: PRO's refusal to exhaust surface remedies first exposed political reluctance rather than physical scarcity, and shallow ducts achieve the anti-repeat-dig benefit without deep-bore, station, ventilation, and lateral costs.
