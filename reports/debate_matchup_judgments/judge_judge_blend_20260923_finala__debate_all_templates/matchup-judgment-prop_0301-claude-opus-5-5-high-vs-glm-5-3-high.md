### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = GLM-5.3 (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON)): A won by defending the ordinary distinction between comparable and identical standards. B’s own preferred systems use state curricula, inspections, and funding conditions, supporting A’s principle while leaving B mainly disputing where exemptions should fall. A also exposed important omissions in B’s limited non-discrimination floor, especially disability and family status. B raised serious concerns about doctrinal autonomy and vague civic requirements, but repeatedly treated comparability as public-school sameness and never fully established that tailored religious exceptions are incompatible with standards of comparable strength. Both incurred clipping costs, with B clipped more often and also using two nonexistent reference labels in the closing.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s strongest exchange was turning B’s international examples back against him: national curricula and common inspections showed that publicly funded faith schools can retain religious identity while meeting strong public-comparable standards. B’s reply about faith-based exemptions created pressure, but it did not defeat A’s strength-not-sameness interpretation.

PRO scored on mutual dependence on state-written standards and the race/religion asymmetry, but never neutralized CON's capture-in-kind point: civic/affirmation content auto-tracks the public syllabus in a way literacy floors do not. Definitional consistency and cleaner comparative weighing decided it.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's structural trap (Rebuttal 1 into Rebuttal 2): once PRO allows preference for co-religionists and faith requirements for worship roles, the standard is no longer comparable to public schools—it is tailored—so PRO either guts the mission or rewrites the motion.

Furthermore, Side B brilliantly exposed the contradiction in Side A's non-discrimination stance: Side A praised England's faith-based admissions while simultaneously arguing that no child should be rejected "because of who they are."
