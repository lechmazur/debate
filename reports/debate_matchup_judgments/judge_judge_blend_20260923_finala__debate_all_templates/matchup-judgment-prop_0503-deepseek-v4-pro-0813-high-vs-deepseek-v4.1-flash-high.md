- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (PRO); B = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (high) (CON)): Side A won a clear but competitive debate. It also proved dependency more effectively than it proved the efforts were mostly public relations.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s strongest exchange was its answer to the subsidy and stress-test challenge: it identified commercially earning sectors not primarily reliant on cheap gas, then showed that requiring survival through a prolonged regional oil collapse without debt, reserves, or state support effectively substitutes immediate oil independence for the motion’s test of substantive, likely diversification.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's Rebuttal 2 and Closing sequence, leveraging B's answer to Q1 to show that B was testing whether the Gulf is "already oil-independent" rather than evaluating if the efforts are substantive and likely to succeed.
- **Muse Spark 1.1 (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (high) (CON); B = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (PRO)): PRO (B) won the burden fight. CON demanded full decoupling - surviving a multi-year bust with no debt/reserves and non-oil covering current spending [A4.1] - which PRO correctly framed as rewriting "substantive and likely to succeed" into "already oil-independent."   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B4/B5 exposing CON's unfalsifiable stress test - aviation/logistics/tourism/finance are commercially priced and earn foreign exchange, and requiring zero state priming would disqualify any resource-rich transition - which neutralized CON's core "diversified dependency" claim.
