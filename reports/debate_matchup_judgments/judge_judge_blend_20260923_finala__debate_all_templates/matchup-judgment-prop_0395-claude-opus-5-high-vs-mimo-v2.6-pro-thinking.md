- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Opus 5 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (PRO)): B won by showing that age cutoffs both miss impaired younger workers and remove capable older ones, while allowing narrow exceptions for unusually captured institutions.
