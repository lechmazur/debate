### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (PRO); B = GLM-5.3 (high) (CON)): B won by showing that measurable accountability is not necessarily review quality. Its distinction between voluntarily chosen public criticism and assigned criticism of powerful scholars decisively undermined A's career-credit claim. A made a plausible case that payment could expand reviewer supply, but B showed that payment still relies on existing editorial quality judgments and may incentivize superficially compliant piecework. A also mischaracterized the pressure exchange in closing and had two clipped turns.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's response that anonymous suspicion remains deniable while a published signature creates a permanent searchable target was the strongest exchange. It directly answered A's narrow-field argument and exposed why public credit may not compensate vulnerable reviewers for retaliation risk.
- **Muse Spark 1.1 (high)** (A = GLM-5.3 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (PRO)): CON wins the decisive openness clash. PRO's asset story collapses when pressed to name a junior scholar rewarded for assigned signed critique of a power-holder and concedes it, while CON explains why suspicion-is-not-proof protects candor and why open review selects out the cautious, creating diplomatic mush before any report is written. Payment clash is roughly a wash, so the clear loss on candor means the bundled reform fails to work better.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's Rebuttal 2 answers on selection and accountability: open review self-selects for senior/secure reviewers, and accountability to editor targets quality whereas accountability to everyone targets palatability, plus the chosen-vs-imposed distinction.
- **Qwen 3.7 Max** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (PRO); B = GLM-5.3 (high) (CON)): Side B wins by effectively dismantling Side A's core mechanisms. On openness, B exposes the severe chilling effect on junior scholars, forcing A into a damaging concession that they could not name a single example of a junior scholar rewarded for a signed critique. On payment, B persuasively argues that A's proposed enforcement mechanisms merely replicate existing editorial judgment while introducing perverse incentives to game length and structure metrics. Furthermore, Side A suffered notable execution costs by having two substantive speeches clipped for exceeding the word limit, truncating their arguments. Side B's closing frame contrasting "countability" with "quality" crystallized the debate decisively in their favor.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's Rebuttal 2 and Closing, where they capitalized on Side A's concession regarding the lack of evidence for junior scholars benefiting from signed critiques, and effectively framed PRO's payment mechanisms as redundant metrics that incentivize superficial "palatability" over actual scientific candor.
