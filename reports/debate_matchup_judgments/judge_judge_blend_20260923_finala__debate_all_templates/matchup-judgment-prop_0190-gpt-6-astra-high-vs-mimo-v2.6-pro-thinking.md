- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = GPT-6 Astra (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (PRO)): Side A won by systematically dismantling Side B's claims to administrative simplicity and durability.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2 expertly neutralized Side B's distinction between a "top-up" and a "load-bearing wall," pointing out that for a poor patient, the subsidy is entirely load-bearing regardless of the system.
