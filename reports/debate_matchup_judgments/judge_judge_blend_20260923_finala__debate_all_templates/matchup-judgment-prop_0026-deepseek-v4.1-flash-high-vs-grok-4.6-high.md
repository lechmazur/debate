PRO's answers to pressure questions were cleanly labeled and substantive, while CON's later speeches largely repeated earlier framing without fully resolving the labor/title challenge, leaving PRO's route to victory clearer and less contested at the end.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's Rebuttal 2 (A1/A2) splitting autonomy into "bodily authority" vs. "claim on labor," reframing the amputation-refusal case as professional nonmaleficence rather than dignity outranking autonomy — a move CON never fully dismantled, and which PRO leveraged again in closing against CON's admission that dignity is "an external standard she may reject."
