### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON); B = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (high) (PRO)): Side B won by showing that compensation alone leaves compulsory availability intact, while a protected default makes refusal safer for workers with little bargaining power.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's closing flip of Side A's "receipt" metaphor: pointing out that silencing the receipt does not cancel the debt (the 9 a.m. deliverable), sharply crystallizing the displaced pressure argument.
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON); B = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (high) (PRO)): PRO (Side B) built the stronger path by showing a legal default of protected rest forces employers to internalize costs rather than merely displacing pressure. The "ping is the receipt" crystallization and the rebuttal showing the missed deadline becomes the employer's problem were material damages to CON's route.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's second rebuttal answer that when a deliverable requires after-hours labor, the protected worker may refuse to monitor and the missed deadline lands on the employer, directly neutralizing CON's core argument that the right merely displaces coercion to implicit metrics.
