- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (CON); B = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (PRO)): PRO won a close, high-quality clash by keeping the motion as written in view and forcing the clean choice: both sides eventually treat stranding (“no cars available”) as illegitimate, so the live question is whether reliability is a license condition on public streets or a purchase the city must keep funding. CON over-read the motion as strict density parity and under-answered why a contractable reliability duty becomes “unwritable” as a permit condition.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO’s pressure Q1 equating CON’s own “contracted rides / pay to show up” remedy with a permit reliability duty; CON’s later ratio-vs-purchase distinction never fully recovered the concession.
- **Muse Spark 1.1 (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (PRO); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (CON)): CON wins the cost-incidence clash that PRO staked its case on. PRO’s live route was that idle overnight capacity makes a permit floor nearly free.
