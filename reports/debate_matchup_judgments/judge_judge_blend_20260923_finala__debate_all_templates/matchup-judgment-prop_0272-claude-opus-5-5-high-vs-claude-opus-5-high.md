  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's closing argument distinguishing a 12-person varsity roster from true after-school availability (open gyms, intramurals) perfectly neutralized Side A's claim that typical schools already have sports covered, exposing a major flaw in Side A's ordinary district argument.
