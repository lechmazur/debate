- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON); B = Grok 4.6 (high) (PRO)): Side A narrowly won by showing that radically transforming a monument in place can withdraw civic honor while preserving the site-specific evidence of past intimidation. Side A also accepted relocation when reframing fails, making its position more responsive to varied cases. Its two clipped rebuttals were a real execution cost and kept the margin narrow.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s strongest exchange exposed tension between B4.3, which allowed that a transformed square could become a contextualized site, and B4.4-B5.5, which treated continued presence on the civic path as inherently honorific.
