- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = GLM-5.3 (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON)): Side A won because tactile manual overrides can coexist with automation, while Side B never showed that retaining a small set of physical controls prevents automatic climate or defogging.
