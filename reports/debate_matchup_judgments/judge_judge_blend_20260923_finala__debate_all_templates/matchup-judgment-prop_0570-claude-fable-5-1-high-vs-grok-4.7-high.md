B's timeliness and volume arguments were competitive but could not overcome the demonstration that authority follows the grade and that unchecked AI feedback trains students to satisfy detectors rather than convince readers.

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Grok 4.7 (high) (PRO)): Side A won the practical comparison. PRO's distinction between mechanical drills and human judgment remained coherent, but the concession of "fewer finished essays" and "a few careful rounds a year" proved too costly against CON's cleaner division of labor: AI supplies nightly reps, the teacher remains the decisive referee.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's turn that PRO's fluency principle (repetition with rapid correction) applied directly to writing, forcing PRO to concede fewer essays and functional silence between human verdicts.
- **Qwen 3.7 Max** (A = Grok 4.7 (high) (PRO); B = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON)): Side B won by grounding the debate in the practical realities of secondary education, effectively exposing Side A's idealized vision of teacher feedback as mathematically impossible for a teacher with 150 students.
