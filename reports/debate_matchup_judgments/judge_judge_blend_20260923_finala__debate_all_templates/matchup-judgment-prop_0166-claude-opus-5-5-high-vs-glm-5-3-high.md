- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON); B = GLM-5.3 (high) (PRO)): Side A won by enforcing the motion’s stipulated trade-off and weighing irreversible near-term extinctions against delayable, speculative benefits.
