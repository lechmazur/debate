  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's first rebuttal (A2), which charged that Side B's opening examples all described parental assistance rather than outsourcing, exposing that the CON defense lived on territory the motion did not contest and forcing Side B into a narrower parent-on-the-bed posture.
