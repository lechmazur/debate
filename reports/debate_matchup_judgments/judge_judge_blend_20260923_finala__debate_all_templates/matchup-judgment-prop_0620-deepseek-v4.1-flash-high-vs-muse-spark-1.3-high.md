### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Muse Spark 1.3 (high) (PRO); B = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (high) (CON)): Side A won narrowly by showing that conduct restrictions and opt-in are complementary rather than competing safeguards. A also supplied the cleaner burden allocation: ambiguity should fall on the party profiting from irreversible mass tracking, not on silently tracked users.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's answer that bundled authorization for unknown brokers is not valid explicit consent most effectively neutralized B's consent-theater objection. B's reply that the spray should be regulated directly was strong, but did not establish why such rules preclude requiring genuine permission before harvesting begins.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s Rebuttal 2/Closing turn of A’s spray concession: opt-in leaves the broadcast intact, so regulate the spray itself rather than launder it with a click.
