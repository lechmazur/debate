The calendar win on new-build wasn't enough to outweigh PRO's system-level per-dollar comparison.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Closing A5.1-A5.3: turns CON's clean-backfill concession into a wash argument — renewables replacing clean nuclear is zero net, so extension frees renewables to cut fossil, salvaging the avoided-emissions metric.
