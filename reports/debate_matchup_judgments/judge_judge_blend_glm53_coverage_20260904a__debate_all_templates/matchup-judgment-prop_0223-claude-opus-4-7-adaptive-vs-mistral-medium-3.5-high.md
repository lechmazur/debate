- **Grok 4.3** (A = Claude Opus 4.7 (high) (CON); B = Mistral Medium 3.5 (high) (PRO)): Side A (CON) wins by consistently narrowing to the motion's core: cameras structurally miss impairment/restraint/reckless behaviors that drive most fatalities, while police can target them.
