- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Qwen 3.8 Max (CON); B = Claude Opus 5 (high) (PRO)): Side A won a clear but competitive debate by showing that Side B’s privacy concerns justify strict controls, not the motion’s categorical ban.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's closing effectively turned Side A's admission that notice is 'unnecessary' for probabilistic leads into a devastating critique, pointing out that a safeguard only the guilty can invoke is not a safeguard at all.
