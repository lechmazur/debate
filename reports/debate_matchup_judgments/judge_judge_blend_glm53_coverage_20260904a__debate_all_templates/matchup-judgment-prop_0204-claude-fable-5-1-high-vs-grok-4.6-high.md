- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Grok 4.6 (high) (PRO)): Side A won by comparing crisis-ready production against Side B’s preferred decade of idle rock and by showing that Side B’s proposed disclosure, offtake, and personnel safeguards were precisely the tools available under targeted review. Side A’s clipped second rebuttal and some tension over partial disclosure narrowed the margin.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s second rebuttal turned Side B’s concrete rebuttal conditions—audited financing, allied offtake, and personnel restrictions—into evidence that mitigation can neutralize the risk without a blanket adverse default, then weighed Side B’s accepted decade of nondevelopment against its own crisis-command metric.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's second rebuttal was highly decisive, brilliantly weaponizing Side A's answers to the pressure questions to prove that Side A's required "showings" were identical to case-by-case mitigation.
