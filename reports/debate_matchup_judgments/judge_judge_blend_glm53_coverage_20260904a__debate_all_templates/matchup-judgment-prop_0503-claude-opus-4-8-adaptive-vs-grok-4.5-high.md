### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Grok 4.5 (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 4.8 (high) (CON)): Side B won by consistently separating substantive expenditure from evidence that diversification will become self-sustaining. Side A showed real investment and plausibly exposed B's overnight-independence test as too strict, but relied heavily on Dubai, operating assets, and sunk costs without adequately proving that the wider Gulf portfolio can thrive without continuing oil support. B's fiscal-breakeven claims were asserted too confidently and its exact threshold was arbitrary, which keeps the debate competitive, but its outcome-versus-activity distinction remained decisive.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's strongest exchange was its answer that sunk capital proves effort, not likely success, followed by the point that pruning projects only when they threaten sovereign finances is damage control rather than reliable market discipline. Side A's incentives and irreversibility framing did not neutralize that distinction.
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Claude Opus 4.8 (high) (CON); B = Grok 4.5 (high) (PRO)): Side A established fiscal breakeven as the decisive metric: after two decades of vision plans, the flat dependence trajectory shows oil still balances the books. Side A also successfully framed Dubai as survivorship bias driven by unique geography, undermining the regional template claim.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2 (A4), where it named a falsifying breakeven threshold Side B could not meet, showed Side B had no concrete evidence of trajectory, and exposed Side B's attempt to replace "likely to succeed" with "hard to reverse."

A argued sunk costs and Dubai prove oil seed capital can graduate; B answered that one graduate is survivorship bias, that VAT/taxing oil-funded consumption is a costume, and that fiscal breakeven flat for 20 years is the one test that nets oil out. A never named a second economy that survives without oil and sidestepped B's Q1, while B named a clear falsifier ($40 breakeven through a trough).   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B R1/R2 flat-slope breakeven + costume + survivorship bias: turned PRO's sunk-capital evidence into proof of dependence and forced PRO to concede no second Dubai-scale graduate exists.
