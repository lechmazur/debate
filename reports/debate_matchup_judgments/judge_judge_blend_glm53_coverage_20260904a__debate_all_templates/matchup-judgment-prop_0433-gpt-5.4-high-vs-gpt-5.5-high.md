- **Qwen 3.6 Max Preview** (A = GPT-5.5 (high) (CON); B = GPT-5.4 (high) (PRO)): PRO wins by successfully framing the debate around information asymmetry and the practical impossibility of tenants discovering chronic, time-sensitive building issues through daytime tours or broad questions.
