- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Tencent Hy3 Preview (high) (PRO); B = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (CON)): B won by making the mechanism itself the center of the debate and repeatedly showing that A relied on undefined assurances: reasonable royalties, affordable prices, and narrow essentiality.
