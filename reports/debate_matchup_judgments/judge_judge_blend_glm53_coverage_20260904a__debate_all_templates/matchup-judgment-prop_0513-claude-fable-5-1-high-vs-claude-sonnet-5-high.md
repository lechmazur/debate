- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Claude Sonnet 5 (high) (PRO)): Side B won by showing that labor markets can be screened with analogous substitution tests and that alternative labor remedies cannot prevent merger-created structural scarcity.
