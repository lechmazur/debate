  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s A4.1 was decisive: it identified the forgone spending as the next linear channel or broadcast relaunch, not the existing destination. This undercut Side B’s central claim that prioritizing the on-ramp necessarily leaves nowhere for audiences to go.

CON’s sign-before-room attack is sharp but overextends "channel expansion" to include all owned distribution and treats platform and creator risks as fatal without showing that traditional channel expansion actually reaches young audiences.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON’s [B2.1]–[B2.2] turn that PRO’s own framing makes the clip an advertisement and the channel the product, which forced PRO to clarify what "rather than" actually cuts and exposed the central tension of the debate.
