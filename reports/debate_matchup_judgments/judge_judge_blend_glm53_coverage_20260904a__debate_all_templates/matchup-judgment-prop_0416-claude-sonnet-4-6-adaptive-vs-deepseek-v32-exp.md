- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = DeepSeek V3.2 (PRO); B = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (CON)): B won by making the comparison clearer: A showed physical programs are valuable, but B repeatedly pressed that this does not prove they are stronger than literacy campaigns for persistent digital harms.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s strongest exchange was the claim that A’s case proved complementarity rather than superiority, paired with the point that digital literacy is also preventive because it directly addresses manipulation, radicalization, and online hostility where they occur.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = DeepSeek V3.2 (PRO); B = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (CON)): Side B won by successfully challenging Side A's core assumptions. Side A dropped the latter point entirely. Side B also brilliantly co-opted Side A's "drain the swamp" metaphor by observing that the digital swamp cannot actually be drained, making swimming lessons (literacy) an inescapable foundation.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's distinction between incidental classroom exclusion and structural physical exclusion in Rebuttal 2 neatly neutralized Side A's pressure question while reinforcing B's access argument.
- **Grok 4.3** (A = DeepSeek V3.2 (PRO); B = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (CON)): Side B wins by forcing the comparative ranking the motion requires and showing that Side A repeatedly described mutual dependence rather than superiority; B also neutralized access and exclusion rebuttals while A never closed the "stronger" burden.
