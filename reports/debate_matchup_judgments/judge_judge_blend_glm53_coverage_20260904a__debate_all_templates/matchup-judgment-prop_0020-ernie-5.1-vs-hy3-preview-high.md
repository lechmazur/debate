- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (PRO); B = Tencent Hy3 Preview (high) (CON)): B wins because it more effectively attacked A's counterfactual: A never fully proved that a euro-less EU would keep single-market benefits while avoiding currency volatility, competitive devaluation, and weaker geopolitical scale. B's own claims sometimes overstated the euro as the only anchor of EU unity, but its comparative weighing was cleaner and its final speeches made the decision easier.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's most decisive exchange was pressing A on how devaluations would avoid currency wars, then using A's own reliance on EU coordination and the Stability and Growth Pact to argue that synchronized currency management was less credible than the euro itself.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (PRO); B = Tencent Hy3 Preview (high) (CON)): Side B wins by systematically dismantling Side A's counterfactual. Side A argued the EU could abandon the euro and avoid currency wars by coordinating devaluations, using the Stability and Growth Pact as a precedent. Side B countered that the EU already struggles with basic fiscal coordination, making synchronized currency adjustments improbable. In a fatal execution error during the closing, Side A lost track of the flow, falsely attributed the SGP coordination claim to Side B, and attacked it—effectively conceding that their own mechanism for coordinated devaluations would fail. Side B cleanly capitalized on this contradiction to secure the win.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's Rebuttal 2 response to the UK comparison. Side A asked why the UK traded smoothly with the EU without the euro; Side B sharply noted that the UK has a large, liquid currency with manageable hedging costs, unlike the highly volatile, illiquid national currencies smaller EU states would face.
- **Grok 4.3** (A = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (PRO); B = Tencent Hy3 Preview (high) (CON)): Side B wins: it directly neutralized A's coordination-devaluation claim with A's own Stability and Growth Pact concession, preserved a cleaner counterfactual on supply-chain fragility versus euro rigidities, and consistently tied responses back to live clashes without evasion.

- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Tencent Hy3 Preview (high) (PRO); B = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (CON)): B wins a close but clear debate by giving the broader net-strength comparison: trade integration, reserve-currency influence, ECB backstops, and collective crisis capacity.
