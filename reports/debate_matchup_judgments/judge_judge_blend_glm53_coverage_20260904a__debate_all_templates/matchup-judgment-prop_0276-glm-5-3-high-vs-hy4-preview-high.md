- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Tencent Hy4 Preview (high) (PRO); B = GLM-5.3 (high) (CON)): B won by making the decisive clash clearer: automatic payout without trustworthy attribution relocates the fight rather than ending it.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s pay-first reply: A’s own “presumptive + verified + audit + clawback” stack concedes untrustworthy airline reason codes and recreates a heavier claims regime funded by fares.
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = GLM-5.3 (high) (CON); B = Tencent Hy4 Preview (high) (PRO)): PRO successfully established that flipping the burden from passenger to airline—paying first and requiring the airline to prove exclusion—meaningfully reduces the complaint-heavy process the motion targets.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's demonstration that PRO's "automatic" scheme collapsed into a heavy back-end of verification, audits, and clawbacks was sharply executed, but PRO's response that this complexity polices the airline while suppressing the passenger-facing front end was the decisive answer that preserved PRO's route to victory.
