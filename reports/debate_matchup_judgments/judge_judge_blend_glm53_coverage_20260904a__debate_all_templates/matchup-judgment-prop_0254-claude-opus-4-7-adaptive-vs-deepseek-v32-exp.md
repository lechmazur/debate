- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = DeepSeek V3.2 (PRO); B = Claude Opus 4.7 (high) (CON)): B won by making the key practical burdens clearer: a well-being headline is politically malleable, less comparable, and especially risky for poorer countries where growth funds basic welfare.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's decisive exchange was against A's dashboard criticism: B argued that GDP need not moralize every outcome because environmental law, taxes, and targeted indicators address harms, while a composite well-being headline can hide unemployment or be reweighted by incumbents.
