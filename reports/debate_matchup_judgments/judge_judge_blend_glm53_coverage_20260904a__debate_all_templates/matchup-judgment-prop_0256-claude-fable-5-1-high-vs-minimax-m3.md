- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = MiniMax-M3 (PRO); B = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON)): Side B won by showing that planned refuge cities do not reliably replace coastal spending: essential coastal economies still require protection, while voluntary greenfield cities risk becoming an additional speculative bill. A answered the feasibility pressure with phasing and public anchors, but its own examples largely depended on established metropolitan economies or compelled agency relocation, and its late claim that the plan was merely additive weakened the motion's rather-than comparison.

Side A also brilliantly neutralized Side B's examples of successful planned cities by pointing out they were either suburbs of existing metros or relied on the very government coercion Side B had disavowed.
