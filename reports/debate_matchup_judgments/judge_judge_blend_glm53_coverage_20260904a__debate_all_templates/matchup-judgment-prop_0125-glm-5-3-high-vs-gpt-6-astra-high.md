Pressure handling favored B.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Rebuttal 2 A1/A2 plus B4.4 capital vs operating: B shows vouchers sufficient to fund construction are themselves expansion subsidies, and technology-neutral bidding includes subsidized wireless, not only places wireless cannot serve, leaving CON without a supply solution.
