CON overplayed the "abandonment" charge and never fully solved the live receiving-end problem, leaving PRO's core comparative intact.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's turn on Katrina in Rebuttal 1 and the Closing: PRO showed that the devastating scattering CON cited was not the alternative to planned relocation but the consequence of its absence, converting CON's strongest humanitarian evidence into a liability and proving that planning the arrival is the only humane option.
