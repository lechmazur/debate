Its preferred material remedies largely fit B's broader, more natural account of reparations.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's strongest exchange distinguished reparations from ordinary aid: an unconditional transfer justified by a specific historic extraction is reparative even when administered nationally or geographically. That answered Side A's World Bank analogy and weakened the claim that nation-level debt cancellation cannot count as a direct reparations program.

B's identification objection and definitional wall (nation ≠ descendant community; structural ≠ direct) were real and repeatedly pressed, especially via documentary asymmetry, but they never restored reliance on apology and struggled once A answered that overwhelmingly descendant populations make the place the community, and that loans/aid differ from unrepayable justice transfers. A's Pressure answers and closing crystallization (justified by the theft or not) left the cleaner voting path.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's R1 absorption of B1.6 (debt/land/institutions as the motion relabeled) plus R2 A1 distinguishing World Bank loans from Haiti-style justice transfers.
- **Muse Spark 1.1 (high)** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (CON); B = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (PRO)): PRO won the definitional and comparative clash. CON conceded apologies are insufficient and that material transfers are required, leaving only whether those transfers count as "direct reparations." PRO showed its opening list (trusts, land, debt cancellation) already encompassed CON's own proposals and answered the World Bank vs reparations challenge with principled distinctions (non-repayable, justified by wrong, unconditional), while CON never rehabilitated the apology or answered why imperfect identification justifies zero direct payment when place-based programs bypass lineage.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Rebuttal 1 and Rebuttal 2 where PRO reframes CON's structural reforms (debt cancellation, land reform) as the motion itself, and in A1 answers the World Bank loan objection with three distinctions, exposing that CON's alternative is reparations relabeled.

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (PRO)): B narrowly won because A largely conceded historically named, targeted material remedy while construing descendant-community reparations too narrowly as individual cash sorted by ancestry.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's strongest move was turning A's proposal to cancel debt explicitly because of past extraction into a concession that material, historically tied reparative action is owed.
