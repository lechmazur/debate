- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = Grok 4.6 (high) (PRO); B = Grok 4.5 (high) (CON)): Both sides built coherent, well-grounded cases, but A more effectively exploited the pivotal exchange in the Pressure Questions round. A also used the choir/band analogy consistently to humanize the claim that comparable alternatives needn't be mere props, and turned B's own concession that released time still rides the compulsory clock into an equity argument (the ban simply shifts sorting onto families with vans and nearby congregations), which B never squarely answered beyond asserting that curriculum/staff purity matters more.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B’s second rebuttal was decisive.
