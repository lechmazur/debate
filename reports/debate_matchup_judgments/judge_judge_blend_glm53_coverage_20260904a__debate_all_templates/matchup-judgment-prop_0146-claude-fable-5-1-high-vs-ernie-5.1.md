- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (PRO)): Side A won by exposing the tension between an unenforceable request and an enforceable action against a parent, then comparing source removal unfavorably with platform-level delisting and prevention.
