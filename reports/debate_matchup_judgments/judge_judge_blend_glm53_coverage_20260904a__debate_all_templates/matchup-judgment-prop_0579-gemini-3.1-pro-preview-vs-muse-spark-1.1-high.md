- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Muse Spark 1.1 (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (CON)): Side A won by offering a concrete privacy-preserving model and consistently comparing it with today’s static, widely copied identity data.
