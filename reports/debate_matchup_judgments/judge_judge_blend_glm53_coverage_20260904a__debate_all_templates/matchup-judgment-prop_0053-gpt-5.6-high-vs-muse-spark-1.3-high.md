Side A successfully grounded the prompt's "likely to include minors" standard in existing regulatory frameworks (like alcohol and gambling), neutralizing Side B's vagueness attacks. Crucially, Side A trapped Side B during the pressure and rebuttal phases by highlighting the impossibility of proving "deliberate child targeting" (Side B's preferred conduct-based standard) without analyzing audience demographics.
