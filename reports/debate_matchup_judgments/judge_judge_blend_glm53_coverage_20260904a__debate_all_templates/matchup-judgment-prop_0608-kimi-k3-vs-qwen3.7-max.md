- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Kimi K3 (PRO); B = Qwen 3.7 Max (CON)): Side A won by distinguishing regulation of offset quality from regulation of the truthfulness and incentives behind net-zero claims. Furthermore, A used PQ1 to force B into conceding that 100%-offset claims violate existing scientific frameworks, allowing A to brilliantly frame the debate in closing as a choice between B's failed voluntary standards and A's legally binding version of the exact same standard.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s Rebuttal 2 + closing on B’s concession that a zero-cut full-offset firm “isn’t achieving net-zero,” converting it into proof that the legal cap simply binds the standard B already accepted in theory.
