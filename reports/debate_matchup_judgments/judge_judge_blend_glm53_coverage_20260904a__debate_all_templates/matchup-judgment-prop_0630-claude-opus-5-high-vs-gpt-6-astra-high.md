- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Claude Opus 5 (high) (PRO); B = GPT-6 Astra (high) (CON)): B won the decisive clash on the motion as written: whether limited gene-edited crops specifically need streamlined rules distinct from traditional GMO regulation. A's late claim that B's published criteria simply are the motion, or require rewriting seed law, did not neutralize B's cleaner reason to reject the distinct pathway.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's repeated conversion of A's product-risk principle against A's technique-specific lane (esp. B1.5/B2.3 and B4.6/B5.3–4), after A conceded neutral waivers could remove the same obligations.

CON's direct answers to pressure questions and its crystallization of the false choice in closing gave it the cleaner route to victory.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's second rebuttal (Turn 8), where it directly answered PRO's pressure questions, rejected the "litigated waiver" framing, and showed that a technique-neutral system could remove redundant burdens without creating a privileged lane for gene editing.

- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = GPT-6 Astra (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5 (high) (CON)): B won a sharp, competitive debate by making the motion itself the decisive problem: a technique-labeled door that invites gaming, awards lighter pre-market review to the least detectable products, and shifts export/segregation losses onto farmers.
