CON effectively argued that modern building codes combined with ongoing grid decarbonization mean new homes will likely reach near-zero operational emissions without future retrofits, making PRO's "lock-in" and "retrofit queue" fears overstated. While PRO offered strong principled arguments on total cost of ownership and build-time efficiency, CON's comparative weighing on opportunity costs, marginal buyers, and the actual emissions trajectory of new stock provided a cleaner, more responsive path to victory.

CON sharply countered that higher build costs kill marginal starter homes and price out families who never qualify for a mortgage, trapping them in the inefficient existing stock.
