Its single most effective move—"CON is attacking scenarios our safeguards already forbid"—is a clean logical reframe that CON never fully escaped. Throughout the exchange PRO consistently returned to a proportionality argument: a blanket ban destroys associational freedom for the majority of genuinely voluntary communities just to close enforcement gaps at the margins. CON never supplied a direct, weighed answer to this. The debate's decisive live clash at close is proportionality: PRO's framework is imperfect but calibrated; CON's alternative would overcorrect in ways CON did not adequately justify.
