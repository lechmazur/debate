- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Tencent Hy3 Preview (high) (PRO); B = Grok 4.3 (CON)): B won by making the sequencing problem sharper: physical-first funding still leaves commuters, caregivers, chronically ill, and otherwise schedule-constrained students without meaningful access when they need low-stakes connection. B also blunted the cost argument by pointing out fixed overhead and underuse risks for physical spaces.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's rebuttal that the real barrier for many students is schedule and location, not the existence of an accessible lounge, most directly damaged A's universal-access framing and made digital expansion look necessary rather than merely supplementary.
