- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON); B = Muse Spark 1.1 (high) (PRO)): Side A won by making a clean, decisive distinction between "locked exits" (foreclosing access and using deceptive scare screens) and "pre-selected entries" (the out-of-box default experience). - **Qwen 3.7 Max** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON); B = Muse Spark 1.1 (high) (PRO)): Side A wins by drawing a decisive and highly persuasive distinction between "locked exits" (access denial, scare screens) and "pre-selected entries" (default settings).
