### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5 (high) (CON); B = Kimi K3 (PRO)): Side A won the central economic clash: outcomes contracts can limit payment for failures without necessarily lowering the price paid for successful, long-term treatment, while mandatory full-label coverage weakens exclusion leverage and greatly expands eligible volume. A overstated the link between insurer churn and treatment cessation, yet its broader distinction between targeted high-risk coverage and a full-label mandate remained intact.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s first rebuttal was decisive: it separated waste control from price control, arguing that refunds for nonresponders do not solve high prices for responders, then contrasted narrow high-risk coverage with the mandate’s much broader eligible population. PRO framed parity and stigma effectively and adapted on installments/population rebates, but never neutralized the leverage or savings contradictions, leaving CON with the clearer reason to reject the proposition as written.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON R2 (A4.5-A4.6) stacking PRO concessions on weight regain + churn + milestone stop-payment to kill the substitution story
