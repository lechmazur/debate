- **Grok 4.3** (A = Mistral Medium 3.5 (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (CON)): Side B won by turning Side A's DMA example against them to show behavioral rules can address issues without separation, while exposing that separation would break security and incentive alignment for consumers; Side A left this burden under-addressed despite strong initial examples.

A's closing crystallized the root-cure vs. symptom-treatment comparison more decisively, preserving a clearer path to victory on the motion's specific remedy.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: The exchange over the EU's DMA. B sharply noted the DMA is behavioral, not structural, attempting to turn A's own example into proof that separation is unnecessary. A parried by conceding the DMA's technical success refutes the "dumb device" fear, but pivoted to Apple's rapid evasion tactics to demonstrate why behavioral rules are chronically insufficient, thereby reinforcing the necessity of structural separation.
