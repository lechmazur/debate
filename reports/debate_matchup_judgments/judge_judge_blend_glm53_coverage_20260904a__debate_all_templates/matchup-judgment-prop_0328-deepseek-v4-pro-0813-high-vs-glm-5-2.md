- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON); B = GLM-5.2 (max) (PRO)): Side A won by showing that the proposition permits exceptions only for anti-scalping, leaving legitimate age, identity, fraud, and security-based transfer restrictions legally vulnerable.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s strongest exchange was its response to the gate-check argument: checking age or identity at entry does not eliminate the transfer, reissuance, dispute, or credential-management problems, while the motion supplies no clear non-scalping exception for those restrictions.

CON’s cleaner comparison—stranded-fan harm is better met by mandatory refund/disclosure than by forcing negotiability that fuels litigation and inventory for resellers—survived pressure better than PRO’s ownership default.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON’s age/ID transfer collision: gate checks do not neutralize an upstream transfer right limited only by anti-scalping carve-outs, so legitimate non-scalping controls become litigation targets.
