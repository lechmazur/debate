### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5 (high) (CON); B = Grok 4.6 (high) (PRO)): Side A won by isolating the proposition’s distinctive burden: a credible enforceable penalty. Side B’s transition funding helped answer the fairness concern but did not resolve this enforcement dilemma.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s A4.1-A4.5 was decisive: it answered both pressure questions directly, showed why coal-project finance restrictions miss sovereign and state-bank lending, and then contrasted that toothlessness with the collateral damage of broader trade penalties. Side B never resolved this contradiction, leaving Side A with the cleaner path to victory.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A4's A1 effectively neutralized B's "surgical" finance penalties by pointing out that Indian and Chinese coal is financed domestically by state banks, rendering export-credit bans irrelevant.
