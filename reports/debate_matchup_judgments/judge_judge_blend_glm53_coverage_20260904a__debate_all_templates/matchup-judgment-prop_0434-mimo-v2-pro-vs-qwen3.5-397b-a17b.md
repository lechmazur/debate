### Judge Notes

- **Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high)** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (PRO); B = Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (CON)): PRO built the cleaner path to victory across all five rounds. The core analytical move—that recordings capture actual audio content for an adjudicator's review, not merely a bare number, meaning human judgment of reasonableness still operates but now on verifiable facts rather than competing testimonies—was introduced in Rebuttal 1 and never squarely neutralized by CON. CON kept insisting that "a machine cannot distinguish between a party and a panic attack," but this characterization ignored PRO's consistent point that certified recordings play back the actual sound, not just a decibel spike, leaving the reasonableness determination to the adjudicator. CON's equity argument also backfired: CON alleged a "paywall" for certified equipment, but PRO credibly flipped this by noting the current system already favors those who can afford lawyers or prolonged litigation, while a shared-device certification regime lowers barriers. CON never seriously developed the "mediation" alternative it proposed, leaving it a bare assertion. CON's strongest move was the burden-shift framing—"the accused must explain away the objective number"—but PRO answered it by pointing out that certification actually constrains arbitrary landlord action rather than enabling it, and that adjudicators still apply a reasonableness standard. The A2 response in Rebuttal 2 was the most decisive single exchange: CON asked whether landlords would mechanically evict on metric alone, and PRO turned it cleanly—today landlords can evict on rumor or personal bias; certified evidence replaces that with a documented factual pattern. CON never recovered from that turn. CON's case was competent but repetitive, cycling through "machine vs. human judgment" and "home as sanctuary" without sharpening how they answered the live pressure PRO had created.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's A2 response to CON's pressure question about risk-averse landlords evicting mechanically on the metric. PRO flipped the concern: the status quo already allows arbitrary eviction based on unverified complaints or personal bias, whereas certified evidence requires a documented, objective pattern of violation before action is warranted. This directly neutralized what was CON's strongest live attack and exposed that CON's preferred baseline is actually less protective of tenants than the reform being proposed.

- **Grok 4.3** (A = Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (PRO)): Side B wins because its core route (certified recordings anchor rather than replace reasonableness judgment, democratize proof, and constrain arbitrary landlord action) was never neutralized; B directly answered pressure questions on access and burden reversal while CON left the chilling-effect and independence objections largely unrefuted after the exchange.

### Judge Notes

- **Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high)** (A = Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (PRO); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (CON)): PRO built a consistently stronger cumulative case across all rounds. The opening was more complete, preemptively addressing the privacy objection before CON could raise it. The core framing—that without objective data, victim complaints die in landlord inboxes and context can never reach a judge—was stated early, maintained, and directly pressed through the Q1 pressure question. CON's most interesting argument (that certified logs shift the burden to the accused and remove context from the analysis) was never fully developed, and CON's Rebuttal 2 answer to PRO's Q1 was the debate's clearest execution failure: saying context reaches judges through "existing channels" without explaining how the noise occurrence itself gets established without any evidence was a non-answer that PRO had already anticipated. PRO's comparative framing—"CON prefers a system where harm is guaranteed to avoid a system where abuse is merely possible"—was the clearest statement of the central weighing question and was left unanswered. CON raised legitimate concerns about landlord overreaction and chilling effects, but both were partially neutralized: PRO correctly noted that wrongful-eviction liability constrains landlords, and that certified thresholds far exceed the level of normal domestic life. CON also ran consistently short on word count, leaving real arguments underdeveloped. PRO's breathalyzer analogy in Rebuttal 1 was sharp and memorable. Both sides stayed epistemically disciplined without fake precision.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's response to CON's "conversation ender" claim and context defense: PRO argued that context is a defense raised after a violation is established, not a reason to blindfold the court, and pressed this through Q1 by asking how a victim's complaint ever reaches a judge at all without objective proof. CON's answer—that context flows through "existing channels"—conceded the core problem: without evidence that the noise occurred, there is no case for context to attach to. This exchange most clearly revealed the gap in CON's position.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (PRO); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (CON)): Side A won by successfully arguing that objective data is a prerequisite for the contextual dialogue Side B champions, not a replacement for it. Side A effectively handled the pressure questions by pointing to continuous logging and existing tenant laws, while Side B struggled to explain how victims can prove harm in the status quo without falling victim to the exact he-said-she-said dynamic Side A indicted.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's argument that "context is a defense raised after a violation is established" and the accompanying breathalyzer analogy effectively dismantled Side B's core argument against objective data.
- **Grok 4.3** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (CON); B = Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (PRO)): Side B wins because its core path—objective evidence rectifying power asymmetry and health harm—survives A's context and chilling rebuttals through consistent weighing and direct pressure responses, while A never neutralizes the proof gap under the status quo.
