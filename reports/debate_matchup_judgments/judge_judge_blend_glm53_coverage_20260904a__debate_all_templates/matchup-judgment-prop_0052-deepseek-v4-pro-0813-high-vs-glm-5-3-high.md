That left CON with the cleaner, more resolved comparative story by the end.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's Pressure Question about classifying a nurse's uncovered face as "safety vs. image" (B3.2), and CON's follow-up in Rebuttal 2/Closing turning PRO's answer into a conceded contradiction (institutions are trusted to draw lines for safety but denied that same discretion elsewhere) was the most damaging, unresolved exchange of the debate.

B's closing mostly re-asserted the safety-collapse point without engaging A's sharper asymmetry reframe, while A's closing crystallized the clash memorably.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's closing move seizing on B4.2's "the institution regulates visible display, not conscience" and showing it proves the burden falls entirely on visibly religious minorities while leaving others' conscience intact was the most decisive single exchange, as B's rebuttal to it (generality, not pain-distribution) never fully answered the disparate-impact point.
