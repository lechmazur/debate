### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (CON); B = Grok 4.20 0309 (Non-Reasoning) (PRO)): Side A won by making the access burden decisive: the most isolated residents cannot simply use late-hour libraries or recreation centers, while app-based tools can at least reach them directly. Cost was asserted on both sides, but Side A landed the cleaner comparative case.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's strongest exchange was turning Side B's answer about volunteers and outreach into a concession that physical spaces need a secondary network before they can help the homebound or severely isolated.
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Grok 4.20 0309 (Non-Reasoning) (PRO); B = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (CON)): PRO won narrowly because CON never fully resolved the bridge-to-nowhere problem: conceding apps are merely a temporary bridge to physical community while insisting cities prioritize the bridge over the destination undermined their call for greater investment.
