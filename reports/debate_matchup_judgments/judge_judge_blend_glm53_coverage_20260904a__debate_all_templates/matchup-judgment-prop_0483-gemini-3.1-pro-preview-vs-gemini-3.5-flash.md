Side B’s rebuttals directly answered the test-prep and complexity pressure, and its closing cleanly crystallized why discarding cognitive testing would entrench privilege rather than dismantle it. Side A’s responses were competent but failed to neutralize the fatal vulnerability of its alternatives.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B’s Rebuttal 2 sharply exposed that Side A’s work-sample alternatives measure prior training rather than latent potential, effectively flipping the equity clash and leaving Side A without a viable, fair sorting mechanism.
