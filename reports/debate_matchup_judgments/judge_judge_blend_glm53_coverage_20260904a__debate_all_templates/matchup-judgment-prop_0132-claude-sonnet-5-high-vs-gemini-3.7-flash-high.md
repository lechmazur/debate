- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Claude Sonnet 5 (high) (CON); B = Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO)): CON won by exposing two unresolved tensions in PRO's case. Side B effectively weaponized this contradiction, alongside a highly damaging and original distinction between algorithmic "ranking errors" (demotion) and "gate errors" (erasure from feeds). Side A failed to resolve this core clash in the final speeches, relying instead on repeating its opening premises, while Side B's closing sharply crystallized the unrebutted logical trap.
