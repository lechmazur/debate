- **Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high)** (A = Grok 4.20 0309 (Non-Reasoning) (PRO); B = DeepSeek V3.2 (CON)): Side A built and sustained the most decisive live argument across all five rounds: the incentive-structure case that without changing the default at the physical border, no amount of adjudication capacity prevents smuggler-coached claims, cartel profits, and eroded public trust that ultimately kills generous immigration policy. CON offered no credible mechanism for why this time would be different. PRO's closing crystallized the clash cleanly; CON's closing (138 words) left ground unaddressed. The opening clip cost PRO a minor format penalty but did not materially damage the substance. On balance, PRO's narrative held more live routes to victory after the exchange: the incentive structure argument, the catch-and-release-as-lopsided-bargain counter, and the sustainable-compassion framing all survived substantially intact.
