### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (PRO); B = Grok 4.5 (high) (CON)): Side A won a clear but competitive debate by defending a workable treatment-versus-enhancement boundary and better weighing genetic inequality, market convergence, and coercive competition. Side B made a strong liberty case, but its tourism and democratization response relied on speculative price competition and subsidies without showing why legalization would not first broaden an affluent genetic advantage. A incurred execution costs from three clipped turns, but its central case remained visible and responsive.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A most decisively answered the claim that health correlations erase the disease boundary: primary medical purpose distinguishes treating suffering from purchasing enhancement, while incidental correlations cannot make every preferred trait therapeutic. A then turned the tourism objection by comparing imperfect prohibition with broad domestic normalization and challenging B's unsupported promise of ordinary access.
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Grok 4.5 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (PRO)): CON won by showing that PRO never established the categorical moral break required to criminalize parental choice. CON persuasively argued that ranking existing viable embryos by polygenic scores is continuous with mate choice and existing IVF screening for viability and aneuploidy—merely better information, not the manufacture of a product. This deflated PRO's core "medicine versus curation" distinction. CON also successfully flipped PRO's inequality and diversity arguments, demonstrating that domestic bans would drive selection offshore to the wealthy while legalization creates pathways for broader access through scale and regulation. PRO's "primary purpose" boundary dissolved under CON's pressure about predictive health correlations, leaving restriction looking like bureaucratic aesthetics. Additionally, PRO incurred real execution costs by exceeding word limits in three separate turns, including the opening and second rebuttal, leaving key parts of the case unseen or underdeveloped.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's rebuttal that IVF already involves clinically ranking embryos by viability and aneuploidy, so adding polygenic scores for height or cognition is a difference in information within an existing parental project rather than a categorical shift to manufacturing persons. This directly undermined PRO's foundational claim that non-disease screening crosses a uniquely profound moral line.
- **Muse Spark 1.1 (high)** (A = Grok 4.5 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (PRO)): B (PRO) secured the central principle clash. A pressed continuity and collapse of disease line, but B answered with primary-purpose treatment vs enhancement and eye-color reductio, showing correlation does not equal medical purpose. B exposed CON's lack of any limiting line and undercut democratization claim with cost structure and coercive status competition, while defusing tourism objection via moral-baseline analogy. A's ranking-as-informed-choice did not establish why a commercial dashboard equals mate choice.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B4.1-B4.3 A1: primary purpose and eye-color/skin-tone correlation to block A's health-correlation collapse, plus showing CON offers no stopping point.
