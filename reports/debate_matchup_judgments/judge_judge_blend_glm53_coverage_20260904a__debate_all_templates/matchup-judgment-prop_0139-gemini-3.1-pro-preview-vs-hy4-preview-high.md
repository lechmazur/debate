- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (CON); B = Tencent Hy4 Preview (high) (PRO)): Side B won by keeping the comparison focused on immediate safety: allowing an optional refuge can reduce present harm, whereas banning it does not itself produce guards, cameras, or enforcement. B also showed that safeguards and continued investment could address permanence without denying partial relief.

A’s response simply asserted an immediate mandate for universal security without supplying a mechanism, leaving B’s present-harm comparison intact.

Side B's powerful "left behind" and institutionalization arguments created real pressure, but A's consistent framing—demanding what a ban offers a woman assaulted tomorrow—proved harder to answer without relying on unsupported political psychology.
