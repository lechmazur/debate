- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Qwen 3.8 Max (PRO); B = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON)): CON won because it made the decisive clash clearer and forced PRO into an unstable choice. The visibility trap—that a default seal blinds the private parties (especially protective-order petitioners) supposed to rebut it—and the digital-fragments point that sealing removes the docket’s corrective context while leaving scraped accusations were never fully neutralized.
