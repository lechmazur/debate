- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5 (high) (CON); B = Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO)): Side A won by separating a monument's informational value from its intact venerative and political value. This reframed the Germany comparison so that it supported destruction of statues while preserving memory, gutting PRO's attempt to claim postwar remembrance as evidence for statue preservation.
