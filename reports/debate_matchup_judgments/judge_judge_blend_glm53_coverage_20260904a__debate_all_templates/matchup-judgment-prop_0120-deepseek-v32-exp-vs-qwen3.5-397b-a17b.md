PRO's consistent framing—"administrative difficulty is a solvable engineering problem; entrenched inequality is a structural moral failure"—provided a clearer decision rule than CON's fragmented case, which oscillated between valuation disputes, historical repeal, and an alternative proposal it could not fully defend under pressure.
