- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Mistral Medium 3.5 (high) (PRO); B = Tencent Hy4 Preview (high) (CON)): CON reframed the debate as "making versus nurturing" identity and did more damage to PRO's live examples.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's systematic rebuttal of PRO's flagship examples—showing Barcelona's frame came from the 1992 Games, the Fringe grew on the International Festival's stage, and Asheville built on an existing tourism economy—undermined PRO's claim that organic local festivals can create place identity without a catalytic frame.

London 2012 undercut PRO’s structural inevitability claim about Athens. CON’s hierarchy – mega-event makes the frame and global story, local practice then makes it livable – better explains place-identity building for unknown/stigmatized places.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s Rebuttal 2 answers on Asheville and Athens/London – Asheville was already a tourism magnet not from-scratch, and London shows Athens was governance not structure, neutralizing PRO’s best new example and structural-inevitability thesis.
- **Qwen 3.7 Max** (A = Mistral Medium 3.5 (high) (PRO); B = Tencent Hy4 Preview (high) (CON)): Side B won by establishing a decisive conceptual frame—that mega-events "make" the physical and narrative stage while local festivals merely "nurture" it. B successfully neutralized A's examples, pointing out that Asheville already had a pre-existing tourism economy and that Athens' failure was a matter of governance rather than structural inevitability, as proven by London 2012. A's case relied on a romanticized view of local festivals that struggled to answer how unknown or stigmatized cities could build external identity and infrastructure without a massive catalyst. B's rebuttals were sharper, and their closing perfectly crystallized the core clash.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's dismantling of the Asheville example and the Athens/London comparison in Rebuttal 2, which effectively stripped Side A of its primary evidence for local festivals building identity from scratch and mega-events being structurally doomed.
