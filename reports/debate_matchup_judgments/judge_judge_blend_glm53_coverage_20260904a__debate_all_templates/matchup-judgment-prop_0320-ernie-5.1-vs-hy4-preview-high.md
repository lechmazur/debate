- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Baidu Ernie 5.1 (CON); B = Tencent Hy4 Preview (high) (PRO)): B won by making forced, simultaneous dispersal the clearest comparative harm and explaining how transit, lighting, supervision, and retained venue rules address it. A's arguments on total patron-hours and moral hazard were logical but less grounded in the physical reality of nightlife management, and A suffered minor execution costs from clipped turns.
