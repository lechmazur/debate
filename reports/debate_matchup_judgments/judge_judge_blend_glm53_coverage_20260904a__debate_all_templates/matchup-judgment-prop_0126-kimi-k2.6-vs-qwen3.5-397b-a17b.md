Second, CON's introduction of means-tested subsidies in Rebuttal 2 directly answered PRO's core "any fee equals a ban for the poorest" refrain, and PRO never engaged with that answer; in closing PRO simply repeated the slogan without addressing the subsidy mechanism. PRO's rhetoric was emotionally effective throughout, but repeated moral slogans ("monetizing mercy," "fee is a wall") without adapting to CON's evolving counterplan, which is the key reason CON wins clearly but not dominantly.
