CON's pressure questions (on who polices drinkers after the cup leaves the vendor, and on closing-time egress surges) forced PRO to keep adding structural fixes—staffed sightlines, welfare checks, marshals, phased closings—which CON effectively turned into a concession: PRO was reconstructing an outdoor bar without walls, admitting the complexity and fragility of its own proposal. CON's closing crystallized the clash most effectively: "a lever that acts after the surge... is documentation, not control."
