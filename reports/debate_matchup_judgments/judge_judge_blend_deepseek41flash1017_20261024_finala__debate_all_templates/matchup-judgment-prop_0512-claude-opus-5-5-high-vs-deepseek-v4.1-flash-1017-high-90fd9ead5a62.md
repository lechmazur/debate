### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON); B = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (high) (PRO)): Side B won a clear but competitive debate. A persuasively showed that a tax cap does not itself reprice substations or water infrastructure, but B correctly explained that the proposition need not solve every externality: it prevents governments from adding an uncapped tax concession while those costs remain unpaid. B also showed that A's preferred tariffs and connection charges provide the route for satisfying the condition.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's second rebuttal was decisive: it directly conceded that no substation line item moves, then showed why this did not defeat the motion. PRO’s residual “second transfer / default risk” case never established the counterfactual that projects would arrive at full tax, so the cleaner vote is against the proposition.
