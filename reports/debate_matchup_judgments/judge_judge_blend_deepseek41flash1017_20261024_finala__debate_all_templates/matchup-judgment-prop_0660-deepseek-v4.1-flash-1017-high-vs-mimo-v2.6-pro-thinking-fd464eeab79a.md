### Judge Notes

- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (PRO)): Both sides built coherent, developing cases, but CON (A) more successfully resolved the two central pressure points. When PRO demanded a concrete mechanism for output loss beyond mere time-shifting, CON supplied one (sequencing/timeliness propagation: delayed clarifications compound into rework and compressed review windows), directly answering the pressure question in required A1/A2 form. When PRO pressed the apparent contradiction between "blanket bans destroy flexibility" and "covert checking persists," CON resolved it cleanly by arguing that persisting contact is precisely the low-quality, anxious, unreliable kind rather than genuine coordination value, which defuses the "gotcha." PRO's closing analogy (vacations/weekends already manage absence, so latency isn't a new cost) was clever but got directly rebutted by CON's distinction between planned, scheduled absence and unpredictable legal-suspicion-driven suppression, and this exchange was not re-engaged by PRO. PRO also suffered a real execution cost: both Rebuttal 1 and Rebuttal 2 were clipped for exceeding word limits, cutting off argument payoffs (including the final line of the "whichever is real, CON loses" contradiction trap), which the judging frame treats as a genuine cost rather than a cosmetic issue. CON's speeches were all complete and consistently sharpened the comparison each round, giving it the cleaner, more fully executed path to victory.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's A4.3 answer to PRO's "contact persists, so coordination value persists too" challenge — arguing that persisting contact is covert, anxious, and unreliable rather than legitimate coordination — most effectively neutralized PRO's sharpest pressure point and reframed the welfare-vs-productivity tradeoff in CON's favor.
- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (PRO)): Side B won a clear but competitive debate. Side A established a credible sequencing mechanism for productivity loss, especially in ordinary time-sensitive coordination, but largely asserted that this cost would be systemic. Side B offered the stronger comparison: recurring recovery and autonomy gains across most evenings versus latency costs bounded by handoffs, overlap windows, and urgency exceptions. B also persuasively distinguished voluntary flexibility from compelled availability. Its two clipped rebuttals reduced the margin, while A answered the pressure questions well.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B most decisively answered A's claim that welfare gains require separate norm change: the prisoner's dilemma means legal protection is the mechanism that enables workers to disconnect without individual career risk, so enforcement and changing norms complement rather than negate the law.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) (PRO)): Side A won by successfully proving a clear mechanism for productivity loss (latency propagation and sequencing) that Side B could not dismiss as mere 'substitution.'
