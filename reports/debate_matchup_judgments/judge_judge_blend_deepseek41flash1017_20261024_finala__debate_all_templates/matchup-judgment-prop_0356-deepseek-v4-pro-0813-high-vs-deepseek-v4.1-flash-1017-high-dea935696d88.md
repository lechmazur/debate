- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (high) (PRO); B = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (CON)): B won a clear but competitive debate by exposing the unresolved contradiction in A’s fairness standard. B’s expected-loss standard was more operable and directly answered A’s pressure questions.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s adverse-selection response was decisive: once safer drivers enter the monitored pool, the remaining non-surveillance pool can become costlier for legitimate actuarial reasons. Side B’s pressure questions and closing crystallized that CON had no safeguard against market tipping beyond competition and filed-rate review, which cannot prevent the non-surveillance alternative from becoming a punitive residual category. While CON landed real workability worries, it could not show that its operable baseline distinguished a legitimate discount from a punitive baseline in a tipped market, whereas PRO supplied a coherent regulatory line and the decisive weighing that arithmetic coercion is still coercion when exit is impossible.
