- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = Kimi K3.1 Pro (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON)): Side B won by successfully framing the motion's impacts on a comparative scale: minimal convenience for healthy families versus a devastating, invisible trap for vulnerable teens.
