- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO); B = GPT-5.6 Sol (high) (CON)): Both sides built coherent, well-developed cases, but A's core causal mechanism—that always-on surveillance chills the voluntary disclosure that actually protects teens, and that even "automated trigger" systems still process and sweep in innocent and third-party content—survived the exchange largely intact.
