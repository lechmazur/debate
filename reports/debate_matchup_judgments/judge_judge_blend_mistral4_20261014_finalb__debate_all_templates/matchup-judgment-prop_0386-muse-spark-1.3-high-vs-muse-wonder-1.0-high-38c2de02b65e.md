- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO); B = Muse Spark 1.3 (high) (CON)): Both sides built coherent, well-grounded cases and the debate converged cleanly on the sole-provider scenario exposed by the pressure questions. But A's pressure question forced B into the costlier concession: under B's regime, a patient who sought lawful, time-sensitive care may simply be left without it, with "system duty" offered as cold comfort. B better resolved the central clash over who bears the cost of scarcity.
