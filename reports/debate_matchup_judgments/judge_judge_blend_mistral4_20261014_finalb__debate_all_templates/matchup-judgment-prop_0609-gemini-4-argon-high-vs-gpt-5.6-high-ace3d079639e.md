- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = GPT-5.6 Sol (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (CON)): Both sides built coherent, well-structured cases and handled the Q&A format cleanly, but B's cumulative pressure proved harder for A to fully neutralize. Overall B provided the more sustained, harder-to-answer comparative pressure and ended with the cleaner, more memorable framing of the core flaw in PRO's mechanism.
