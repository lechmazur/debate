- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON)): A won because B established that removal rights are incomplete, but not that adolescents should lack them.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's strongest response was that upload restrictions and removal solve different problems: objective rules may prevent some exposure, but only later adolescent judgment can identify changing embarrassment, and taking down the searchable primary copy still materially reduces ongoing harm despite surviving duplicates.
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON)): A won by better defending the motion as written.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's pressure on the coercion problem (that the right fails in exactly the profiteering households it targets because pressure happens before filing) and A's decisive counter that legal rights are not stripped from dependents simply because exercise is difficult.
- **Qwen 3.7 Max** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5.5 (high) (CON)): Side B wins by successfully identifying and exploiting the structural flaw in Side A's mechanism: placing the burden of enforcement on a dependent minor in a household where the parent controls the finances and the child's living situation.
