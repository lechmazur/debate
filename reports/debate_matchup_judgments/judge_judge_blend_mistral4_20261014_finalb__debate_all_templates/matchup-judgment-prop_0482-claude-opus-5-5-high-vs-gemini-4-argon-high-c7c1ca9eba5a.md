  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's closing and second rebuttal dismantling Side B's reliance on ministry procurement leverage, effectively arguing that expecting underfunded ministries to force multinational corporations to abandon lucrative proprietary lock-in is a fantasy that exposes the fatal flaw in the traditional pipeline's applicability to low-income markets.
