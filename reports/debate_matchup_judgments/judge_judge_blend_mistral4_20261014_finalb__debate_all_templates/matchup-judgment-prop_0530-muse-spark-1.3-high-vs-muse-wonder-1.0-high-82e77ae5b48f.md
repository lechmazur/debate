- **Claude Sonnet 5 (high)** (A = Muse Spark 1.3 (high) (CON); B = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO)): The pivotal exchange was A's pressure question about mixed-constituency sites (multiple nations/faiths/generations) and who adjudicates rival descendant claims. B's case was well-argued and emotionally resonant, with good original framing ("taken twice") and strong initial examples, but the design concessions under pressure weakened the core "veto that cannot be ignored" claim. This makes A's path to victory cleaner and more fully defended against the key test case.
