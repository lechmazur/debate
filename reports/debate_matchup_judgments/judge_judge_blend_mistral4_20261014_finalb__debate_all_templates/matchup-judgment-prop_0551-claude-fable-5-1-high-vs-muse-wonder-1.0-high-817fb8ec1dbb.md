- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO)): Side B won a clear but competitive debate by preserving the decisive distinction between a discretionary program and an enforceable individual guarantee.
