- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 4 Argon (high) (PRO); B = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (CON)): A won a competitive debate by keeping the decisive comparison on who permanently pays and who holds the veto if an aggressor never settles.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s A1/A2 answers (Rebuttal 2): other sanctions still supply leverage, and ICJ findings still need domestic courts with territorial reach—then turning B’s own reparations claim into an aggressor-veto problem.

Side A's attempt to pivot to unfreezing the "broader economy" felt like moving the goalposts. Side B also effectively defended against the capital flight argument by grounding their case in the structural necessity of reserve currencies, making their comparative weighing much stronger.
