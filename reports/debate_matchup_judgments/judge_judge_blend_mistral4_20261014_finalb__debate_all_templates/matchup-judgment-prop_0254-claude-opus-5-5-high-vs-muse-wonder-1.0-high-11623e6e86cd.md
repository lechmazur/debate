A's repeated clipping and some overstatements about market prices and GDP reduced the margin.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2, where it used Side B's own answers from the Pressure Questions round to prove that the dashboard model engineers clear failure out of existence and replaces a strict headline target with a political negotiation.
