- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5.1 (high) (CON); B = Muse Wonder 1.0 (high) (PRO)): Side A won a strong, competitive debate by isolating the ancestry gate as the key difference between reparations and durable need-based investment. A therefore showed that lineage targeting mainly added exclusion, eligibility disputes, and political closure without uniquely improving material repair.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's closing effectively neutralized Side A's strongest point (that the gate only subtracts poor neighbors) by showing that folding reparations into general welfare allows the state to claim credit for repair while actually paying nothing for its expropriation.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s R1 collapse of B’s endowments into need-based policy plus a gate (A2.1–A2.2), later sealed when A used B’s wealth-testing concession to show the gate only subtracts.
