### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Mistral Medium 3.5 (high) (PRO); B = Mistral Large 4 (high) (CON)): B won by showing that A never established a credible path from premium fermentation ingredients to affordable, nutritionally broad protein at global scale.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s strongest exchange distinguished protein from solar electricity: food is not fungible, requires specialized production and distribution, and depends on consumer diets. A largely repeated that scale and public investment would lower costs without resolving those concrete differences.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = Mistral Medium 3.5 (high) (PRO); B = Mistral Large 4 (high) (CON)): Side A established a decisive comparative framework: animal agriculture has structural, biological limits to decarbonization, while precision fermentation faces engineering and cost hurdles that subsidies historically solve.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's pressure question regarding the 30% limit of feed additives, and their subsequent capitalization on it, successfully demonstrated that Side B's proposed alternatives are structurally incapable of meeting necessary climate targets.
- **Grok 4.5 (high)** (A = Mistral Medium 3.5 (high) (PRO); B = Mistral Large 4 (high) (CON)): CON built the clearer path against the motion as written: shifting "far more" support is a high-stakes food-system gamble on still-niche, capital-intensive technology whose near-term staple scale, full nutrition profiles, and rural skill transfer remain underspecified. PRO’s climate-urgency and solar-analogy core stayed live but was repeatedly damaged by CON’s fungibility, cold-chain, feedstock, and jobs-transfer attacks; PRO’s replies (retraining, waste sugars, “same learning curve”) stayed hand-wavy. A’s clipped opening and thin pressure round were execution costs; B’s rebuttals more consistently narrowed the clash to feasibility and sovereignty.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON’s Rebuttal 1/2 dismantling of the solar analogy (protein is perishable, requires behavior change and complex chains; bioreactors need PhDs/clean-rooms that dairy skills do not transfer) plus rejection of the arbitrary 50% trap most damaged PRO’s live scaling-and-transition route.
