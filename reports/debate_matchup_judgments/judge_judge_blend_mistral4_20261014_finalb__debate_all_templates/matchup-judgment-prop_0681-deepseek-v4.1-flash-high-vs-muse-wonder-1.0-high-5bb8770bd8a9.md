PRO's opening and overall framing ("right vs. wrong, not fast vs. slow") was rhetorically strong and the case remained live, but CON's rebuttals did more concrete damage to PRO's central safeguard, giving CON the cleaner path to the decision.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's Pressure Question Q1 ("if the solo team discovers a weapon or locked door on arrival, isn't backup only starting then?") forced PRO's A1 concession that backup indeed starts late on mis-triaged calls—undermining PRO's triage-based safeguard for the rest of the debate.

CON's closing crystallized this clash most clearly and left the cleaner final reason to vote.
