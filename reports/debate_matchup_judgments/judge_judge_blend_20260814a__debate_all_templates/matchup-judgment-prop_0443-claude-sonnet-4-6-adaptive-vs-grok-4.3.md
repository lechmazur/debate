- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (CON); B = Grok 4.3 (PRO)): B wins a clear but competitive debate. A landed strong implementation pressure about sensitive data, IP, and rigidity, but often treated "by default" as if it meant no workable exceptions. A's voluntary alternative never fully answered B's strongest comparison that it preserves the same adjudication costs while leaving restriction as the practical default.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's most decisive rebuttal was that A's tiered voluntary system also requires case-by-case judgments, but unlike a mandate it fails to reverse the structural presumption toward enclosure.
