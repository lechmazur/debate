- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Step 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO); B = Claude Sonnet 5 (high) (CON)): Side B won by exposing that Side A defined DOGE-style drives to include the very implementation failures it needed to prove, while excluding more successful rapid reforms.
