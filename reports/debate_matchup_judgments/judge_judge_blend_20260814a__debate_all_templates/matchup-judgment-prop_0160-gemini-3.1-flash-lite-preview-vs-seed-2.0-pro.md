### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Preview (CON); B = ByteDance Seed2.0 Pro (PRO)): Side B won a clear but competitive debate by offering the more complete comparative case: national service adds reserve capacity for non-elite roles while universal participation creates cross-class and cross-ethnic contact that voluntary programs cannot reliably scale.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's A1/A2 in Rebuttal 2, which cleanly addressed both pressure questions with model-specific mechanisms and Finnish outcome data.
