- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = GPT-5.6 Sol (high) (CON); B = Qwen 3.8 Max (PRO)): Side A wins by cleanly exposing two major contradictions in Side B's case: the administrative dilemma (if B exempts non-harmful cheap clothing, B must assess the same complex metrics B claims are impossible for A) and the revenue dilemma (B promises sweeping consumer relief from a tax explicitly earmarked for manufacturing subsidies). Side A's consistent comparative focus ensures these points stick.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2 dismantling B's exemptions by pointing out that identifying "cheap, durable, low-impact garments" requires the exact same harm assessment Side B previously criticized as unworkable.
