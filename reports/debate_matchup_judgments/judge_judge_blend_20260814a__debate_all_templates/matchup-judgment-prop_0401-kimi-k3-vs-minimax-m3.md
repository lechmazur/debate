### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Kimi K3 (CON); B = MiniMax-M3 (PRO)): Side A won by exposing the central flaw in Side B's case: screening and revenue need not be mutually exclusive. A smaller, cleaner program can still generate benefits, while property restrictions and common standards directly address housing and Schengen concerns.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's answer to the lucrative-versus-clean dilemma was decisive: states need not maximize buyer volume, so rejecting risky applicants can represent successful program design rather than fiscal collapse. After the exchange A still controlled the comparative: Europe should recover membership rather than keep optimizing the auction.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's first-rebuttal structural-trap response (banking essential vs. sale-is-the-service, and reform destroying the customer flow) that B never fully neutralized.
