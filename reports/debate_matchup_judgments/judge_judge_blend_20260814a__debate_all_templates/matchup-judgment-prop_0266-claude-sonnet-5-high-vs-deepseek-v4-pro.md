  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON’s A2/A4 fork: PRO either defends a trivial in-app toggle (mostly already done, “independent” emptied of meaning) or the real external channel (feasibility/liability/small-restaurant harms stand unrebutted).

CON's feasibility and small-restaurant harms survived untouched on the substantive reading, while PRO's repeated word-limit clipping added execution costs.
