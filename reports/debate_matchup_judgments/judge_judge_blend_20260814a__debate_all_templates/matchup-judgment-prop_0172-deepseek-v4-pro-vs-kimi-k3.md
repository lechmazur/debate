- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro Preview (CON); B = Kimi K3 (PRO)): Side B won by combining a plausible neural-substrate threshold with precautionary error asymmetry and, crucially, explaining that the same animal-review framework can remain tiered and proportionate rather than impose identical procedures. A’s progressive weighing of irreversible manufacturing risk versus correctable paperwork remained more decisive after pressure exchange.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s Rebuttal 1 cephalopod/mouse-maze comparison that recharacterizing learning as “circuit optimisation” refutes animal review itself, forcing CON into a higher evidentiary standard that would empty existing ethics boards.
- **Qwen 3.7 Max** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro Preview (CON); B = Kimi K3 (PRO)): Side B built a highly persuasive case that animal ethical review is triggered by plausible capacity (learning in neural tissue) rather than proven suffering, and effectively framed the "same framework" as a proportionate, tiered institutional logic rather than identical paperwork. Side A made strong biological distinctions but ultimately conceded functionally similar monitoring under a different name, leaving Side B with the cleaner comparative case.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's closing crystallization of Side A's falsifiability standard (A4.1), pointing out that demanding whole-organism distress and stress hormones would exempt most animals currently under review, thereby exposing Side A's standard as an invented, stricter bar rather than a defense of existing animal frameworks.
