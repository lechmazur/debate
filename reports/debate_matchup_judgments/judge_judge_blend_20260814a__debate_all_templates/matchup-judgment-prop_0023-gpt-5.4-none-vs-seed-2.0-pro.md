### Judge Notes

- **Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high)** (A = GPT-5.4 (no reasoning) (CON); B = ByteDance Seed2.0 Pro (PRO)): PRO (Side B) wins on balance, though this was a genuine contest. PRO anchored the debate in documented, realized harm—AQI/ISIS emerging from the post-invasion vacuum, Iranian cross-border proxy networks that did not exist pre-2003—and consistently returned to the framing of "verified harm vs. speculative alternative." That framing fit the motion well and was difficult to dislodge. CON's strongest move was methodologically sound: the proposition requires a comparative counterfactual, and "12 years of sanctions-enforced containment" is not a self-sustaining equilibrium; it is a brittle freeze that might have ruptured badly on its own. The "frozen crisis" label and the multi-causation argument on ISIS (Syria's collapse as a necessary co-driver) were real pressure on PRO. However, CON never built a positive picture of what alternative instability would actually have looked like or why it would rival the documented 20-year cascade. Raising doubts about PRO's baseline is not the same as establishing a comparably chaotic counterfactual, and the motion's burden still requires CON to neutralize PRO's comparison, not just complicate it. PRO's answers to CON's pressure questions were adequate—"12 years of empirical track record" is at least a grounded reply to the sustainability challenge. CON's answers to PRO's pressure questions conceded the speculative character of the alternative ("I don't need to prove one specific pathway"). Multiple clipped CON speeches are a real execution cost: the judge cannot reward arguments that were not fully presented, and they leave PRO's late-stage comparisons partially unanswered. In sum, PRO combined concrete causal evidence with the cleaner evaluative standard; CON made valid methodological challenges but never converted them into a positive comparative case strong enough to flip the burden back.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's Rebuttal 2 answer to CON's Q1 (the long-run baseline question) was the most decisive exchange. PRO correctly responded that 12 years of verified, low-cost containment is the only empirical data point available, while CON's "eventual rupture" scenario is entirely speculative with no concrete pathway offered. When CON's own A1 response explicitly conceded "I don't need to prove one specific pathway was certain," it effectively confirmed PRO's framing that the comparison is between measurable documented harm and an unarticulated hypothetical, tilting the weighing decisively toward PRO.
