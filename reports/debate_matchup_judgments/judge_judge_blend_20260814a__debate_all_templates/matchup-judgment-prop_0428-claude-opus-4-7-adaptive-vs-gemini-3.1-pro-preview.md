### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (PRO); B = Claude Opus 4.7 (high) (CON)): Side B won by making the decisive clash about the burden shift created by “even when residents worry.” B conceded the value of third places but argued those benefits can be achieved through siting, hours, and resident-informed design, while A repeatedly minimized foreseeable sleep, enforcement, and equity harms as mere friction. A’s worry-versus-violation distinction was strong, but B answered that forcing residents to wait for harm and then enforce it is itself the problem.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s second rebuttal was decisive: if venues are quiet because resident worries shaped limits, that supports CON; if they are not, PRO is licensing sleep costs onto nearby residents. That dilemma sharply undercut A’s attempt to keep both “ignore preemptive worries” and “trust mitigation.”
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (PRO); B = Claude Opus 4.7 (high) (CON)): Side B wins by exposing a structural dilemma in PRO's case: either resident worries legitimately shape venue design (which is CON's position and contradicts the motion's "even when residents worry"), or PRO must license foreseeable harm onto vulnerable residents. B's sharp textual focus on the word "even," combined with the devastating catch in Rebuttal 2 that PRO's promise of "quiet operation" implicitly concedes the legitimacy of resident concerns, proved decisive. A's case about commercial rents and NIMBY vetoes was competent but could not overcome B's demonstration that PRO's benefits are achievable without overriding residents, while the costs of doing so fall specifically on sleep-deprived, immobile renters.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's Rebuttal 2 [B4.5-B4.7], which caught PRO promising that venues would "operate quietly" and showed this concession collapses PRO's case: if resident worries shape quiet operation, those worries are legitimate design constraints, making the motion's override unnecessary and unjustified.

This crystallized the clash and dismantled PRO's path to victory.
