But PRO never answered how their harder approach avoids the re-infiltration cycle except by proposing permanent occupation, which undermined the justification claim. The two clipped rebuttals for CON are execution costs, but minimal since the core arguments were intact.

CON never explained how local civic reform becomes viable without some recovery of ground-level coercive control first. The fragmentation argument was CON's strongest card, and it landed real pressure, but A's counter—that 2006 failed because of decapitation-only tactics without simultaneous pressure on finances, prisons, and replacement networks—was analytically specific and was only met with reassertion ("the kinetic model inherently fails") rather than a mechanism-level rebuttal explaining why comprehensive simultaneous pressure would produce the same outcome. A's strawman identification in R1 (CON was refuting "free-fire zones" rather than the proposition) also stuck because CON never fully escaped it; B's subsequent arguments kept treating military deployment as inevitably producing indiscriminate combat rather than engaging A's defined version. A's execution cost from two clipped speeches is real and limits its late-stage crystallization, but the core arguments were established in earlier rounds and never definitively answered. CON's "managed surrender" framing and the "18 years of mass graves" rebuttal were rhetorically effective but couldn't resolve the fundamental sequencing gap CON's own alternative created.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A's Rebuttal 1 identification of CON's bait-and-switch on "all-out militarization" versus the proposition's actual claim, combined with the sequencing argument that reform tools "require coercive force to be credible," established the terms of the decisive clash. CON's R1 response defended against the caricature version rather than engaging A's defined standard, and CON's A1 in R2 ("financial tools work from Mexico City") only deepened the gap by implicitly conceding that local institutional reform requires a precondition CON's strategy never secures.
