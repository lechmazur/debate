- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (PRO)): B wins because its central comparison remained live: warning labels intervene immediately at the point of purchase against packaging and marketing, while A’s preferred campaigns were mostly asserted to build literacy without a concrete mechanism for overcoming distance from the purchase moment.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B’s pressure-question exchange was most decisive: it forced A to explain how campaigns avoid dependency and deliver nuance at the moment of choice, and A largely sidestepped those questions, allowing B’s point-of-purchase advantage to survive.
- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Xiaomi MiMo V2 Pro (PRO); B = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (CON)): Side A won because it maintained a clearer live route to victory after the full exchange. A established that warning labels intervene immediately at the point of decision where engineered marketing and impulse are strongest, using plausible mechanisms and the Chile example.
