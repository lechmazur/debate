- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = GLM-5.2 (max) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5 (high) (CON)): Side A won by effectively neutralizing Side B's standard of "survivable errors." Side B argued that since death is irreversible, systemic errors (like poverty driving the choice) are unacceptable. PRO's reliance on autonomy was undermined by their own answers, and two clipped speeches added execution costs.
