- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Claude Sonnet 5 (high) (CON); B = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (PRO)): PRO wins because CON's core backfire mechanism—platforms fleeing to flat fees—remains insufficiently substantiated against PRO's counter-comparison to music and film, where audit rights and standardized reporting have long coexisted with performance-based pay. CON's attacks on a one-size-fits-all standard also lose force because PRO explicitly endorsed category-calibrated standardization.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's reframing of CON's flat-fee threat in Rebuttal 1 as the very reason the proposition is necessary, coupled with the strikes precedent and the memorable crystallization that "imperfect and auditable beats imperfect and invisible," which CON never fully neutralized without relying on speculative platform behavior.
