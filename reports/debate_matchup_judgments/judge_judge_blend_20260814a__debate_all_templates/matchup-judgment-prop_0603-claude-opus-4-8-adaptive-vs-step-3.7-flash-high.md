- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Claude Opus 4.8 (high) (CON); B = Step 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO)): Side B won because it stayed closer to the proposition’s core burden: approving extraction before robust independent baselines means managing irreversible deep-sea harms without knowing the starting point.
