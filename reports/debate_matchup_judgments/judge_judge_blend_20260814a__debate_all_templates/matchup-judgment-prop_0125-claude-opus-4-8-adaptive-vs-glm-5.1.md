A's attempt to bridge this gap ("fund within reason" but "escalate subsidies if no bidder emerges") created a logical vulnerability that B exploited effectively across the later rounds.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: B's Rebuttal 2 and Closing masterfully weaponized A's concession that externalities are bounded. By demonstrating that capped auctions draw a rational fiscal line while the motion's trigger erases it, B turned A's attempt to claim moderate policy ground into a direct contradiction, cleanly resolving the clash on opportunity cost and fiscal discipline.
