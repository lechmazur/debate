### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Claude Opus 5 (high) (CON); B = Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) (PRO)): Side A won by separating proof that loneliness is harmful from proof that parity-level public-health prioritization has effective, scalable, and auditable levers. Side B established plausible biological and structural harms, but repeatedly asserted rather than demonstrated that social prescribing or health mandates reliably reduce loneliness beyond infrastructure policies both sides already supported. Side A also gave the sharper account of scarce attention and incremental policy value. Side B’s inactivity analogy remained meaningful, but did not fully answer A’s concerns about measurement, durable impact, and the bilateral nature of connection.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A’s strongest exchange was its response to Side B’s proposed lever: it argued that buses, housing, and civic spaces are independently justified planning measures, while social prescribing lacked demonstrated durable population effects. This directly pressured what public-health priority status uniquely adds, and Side B never fully supplied that missing marginal benefit.

PRO never overcame the decisive objection that unlike smoking or lead, loneliness lacks a measurable marker and cannot be "prescribed," making parity-level priority status an unaccountable bureaucratic expansion rather than a necessary response to epidemiological harm.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON's demonstration that PRO's exit condition—"establishing enduring social infrastructure"—is never satisfiable, rendering the priority permanently unauditable, while the concrete remedies PRO named were planning decisions that need no health ministry letterhead ([B4.3]–[B4.7], [B5.3]–[B5.5]).

A's answers recycled planning solutions B already endorsed plus unproven social prescribing, and defined an unfalsifiable exit condition of "enduring infrastructure." B's voluntariness argument – you cannot prescribe a friend because value requires chosen connection, unlike unilateral exercise – directly neutralized A's sedentary-lifestyle analogy and framed priority as permanent unauditable triage.
