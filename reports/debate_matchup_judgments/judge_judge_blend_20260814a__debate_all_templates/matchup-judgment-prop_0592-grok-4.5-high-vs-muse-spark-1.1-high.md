  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's Rebuttal 2, where they directly address the "porosity" comparison. ### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = Muse Spark 1.1 (high) (PRO); B = Grok 4.5 (high) (CON)): A won by comparing the magnitude of each failure: unanimity lets one holdout block sanctions across the entire Union, whereas QMV limits obstruction to narrower enforcement leakage while binding a broad coalition.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s strongest response was that an aggressor values a veto far more than one weak enforcement point: QMV converts total legal obstruction into narrower, punishable non-compliance. Side A's "subscription extortion" frame and the direct comparative weighing of a full veto versus a single frontier leak proved decisive.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2 [A4.1]-[A4.4], which answered Side B's pressure questions by distinguishing a catastrophic veto from a manageable leaky border and collapsed Side B's ownership theory by showing unanimity monetizes rather than exhausts dissent.
