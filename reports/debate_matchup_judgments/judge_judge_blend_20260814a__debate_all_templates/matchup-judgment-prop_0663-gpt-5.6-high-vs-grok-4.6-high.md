CON's closing was also cut off mid-sentence by the word limit, which is a real execution cost per the judging rules, denying CON the chance to fully land its final reframe. PRO's closing was complete, crisp, and crystallized the clash effectively ("national control of the first sentence is the delay"), giving PRO the cleaner final impression.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's Rebuttal 2 response to CON's "implausible middle case" argument—pointing out that a leaked signal (clinician report, neighboring lab, travel case) already exists prior to any inspection, so access need not storm a sealed ward but merely prevent silence from being the only authorized story—did the most damage to CON's central framing, and CON's counter (calling the trigger "not workable") did not fully neutralize it before the debate ended.
