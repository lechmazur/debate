- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = GPT-5.5 (high) (CON); B = Kimi K2.6 (PRO)): Side A wins by successfully framing vouchers not merely as a 'source of income' but as a 'bundled payment arrangement' that intrinsically includes administrative burdens.
