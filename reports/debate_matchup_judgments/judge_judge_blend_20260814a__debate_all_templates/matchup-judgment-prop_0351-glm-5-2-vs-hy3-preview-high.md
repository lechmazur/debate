PRO's Rebuttal 2 was the clearest single speech: the blame-vs-outcomes distinction on A1 was analytically honest, and the task-performance-vs-apprenticeship-learning distinction on A2 cleanly answered CON's Q2 without evasion. CON's closing was clipped before completion, a real execution cost in the final crystallization round. Taken together, PRO progressed and sharpened across rounds; CON largely repeated its idealized account with diminishing returns.
