- **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** (A = Claude Opus 4.7 (high) (CON); B = MiniMax-M2.7 (PRO)): Side A consistently maintained a strong comparative framework, successfully arguing that prohibition merely shifts sales to untrackable actors and destroys the forensic visibility needed to expose abuses.
