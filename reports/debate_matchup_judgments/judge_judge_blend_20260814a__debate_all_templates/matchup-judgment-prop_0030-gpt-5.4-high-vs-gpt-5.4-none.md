The motion only requires that the counterfactual would be stronger, not that Brexit was the dominant cause of Britain's problems; PRO correctly identified and held that asymmetry. CON never named a concrete, economy-wide gain visible today that offset the documented friction costs. PRO's repeated clipping across four speeches is a real execution cost and narrows the margin considerably, while CON's complete late-round speeches gave it a presentational edge in closing. Still, the core argument remained intact in what was visible, and CON never filled the gap that its own framing created: if flexibility gains are real, show them clearly enough to offset documented costs. That gap was never closed.
