### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (PRO); B = Claude Fable 5 (high) (CON)): Side B won by making the key comparative burden much clearer: what does the income-reducing aggressive regulation uniquely buy that targeted peat and forest protection, titling, traceability support, and intensification do not?   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's second rebuttal was decisive: it answered the pressure questions directly, claimed the peat and primary-forest ban as part of its own targeted framework, and turned Side A's price mechanism against it by arguing that raising prices would intensify the very frontier clearing Side A feared.

  Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side B's second rebuttal brilliantly dismantled Side A's price mechanism by pointing out that Side A's own pressure question established high prices as a driver of illegal clearing.
