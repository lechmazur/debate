- **Kimi K2.6** (A = Claude Sonnet 4.6 (high) (PRO); B = Qwen3.5-397B-A17B (CON)): PRO wins by establishing a multi-pronged case (cognitive capacity, long-term stake, legal inconsistency, and uncontested empirical successes in Scotland and Austria) while decisively undermining CON's single pillar of autonomy and coercion.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: PRO's demonstration that CON's coercion framework proved too much: by showing that adults face similar existential pressure (eviction, job loss, family retaliation) and yet are not disenfranchised, and that the secret ballot already severs the observable link between voter and vote, PRO exposed that CON could not principledly explain why eighteen was the magic threshold where coercion suddenly became tolerable.
