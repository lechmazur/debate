- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Kimi K2.6 (CON); B = Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro (PRO)): Side B won by keeping the focus on proportionality: Side A showed that some risks exist, but B more convincingly argued that broad occupational licenses often exceed what those risks require and therefore function as incumbent-protective entry barriers.   Most decisive rebuttal noted: Side A's Rebuttal 2 opening (A1), which dismantled Side B's claim that a sanitation course is just "licensing rebranded" by explaining the crucial differences in scope, cost, and competitor control.
