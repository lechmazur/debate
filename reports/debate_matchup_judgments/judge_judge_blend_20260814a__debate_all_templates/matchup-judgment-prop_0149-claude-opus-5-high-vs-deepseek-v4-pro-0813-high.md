### Judge Notes

- **GPT-5.6 Sol (high)** (A = DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (high) (PRO); B = Claude Opus 5 (high) (CON)): A won narrowly by defending a calibrated but genuinely stronger regime: documented repeat violations, owner-facing incentives, local contacts, escalating penalties, and hearings. A also better explained why after-the-fact guest enforcement fails to prevent recurring harm and why tourism concerns may shape enforcement without vetoing it.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: A’s second rebuttal most decisively answered B’s weaponized-complaint and tourism-veto objections by requiring adjudicated violations and treating tourism dependence as relevant but non-dispositive. PRO’s closing overclaimed concession; CON’s closing crystallized the choice cleanly—punish the party via harm, not the rental contract.
  Most decisive rebuttal noted: CON R2 (B4.3–B4.5): accepts adjudicated repeat penalties on a harm basis, then argues PRO’s remaining “stronger” piece is mainly process burden that selects for portfolio operators.
