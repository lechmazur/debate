- **Kimi K2.6** (A = GPT-5.4 (high) (CON); B = Kimi K2.5 Thinking (PRO)): CON successfully undermined PRO's central mechanism by exposing that indexing retirement age to average life expectancy masks deep class inequality in healthy working capacity, forcing the most vulnerable older workers to absorb the adjustment. B’s pressure that exemptions undermine the “automatic stabilizer” was sharp, but A’s rebuttal reframing carve-outs as precision targeting—and weighing tax-driven labor market collapse against contained disability costs—ultimately controlled the comparative frame.
