A also turned B's own "consent" framework back on itself (splitting consent into "honest count" vs "genuine choice") and pre-empted the inevitable single-instance counterexample before B produced it (the NC-9 case), which let A frame that example as confirming rather than refuting the "anomaly with a repair procedure" story.
