- **GPT-5.5 (high)** (A = Claude Fable 5 (high) (CON); B = Tencent Hy3 Preview (high) (PRO)): Side A won by turning the debate from mandate versus inaction into mandate versus flexible decarbonization, then defending that comparison more cleanly.
