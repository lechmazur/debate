  Most decisive rebuttal noted: The exchange over CON’s Q2 (why in-situ context cannot work if museum context can) and PRO’s A2 in Rebuttal 2. PRO sharply distinguished the functional design of civic honor spaces (built for veneration, encountered during daily routines) from museums (built for critical engagement), effectively neutralizing CON’s core alternative and anchoring the debate on the unavoidable coercive signal of towering statues in public squares.
